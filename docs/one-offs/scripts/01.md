# 01 Your AI has never met you

- **Theme:** Build your AI brain
- **Length:** about 2:25 at an unhurried pace (about 360 spoken words), plus the 4-second end card
- **Format:** to camera, tall and wide, edited with quick-edit
- **Status:** draft for Krish to read, change and record

**The point in one line:** every AI has read the whole internet and knows nothing about you, and five lines of context fix most of it.

**Why this one (for whoever makes it):** this is the door into the whole AI-brain idea, so it has to feel like a two-minute magic trick the viewer can do before the video ends. The feeling is "oh, that's why my AI sounds like a stranger", followed by "I can fix that tonight". Keep it light and practical. No product mentions, and no CTRL pitch.

**News nod:** Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on 22 September 2026 (TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance/). Google released Gemini 4 Argon to cyber security partners first on 30 September 2026 (TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/google-releases-gemini-4-argon-called-its-most-powerful-model-yet/).

## Script

**[0:00]**

**Say:** Two companies that compete with each other ask the same AI the same question: should we put our prices up? They'll get much the same answer. That's the funny thing about the smartest software ever built. It's read the whole internet, and it's never met you.

*On screen:* Face to camera. Then a split screen: two chat windows labelled "Company A" and "Company B", near-identical answers.

**[0:16]**

**Say:** And it keeps getting cleverer. Anthropic released a new top model on the twenty-second of September. Google's newest went to cyber security teams first. Every month the brain gets bigger. It still doesn't know your customers, your numbers, or the thing you hate seeing in an email.

*On screen:* Two proof panels, each headline in the source's own words, source named underneath, the date highlighted in mint.

**[0:33]**

**Say:** So here's a two-minute test. Pick something you ask AI every week. A client update, a sales email, whatever. Ask it cold, in a blank chat. Then open a new chat, paste five lines about you first, and ask exactly the same thing.

*On screen:* Screen recording: a blank chat, the same request typed twice.

**[0:49]**

**Say:** The five lines. Who you are and what you do. Who you're writing for. Your top three priorities right now. One decision you've already made, so it stops suggesting the opposite. And one thing you never want to see, like "I hope this email finds you well".

*On screen:* Card building line by line: Me / Who it's for / Top three priorities / Already decided / Never.

**[1:09]**

**Say:** Put the two answers side by side. The first sounds like a helpful stranger. The second sounds like someone who sat in on your Monday meeting. The gap between them is a tax, and you pay it every time you open a blank chat and explain yourself from scratch.

*On screen:* Cold and loaded answers side by side. One mint highlight on the line in the loaded answer that only you could have got.

**[1:27]**

**Say:** Most people pay it forever, because nobody sends a bill. It just turns up as twenty minutes of rewriting.

*On screen:* Face. Punch word on screen: TAX.

**[1:35]**

**Say:** Keep the five lines short. One page at most. A tight page beats a giant document, because AI gets worse when the important bits are buried in a pile of everything. And leave out anything you wouldn't want read out at a team meeting: passwords, other people's private details, gossip.

*On screen:* Card: "One page. Nothing you wouldn't want read out."

**[1:55]**

**Say:** Save it somewhere you own. A note on your phone is fine. Then you can paste it into any tool. Switch from ChatGPT to Claude to Copilot and your brain comes with you.

*On screen:* The note on a phone screen, pasted into three different chat windows.

**[2:07]**

**Say:** So, this week: write your five lines, run the test, and look at the gap. That gap is the first page of your AI brain.

*On screen:* Full face. The do-this card.

## Do this today (card before the end card)

Ask one real work question in a blank chat. Ask it again with five lines about you pasted first. Compare.
