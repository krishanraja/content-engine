# 00 Index: one-off video scripts

Sixteen ready-to-record scripts for the main makeyourmindup channel, outside the three subchannels. Each one is a practical AI habit anyone can use today, built from ideas already in Krish's own repositories (CTRL, Mindmake, the content engine and makeyourmindup), told through real stories from his own system, with a short nod to the AI news of the last four weeks.

Made on 6 October 2026. Every script is a draft for Krish to read, change and record. None of them has been through the engine's fact gate, so the news lines are marked to check on the morning of recording.

## The sixteen

### Build your AI brain
- **01** **Your AI has never met you.** The two-minute cold and loaded test, and the five lines that fix most of it.
- **02** **Correct it once, with the reason.** The banned phrase my AI learned from my own notes, and how to make a correction stick.
- **03** **When one comment becomes a rule.** The CEO who said "no deck" once. Four words to search for in your AI's memory.
- **04** **Show your AI a yes and a no.** Why "make it punchy" gets you a hotel website, and why your deleted drafts are gold.
- **05** **Write down why you said no.** The four-line decision note that keeps your reasons in the building.

### Level yourself up
- **06** **Make your call before you ask the AI.** Three lines on paper first, then let it argue with you.
- **07** **Never let AI mark its own homework.** $900 million became "close to a million", and the checker gave it 9 out of 10.
- **08** **Never believe the word "done".** 33 hours of "ok" while nothing worked, plus the launch-day crop, captions and pictures-off fails.
- **09** **Reading is free. Sending needs a yes.** The one rule for AI agents that can pay at the checkout.
- **10** **Every blank in your brief becomes a bug.** The five-part brief, the "what's missing" line, and the 78% nobody asked for.
- **11** **New job, new chat.** What to do when you type "I already told you that".
- **12** **Count your copy-pastes.** Find your first AI job, and give the report nobody reads a funeral.

### Ways of thinking about AI
- **13** **Hand over the work. Keep the understanding.** The one-minute test, and keeping the start and the end of the job.
- **14** **Where did your saved hour go?** Time saved is the setup. Where it goes is the payoff.
- **15** **The name-swap test.** Drafts are nearly free now, so the sentence only you could write is what counts.
- **16** **Who can actually answer this?** The web, only you, or nobody yet: three columns for any decision.

## Suggested recording order

Start with 9, 7 and 8. They're the most timely (agents that pay, checkers that lie, "done" that isn't) and they carry the strongest true stories. Then 1, 2 and 13, which set up the AI-brain idea the rest build on. The news lines go stale first, so record the ones that lean on September launches (9, 8, 12, 10) within the next two weeks, or swap the news line for that week's.

## How each script is laid out

- The top says what the video is for and the feeling it should leave, so whoever edits it keeps the intent.
- The script runs in beats: a timecode, the words to say, and what's on screen. Graphics follow the house rule: each one proves one point, from the source's own words.
- Then a "do this today" card for just before the end card, title and description options, and a "before you record" list: facts to check, privacy flags and the repo sources.
- Spoken lengths are worked out at 150 words a minute. With pauses cut in the edit, expect 2:00 to 2:30 finished.

## Calls I made that Krish may want to change

- **No product pitch.** No script sells CTRL or Mindmake. Several of them are CTRL's ideas, so a soft end-card line could point there later.
- **No AI provider named in the war stories.** "My AI system" covers it. One story (an AI assistant telling Krish a redesign was live when nothing had been built) was left out because the assistant was Claude.
- **Clients stay anonymous.** Script 3 says "a CEO I worked with" and nothing more.
- **Krish's own bills stay off screen.** The background-judging cost story is in the bank below if he wants to show it.
- **Kept clear of the subchannels.** Nothing here traces a deal's money, takes a product apart, or makes a multi-story dated call. Banked mind.the.gap pitches ("blaming the tool", "hidden AI use at work", "the calculator kids") and Friday's model-picking piece are left alone.
- **The banned "it's not X, it's Y" shape is said aloud once, as a quoted example,** in scripts 2 and 15. If that's a step too far, show it on screen only.
