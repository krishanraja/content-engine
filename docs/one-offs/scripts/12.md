# 12 Count your copy-pastes

- **Theme:** Level yourself up
- **Length:** about 2:45 at an unhurried pace (about 420 spoken words), plus the 4-second end card
- **Format:** to camera, tall and wide
- **Status:** draft for Krish to read, change and record

**The point in one line:** your first AI job is hiding in your copy-pastes, and once you've found it, sort it into draft, template, automate or stop doing it.

**The question it answers:** everyone's selling you an AI assistant that works while you sleep, so what's the first job you should actually give it?

**Why this one:** everyone is being sold an always-on assistant and nobody knows what to give it first. This answers that in one day, with a tally. The "funeral" line is the laugh, and the four-way sort is the bit people screenshot.

**News nod:** OpenAI launched always-on Dots agents on 29 September 2026 (SiliconANGLE, https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/). Microsoft launched Autopilot on 25 September 2026 (Microsoft blog, link in script 10).

## Script

**[0:00]**

**Say:** Everyone's trying to sell you an AI assistant that works while you sleep. So what's the first job you should actually give it? Here's my answer. Count your copy-pastes.

*On screen:* Face. A tally counter clicks.

**[0:12]**

**Say:** OpenAI launched always-on agents called Dots on the twenty-ninth of September. Microsoft has Autopilot. Plenty of choice. Almost nobody tells you which job to hand over first.

*On screen:* Two proof panels with dates.

**[0:22]**

**Say:** Here's why copy-pastes are the clue. Every time you copy numbers from one place to another, or explain the same thing for the third time, that's a job a machine could be doing. The big AI time savings hide in the boring bits.

*On screen:* Screen recording: numbers copied from an email into a spreadsheet. Click, click, click.

**[0:40]**

**Say:** So here's the test. For one working day, keep a tally. Every copy-paste. Every time you retype something. Every time you explain the same thing twice. At the end of the day, look at your top three.

*On screen:* A sticky note with tally marks next to three tasks.

**[0:54]**

**Say:** Now sort each one with two questions. How often do I do it? And how much judgement does it need, meaning how much real thinking from me?

*On screen:* A two-by-two grid: how often, how much judgement.

**[1:05]**

**Say:** Often, and it needs judgement: the AI drafts, you approve. Like your weekly client update. Rare, but it needs judgement: make a template. Like the yearly pricing review. Often, and no judgement: automate it. Like copying invoice numbers into a spreadsheet. Rare, and no judgement...

*On screen:* Each box fills in as it's said: Draft and approve / Template / Automate / ...

**[1:23]**

**Say:** ...stop doing it. Seriously. A lot of the clever automations people want to build should just be deleted. That report nobody reads? Skip the AI and give it a funeral.

*On screen:* The fourth box: "Stop". A tiny lily on top of a report.

**[1:35]**

**Say:** The same goes for the things you ask AI. If you've asked it to do the same thing three times, save the prompt and give it a name. Next time it's one click. Half the AI set-up I run my business on started this way. A job I'd done by hand ten times became a tool the eleventh time.

*On screen:* A saved prompt list, one new entry added.

**[1:59]**

**Say:** And when you pick, start with the simplest one. Get one small thing working before you build the twelve-step dream. Twelve steps that each work nine times out of ten will fail more often than they work.

*On screen:* Twelve dominoes. Most of them fall over. Small caption: "0.9 x 0.9 x ... twelve times = 28% chance it all works".

**[2:14]**

**Say:** So, back to the question. What's the first job to give your AI? The one your tally says you repeat the most. Sort it, start small, and trust the tally over your gut. The task that annoys you most is rarely the one that costs you most.

*On screen:* Face. Card: Your first AI job = your top tally.

**[2:32]**

**Say:** Your job this week: pick one working day and tally your copy-pastes. The winner is your first AI job.

*On screen:* Face. The do-this card.

**[2:40]**

**Say:** That's makeyourmindup. There's more like this, free, at makeyourmindup.ai. I'm Krish. Make your mind up.

*On screen:* Full face, then the end card.

## Do this today

Keep a tally for one working day of every copy-paste, retype and repeated explanation. Sort the top three: draft and approve, template, automate, or stop doing it.
