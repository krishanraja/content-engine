# 14 Where did your saved hour go?

- **Theme:** Ways of thinking about AI
- **Length:** about 3:09 at an unhurried pace (about 472 spoken words), plus the 4-second end card
- **Format:** to camera, tall and wide
- **Status:** draft for Krish to read, change and record

**The point in one line:** the time AI saves is only the setup, and the payoff is where you put it, so choose that on purpose and write it down.

**The question it answers:** when AI saves you time, where should that time go?

**Why this one:** everyone talks about hours saved and nobody checks where they went. It should feel like a friendly challenge: decide, even if you decide to go home early. It also carries the honest point that the hours you keep get harder.

**News nod:** Gallup's AI and productivity page (updated 30 September 2026) says about two in three employees at organisations using AI say it has helped their productivity (https://www.gallup.com/workplace/713063/ai-workplace-productivity.aspx). Flagged "check" in research: confirm the figure and its date before recording.

## Script

**[0:00]**

**Say:** AI saves you an hour. Where does it go? For most of us, straight back into the inbox. The big report for the board writes itself in twenty minutes, and the three hours you saved vanish into email by lunchtime. So today's question: when AI saves you time, where should that time go?

*On screen:* Face. An hourglass pouring into an inbox icon. Card: "Where should the saved time go?"

**[0:21]**

**Say:** Plenty of people are saving time. Gallup's latest page on this says about two in three people at companies using AI say it's made them more productive. Great. The question nobody asks next is: productive at what?

*On screen:* Proof panel: Gallup's own line, source and date, "productivity" highlighted.

**[0:36]**

**Say:** Here's how I think about it. Saving the time is only the setup. Where you put it is the payoff. Use it one way and you just get faster at the same work. Use it another way and you get better at the job.

*On screen:* Card: "Time saved is the setup. Where it goes is the payoff."

**[0:54]**

**Say:** So pick a place for that time before it arrives. Here are three good ones.

*On screen:* Face.

**[1:00]**

**Say:** First: the part of the job only you can do. The customer call you keep putting off. The hard decision. The thinking time you keep promising yourself.

*On screen:* Card 1: "Only you".

**[1:10]**

**Say:** Second: teach your AI. Next time it gets something wrong, spend fifteen minutes fixing it properly. Tell it the rule it missed, so that mistake never comes back. That's an hour that pays you back every week after.

*On screen:* Card 2: "Teach it".

**[1:26]**

**Say:** Third: build a skill the next ten years will pay for. If AI writes your first drafts, get really good at editing. If it does your analysis, get really good at asking the sharper question.

*On screen:* Card 3: "Level up".

**[1:40]**

**Say:** Or, and this is allowed, go home early. Just decide it on purpose.

*On screen:* Face, grinning. A coat going on.

**[1:45]**

**Say:** Here's a trick that makes it stick. Add a line to your weekly notes called "Time reinvested in". Next to every task AI now does for you, write where that time went. If the answer is "email", choose again.

*On screen:* A notes page with "Time reinvested in:" and a few entries.

**[2:00]**

**Say:** And be honest about one thing. When AI takes the boring work, the hours you keep are the hard ones. Fewer tasks, heavier decisions. That's why people who use AI a lot can feel more stretched than before. So plan for it. Protect one hour a day for the hardest call on your desk.

*On screen:* Card: "Fewer tasks. Heavier decisions."

**[2:22]**

**Say:** If you run a team, ask them the same question. Where's the time going? Plenty of leaders count the hours saved and never check where they landed.

*On screen:* Face.

**[2:33]**

**Say:** So, back to the question. When AI saves you time, where should it go? Somewhere you chose on purpose: the work only you can do, teaching your AI, a skill for the next ten years, or home. Anywhere except straight back into the inbox.

*On screen:* Face. The three cards again, plus the coat.

**[2:50]**

**Say:** So, this week: pick one task AI does for you, and write down where that time actually went. If you don't like the answer, give it a better place to go.

*On screen:* Full face. The do-this card.

**[3:03]**

**Say:** That's makeyourmindup. There's more like this, free, at makeyourmindup.ai. I'm Krish. Make your mind up.

*On screen:* Full face, then the end card.
