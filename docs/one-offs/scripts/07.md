# 07 Never let AI mark its own homework

- **Theme:** Level yourself up
- **Length:** about 3:15 at an unhurried pace (about 490 spoken words), plus the 4-second end card
- **Format:** to camera, tall and wide
- **Status:** draft for Krish to read, change and record (version 3: reworked to pass the five-point story check)

**The point in one line:** the chat that wrote something can't check it, so split finding from fixing, give the checker the evidence, and trace every number to a sentence.

**The question it answers:** can you trust an AI to check an AI's work?

**Why this one:** two true stories from Krish's own system carry it: a $900 million mistake that another AI scored 9 out of 10, and the same draft scoring 3 and then 9 depending on whether the checker could see the sources. Both are funny and both are proof. The three habits are the takeaway.

**News nod:** OpenAI delayed GPT-6.1 Astra on 29 September 2026. Its head of safety systems said it "didn't quite meet the bar" (Euronews, https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/29/openai-postpones-release-of-latest-ai-model-over-security-concerns-as-the-industry-faces-n; OPB, https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/29/openai-delays-latest-model-over-security-concerns/).

## Script

**[0:00]**

**Say:** Can you trust an AI to check an AI's work? Here's what happened when I tried. My AI system once turned nine hundred million dollars into "close to a million dollars". The right number was sitting right there in the source. Then I had another AI check the facts, and it gave the piece nine out of ten.

*On screen:* Face. Caption: "Can you trust an AI to check an AI?" Proof panel: "$900 million annually" in the source, then the AI's line "close to a million dollars a year", mint on both numbers.

**[0:23]**

**Say:** That's AI marking AI's homework. And it gets worse when it's the same chat. Ask the chat that wrote something whether it's good, and it mostly agrees with itself. It's like asking a student whether their essay is any good. They wrote it. Of course it's good.

*On screen:* Face. Caption punch word: HOMEWORK.

**[0:42]**

**Say:** Even the people building AI get someone else to check. On the twenty-ninth of September, OpenAI held back its next top model because, in the words of its head of safety, it "didn't quite meet the bar". The makers don't let a model sign itself off. You shouldn't either.

*On screen:* Proof panel: the quote, source and date, "didn't quite meet the bar" highlighted.

**[1:02]**

**Say:** So how do you get a check you can trust? Three habits fix most of it. The first is to split the jobs. Open two chats. In the first, say: "List the problems only, and say where they are. Don't rewrite anything." Then paste that list into a second chat and say: "Fix these. Change nothing else."

*On screen:* Two chat windows labelled "Finder" and "Fixer".

**[1:24]**

**Say:** Why two? Because a checker that writes the fix and then marks it is marking itself.

*On screen:* Face.

**[1:31]**

**Say:** The second habit is to give the checker the evidence. That means the sources: the articles and reports the draft was built from. My system had an AI checking the facts, and nobody had given it the sources. So it called a properly sourced draft "invented" and gave it three out of ten. Then we pasted the sources in. Same draft. Nine out of ten. The only thing that changed was what it could see.

*On screen:* Two score cards: 3 out of 10, then 9 out of 10. Caption: "Same text."

**[2:01]**

**Say:** So when you ask "is this any good?", paste in what you asked for and the sources first, then the draft.

*On screen:* Card: What you asked for, then sources, then draft.

**[2:09]**

**Say:** The third habit is for anything with numbers in it, like that nine hundred million. Make it show its working. Ask: "List every number, date and name in here, with the exact sentence from the source behind it. Say NOT FOUND where there isn't one." Then check the NOT FOUNDs yourself.

*On screen:* The prompt on screen, big enough to screenshot. A list with two "NOT FOUND" lines highlighted.

**[2:30]**

**Say:** And after any rewrite, check the whole thing again. AI fixing AI often swaps one mistake for a brand new one.

*On screen:* Face.

**[2:38]**

**Say:** So, back to the question. Can you trust an AI to check an AI's work? Only when you set it up properly: two chats, the sources pasted in, and every number traced to a sentence.

*On screen:* Face. Caption: "Two chats. Sources in. Every number traced."

**[2:52]**

**Say:** Here's your job this week. Take your last important AI draft. In a fresh chat, ask for every number, date and name with the exact sentence behind it. Ten minutes. A lot cheaper than explaining a wrong number to a client.

*On screen:* Full face. The do-this card.

**Say:** That's makeyourmindup. There's more like this, free, at makeyourmindup.ai. I'm Krish. Make your mind up.

*On screen:* Full face, then the end card.
