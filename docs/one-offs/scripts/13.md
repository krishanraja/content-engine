# 13 Hand over the work. Keep the understanding.

- **Theme:** Ways of thinking about AI
- **Length:** about 3:16 at an unhurried pace (about 491 spoken words), plus the 4-second end card
- **Format:** to camera, tall and wide
- **Status:** draft for Krish to read, change and record

**The point in one line:** let AI do the middle of the job, keep the framing at the start and the judging at the end, and check you can still explain the work.

**The question it answers:** if AI does the work, what's the bit you have to keep?

**Why this one:** this is the strongest line in the Mindmake canon, turned into a one-minute test anyone can run. It's about agency, so it must never tip into "AI will make you obsolete" fear. The feeling is "I'm still the one who knows", with an honest note that some skills fade if you never use them.

**News nod:** Apple shipped iOS 27 with the rebuilt Siri on 14 September 2026 (CNBC, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/apple-releases-ios-27-redesigned-siri-ai.html). Microsoft's Copilot Autopilot launched on 25 September 2026 (link in script 10).

## Script

**[0:00]**

**Say:** AI can do a lot of your work for you now. Reports, emails, sums, the lot. So here's the question: if AI does the work, what's the bit you have to keep? I'll give you a one-minute test, and a simple way to split any job.

*On screen:* Face. Card: "If AI does the work, what do you keep?"

**[0:18]**

**Say:** And it's a question for right now, because the tools keep doing more. Apple's rebuilt Siri arrived on the fourteenth of September. Microsoft's Copilot now has an agent: a helper that keeps working in the background while you get on with other things. The more they do, the easier it is to stop understanding what they did.

*On screen:* Two proof panels with dates.

**[0:41]**

**Say:** So here's the test. Pick something AI did for you this week. A report, an email, an analysis. Now explain out loud, in one minute, why it's right. If you can, lovely. If you can't, you handed over more than the work. You handed over the understanding too.

*On screen:* A one-minute timer starts.

**[1:00]**

**Say:** And that matters. If you can't explain the report, you can't spot when it's wrong. You can't ask the next question. And you can't defend it when the boss pushes back. That's the point where AI stops making you faster and starts making you a passenger.

*On screen:* Face. Caption punch word: PASSENGER.

**[1:19]**

**Say:** So how do you keep the understanding? Here's how I split every job into three parts. AI does the middle. I keep the start and the end.

*On screen:* A bar in three parts: a small slice, a big middle, a small slice.

**[1:30]**

**Say:** The start is deciding what the job actually is. What are we trying to do, and what does good look like? I call that framing. Five minutes, in my own words, before I ask AI anything.

*On screen:* The first slice lights up: "Frame it".

**[1:44]**

**Say:** The middle is the grind. Research, drafts, number crunching. AI's brilliant at it. Let it go.

*On screen:* The middle lights up: "AI".

**[1:50]**

**Say:** The end is judging. Is it right? Is it true? Would I put my name on it? That's where the quality lives, and it's where your skill stays sharp.

*On screen:* The last slice lights up: "Judge it".

**[2:02]**

**Say:** Roughly, the first fifth and the last fifth are yours. Skip them and you become a very fast rubber stamp. Whatever turns up, you just say yes.

*On screen:* Face.

**[2:13]**

**Say:** There's an honest catch. Skills fade if you never use them. Most of us got worse at finding our way once satnav did it for us. So pick the skills you want to keep, and use them by hand now and then.

*On screen:* A satnav screen. Then a person with a paper map, looking smug.

**[2:30]**

**Say:** If you manage someone junior, have them do one task this week without AI, and explain their thinking to you. That's how people get good enough to judge AI in the first place.

*On screen:* Face.

**[2:43]**

**Say:** So, back to the question. If AI does the work, what do you keep? The start, the end, and the understanding. And you know you've kept it when you can explain the work in one minute.

*On screen:* Face. The bar again, both end slices lit.

**[2:57]**

**Say:** So, this week: one thing AI made for you, one minute out loud on why it's right. If you can explain it, it's yours. If you can't, you've got some reading to do.

*On screen:* Full face. The do-this card.

**[3:10]**

**Say:** That's makeyourmindup. There's more like this, free, at makeyourmindup.ai. I'm Krish. Make your mind up.

*On screen:* Full face, then the end card.
