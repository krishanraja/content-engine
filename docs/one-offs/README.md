# One-off scripts for the main channel

Ready-to-record video scripts for the makeyourmindup YouTube channel, outside
the three subchannels: practical, durable AI habits (building your AI brain,
levelling yourself up, ways of thinking about AI) in the house voice, each with
a short nod to that month's news.

Asked for by Krish on 2026-10-06: "trawl all of these repositories (fully,
there is a lot of good theory and content in there) for all of the individual
top tips and good ideas ... create individual video scripts ... practical,
useful, understandable by a 12-year-old, funny, useful ideas anyone can do,
with real-world examples, tied to productive business outcomes, acknowledging
the latest in AI news briefly in the last few weeks".

## Where things are

Plus `news.md` (dated AI news, 8 September to 6 October 2026) and `voice-format.md` (how a one-off script is laid out and sounds) |

## Rules for anyone writing the next batch

- Start from `trawl/` and the bank at the end of `scripts/00.md`. Check the
  Top 15 lists and the privacy flags before choosing.
- Stay outside the subchannels: no tracing a deal's money, no taking a product
  apart part by part, no multi-story pattern with a dated call. Leave the banked
  mind.the.gap pitches alone (`trawl/engine-mymu.md`, the last section).
- Voice: reading age 12, real humour aimed at the hype, British English, no em
  dashes, no exclamation marks, no "Not X, Y" in any form, no sermons, no
  "like and subscribe". About 330 to 380 spoken words.
- One-offs never pass the engine's fact gate, so every news line and number is
  checked by hand on the morning of recording. `news.md` marks the items that
  only had secondary sources as "check".
- Client names are replaced with `[client]` in `trawl/`. Never put a client,
  their company or a private person into a script. Tell a client story only as
  "a CEO I worked with".
