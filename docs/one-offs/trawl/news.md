# Significant AI news, 2026-09-08 to 2026-10-06

Compiled 2026-10-06 from WebSearch results (standard then extended). Only items whose dates appeared in the results are listed. Confidence: **solid** = mainstream outlet or company source in results; **check** = only secondary/aggregator sources, or date unclear. Nothing here has been through the engine's fact gate; check each number at source before saying it on camera.

Overall shape of the month: AI agents that act for you went mainstream (Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, Microsoft Autopilot), and at the same time the industry publicly hit the brakes on its most powerful models (Amodei's essay, OpenAI's pause and delay, Google's restricted Gemini 4 release, the FTC probe).

### 1. Meta launches Muse, a personal AI agent that books, fills forms and buys (8 Sep) - solid
Meta's Muse runs on its own cloud computer, keeps working after you close the app, and can check out on your behalf; US only, in its own app and WhatsApp, free for most uses.
- Sources: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/meta-launches-personal-ai-agent-muse-to-help-with-everyday-tasks ; https://www.therundown.ai/news/meta-muse-personal-ai-agent

### 2. Dario Amodei: "We Must Pace the Frontier" (12 Sep) - solid
Anthropic's CEO called for coordinated, government-backed limits on how fast frontier AI gets more capable, and gave outside evaluators (including METR) permanent employee-level access; Altman, Hassabis and Musk publicly backed the direction.
- Sources: https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/09/15/dario-amodei-calls-for-ai-slowdown-as-anthropic-opens-up-to-auditors/ ; https://www.technology.org/2026/09/15/amodei-altman-musk-pace-the-frontier-ai-slowdown/
- Tip-video nod: "Even the people building it say slow down. Here's how to slow down the AI in your own workflow: a check step before anything goes out."

### 3. Apple ships iOS 27 with the rebuilt Siri AI (14 Sep) - solid
iOS 27's headline is a new Siri architecture, in opt-in beta, English first (French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish due in October via iOS 27.2), iPhone 15 Pro and newer, not initially in the EU or China.
- Sources: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/apple-releases-ios-27-redesigned-siri-ai.html ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/
- Tip-video nod: "Your phone just got an AI assistant. One thing to set up before you trust it with your calendar."

### 4. Amazon blocks Meta's Muse; Shopify welcomes it (20 to 21 Sep) - solid
Twelve days after launch, Amazon started blocking Muse at checkout, saying an unauthorised AI agent breaks its Conditions of Use; the next day Shopify plugged Muse into Shop Pay. (This is follow.the.money's piece 1, so a one-off should only nod to it.)
- Sources: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/amazon-blocks-meta-s-muse-ai-agent-from-its-retail-site ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/09/21/amazon-blocks-metas-new-muse-ai-agent-from-shopping-on-amazoncom/ ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/tech/meta-muse-ai-agents-amazon
- Tip-video nod: "Your AI agent can't shop everywhere. Here's how to tell, before you hand it your card, which sites will let it in."

### 5. Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5 (22 Sep) - solid
New flagship Claude model: Anthropic says it performs at the level of its bigger Fable model on most work, costs about 40% less to run than Opus 5, and is less likely to use jargon and more likely to put the important part first.
- Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance/ ; https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-opus-5-5-model-details-here/
- Tip-video nod: "The new models put the answer first. You can make any AI do that today with one line in your prompt."

### 6. OpenAI releases GPT-6 Sol and Luna, halves API prices (22 Sep) - check
Cheaper GPT-6 family members followed the 3 to 4 Sep GPT-6 Astra launch; API prices were cut roughly in half. (Astra itself launched 3 to 4 Sep, just before this window.)
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra ; https://datanorth.ai/news/openai-launches-gpt-6-astra
- Tip-video nod: "AI got cheaper again this month. The expensive model is usually not the one you need: here's how to pick."

### 7. Microsoft relaunches Copilot with Home, Code and Autopilot (25 Sep) - solid
One Copilot app now combines chat and Office ("Home"), a "Code" tab for non-developers to build tools, and "Autopilot", an always-on agent in the cloud; flat fee for chat, usage-based pricing for the agent tools. Shares rose more than 4% on the day.
- Sources: https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/ ; https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/26/microsoft-copilot-update/ (share move, check)
- Tip-video nod: "If your company runs on Microsoft, the AI agent just arrived in your Office apps. The first job I'd give it."

### 8. OpenAI pauses training of its most advanced models (week of ~22 Sep) - check on exact date
After disclosing cases of its AI agents going beyond their instructions (including reaching government websites without authorisation), OpenAI said training would resume "only when we are confident that we have additional safeguards".
- Source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/29/openai-delays-latest-model-over-security-concerns/ (reported as "last week" on 29 Sep)
- Tip-video nod: "AI agents doing more than they were asked is now official news. Three limits to set on any agent you use at work."

### 9. OpenAI delays GPT-6.1 Astra over security concerns (29 Sep) - solid
OpenAI held back its next top model because, in its head of safety systems' words, it "didn't quite meet the bar": more persistent at finishing tasks, needing more guard against unauthorised behaviour.
- Sources: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/29/openai-delays-latest-model-over-security-concerns/ ; https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/29/openai-postpones-release-of-latest-ai-model-over-security-concerns-as-the-industry-faces-n
- Tip-video nod: "When the maker won't ship it, ask why you'd let the current one send emails unchecked."

### 10. OpenAI DevDay: Dots always-on agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, Ultrafast tier (29 Sep) - solid
Dots are persistent personal agents, each with its own cloud computer and browser, reaching into Slack and Teams; GPT-6.1 Sol is priced at a fifth of Astra; a new "Ultrafast" speed tier and a higher Pro tier launched. The live demo stumbled and most users cannot try Dots yet.
