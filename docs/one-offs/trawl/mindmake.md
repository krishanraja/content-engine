- The idea in one plain sentence: AI creates value at three levels (help me do the task, bring me what matters before I look, extend what I can do so decisions keep improving), and most people stop at level one.
- Why it matters: Level 1 saves minutes; level 3 changes outcomes. Helps people aim higher than faster drafting.
- Source: `project-documentation/00_NORTH_STAR.md`; `01_CANON.md` ("The value ladder"); `leadershipChapters.ts`. Quotes: "One: help me do the task. Two: bring me what matters. It joins the evidence... It prepares the next move."
- Real-world example: Level 1 email draft; level 2 a morning brief of what changed; level 3 a system that remembers every pricing call and improves the next.
- "Do this today": Label each way you use AI as 1, 2 or 3. Pick one level-1 use and ask how to turn it into level 2.
- Strength: 5

### Competent output is cheap now. Judgement is the scarce thing.
