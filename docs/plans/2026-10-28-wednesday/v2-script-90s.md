I got early access to Claude Duet, and the weirdest thing about it is that it already knows me.

Duet is Claude's new app that messages you first. Every few hours it checks your email, calendar and other apps, sends you a morning brief and drafts the work. Then it waits for your yes before it sends, pays or signs anything.

There's no setup quiz. It brings across everything your Claude already knows about you. The catch is it only reads one email account, and mine is mostly bills.

It found me ten jobs I'd only do once they were on fire. It's slow, it loves a long think, like me. And it uses up your Claude limit.

The best bit: I asked for a calendar scheduler, and it built me a dashboard I can send to people. That's where software is going. You ask once, and you get a tool.

Here's what I'd do with this info if I were you. Pick one job you only do when someone nudges you. Make sure the information sits in apps Duet can reach. When you get it, check every draft before you say yes.
