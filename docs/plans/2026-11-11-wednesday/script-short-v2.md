Does this track sound familiar? It's all over TikTok and all over the charts. But which came first, TikTok or the charts? And is it AI music, or human music? Let's find out.

It started with a Reiki healer and an AI app. She typed her lyrics and a vibe, pressed a button, and got a whole song back.

People put it under their own videos, like cats, weddings and dads dancing badly, so TikTok came first. Strangers went hunting for the song, and a top producer found it. He pulled it apart and rebuilt it by hand, then got the nineties club singer Ultra Naté to sing it. And under her voice, he kept a thin layer of the AI singer. So the hit has a robot humming along underneath.

Even the Grammys reportedly ruled it out for songwriting, because of the AI bits still in it. But it can still compete for singing.

So I reckon AI versus human is the wrong question. Next time I love something, I'll ask who did which bit. Because the song you can't stop dancing to is already both.
