Ask who did which bit

The same three questions work for a song, an advert or the deck your team sent last night.

1
Whose idea was it?
The person who had the idea
2
What did a tool draft?
The first version a machine made
3
Who checked it and put their name to it?
The person who stands behind it, and who you call when it goes wrong
