I've been reading court papers on Character.AI, a chat app with millions of users. It ran low on cash, faced lawsuits over harm to children, and went bust. So why did Google, and now Disney, both pay for it?

Google got the brains first. In 2024 it reportedly paid two point seven billion dollars to use the tech, and hired the founders away.

Disney wanted its characters in AI videos, so it agreed a billion-dollar deal with OpenAI's video app, Sora. Then OpenAI shut Sora down.

So Disney is building its own team, hiring sixty-six staff on sixty million dollars a year in total. And by buying through the bankruptcy court, Disney leaves the lawsuits behind.
