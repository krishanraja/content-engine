I've been reading court papers on Character.AI, a chat app with millions of users. It ran low on cash and faced lawsuits over harm to children. So Disney is buying through the bankruptcy court, and leaving the lawsuits behind. So why did Disney and Google both pay for it?

Google got the brains first. In 2024 it reportedly paid two point seven billion dollars for the tech, and hired the founders away.

Disney wanted AI for its characters, so it agreed a billion-dollar deal with OpenAI's Sora app. Then OpenAI shut Sora down.

So now Disney buys its own team: sixty-six staff, paid sixty million dollars a year in all, plus the code. And the users get no money.
