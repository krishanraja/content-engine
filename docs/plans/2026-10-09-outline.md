# Friday 16 October, mind.the.gap: Soon you won't pick your AI

1. Intro: I've been noticing the AI apps I use every day have split into lots of models, and soon you won't see any of them.
2. The split is real, and the models exist for the companies' reasons, not yours.
3. The turn: the menu is already going. GPT-5 came with a router that picks for you.
4. Money: whoever picks decides how much model your money buys.
5. Getting the job done: when the router broke, GPT-5 "seemed way dumber"; when a task fails you can't tell why.
6. Building on AI: costs and quality move under you; measure the cost of a finished job.
7. Power: loyalty moves from the model to the app; the choice gets buried in settings.
8. What to do: pick the model yourself when it matters; rerun the same test every month.
9. Prediction, no percentage: by the end of 2027 the big AI apps show no model list unless you ask.
