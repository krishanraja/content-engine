Spine: If AI takes the work juniors used to learn on, who trains the seniors you'll need in 2030?
Answer: Earlier tools took the dull bottom of a job and the job came back bigger, but AI takes the work juniors learned on and the cut shows up as people never hired, so before you cut a junior role, name who will be your 2030 senior and what real work they learn on.

1. THE BOTTOM RUNG IS GOING | proves: at Anthropic junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9% since early 2025, and a study of US firms finds junior hiring slowing wherever AI is adopted
2. WE'VE BEEN HERE BEFORE | proves: cash machines and spreadsheets took the dull bottom of a job, the work got cheaper, people bought more of it and the job came back bigger
3. WHAT'S DIFFERENT THIS TIME | proves: this time the work going is the work juniors learned on, and the cut shows up as people never hired
4. WHO TRAINS YOUR 2030 SENIORS | proves: managers now hire for judgement, which people used to learn as juniors, so the gap is every business's to fill
5. MY FIRST RUNG | proves: the dull job is where the lesson lives, so ask who on your team is learning on one, and if you're junior, ask for one
