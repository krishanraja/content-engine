Spine: What does Claude Duet actually change for someone who already works in Claude?
Answer: It turns your job from asking Claude into approving what it brings you, and that only sticks if you hand it a weekly job whose inputs live in the apps it can reach, so start there.

1. WHAT DUET DOES ON ITS OWN | proves: it checks your connected apps every few hours, briefs you each morning and prepares work, and stops for your yes before it sends, pays, signs or deletes
2. WHAT PEOPLE THINK OF DUET | proves: early users find it polished and useful for business tasks, and slow and easy to forget without a job that pulls them back
3. FROM ASKING TO APPROVING | proves: for a power user the change is the habit, starting the day with what it brought you, and that habit forms only around a job that comes round every week
4. WHAT COMES WITH YOU AND WHAT STAYS BEHIND | proves: the apps it reaches carry over, while local files, some skills, custom connectors and your plan limits are the catch
5. WHERE TO HOOK IT IN FIRST | proves: the weekly job you now do by copying and pasting from those apps is the one to hand it first, read-only for a week
6. OUR PREDICTION | proves: by 1 March 2027 Duet starts the bigger Claude jobs on your computer as well as seeing them, which makes it the place a power user starts the day
