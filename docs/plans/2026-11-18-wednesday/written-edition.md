## Note

Anthropic makes Claude, the AI a lot of companies now write software with, and it still pays other companies for its payroll, its customer records and its help desk. When it needed a help desk bot it bought Fin, had it live in under a week, and Fin runs on Claude.

That's the bit I keep coming back to for any business with a keen builder on the team. AI has made the first version of a tool cheap. The expensive part is the next three years: someone fixing it, keeping it safe, and taking the call at two in the morning when it breaks.

So my rule is simple. I'd build the part that makes us different, and buy the boring plumbing where a vendor already takes that call for thousands of companies.
