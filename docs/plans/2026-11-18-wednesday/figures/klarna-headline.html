Klarna: the headline and what happened

The story said AI replaced two of the world's best-known business apps. Klarna's own boss said otherwise.

The headline
Klarna switched off Salesforce and Workday and replaced them with AI.
What happened
Workday went to Deel, another rented app. Customer records went to other rented apps on Klarna's own data layer. Slack, owned by Salesforce, stayed.
The chief executive, 4 March 2025
"No, I don't think it is the end of Salesforce."
Sebastian Siemiatkowski on X, 4 March 2025 · CX Today, 11 December 2024
