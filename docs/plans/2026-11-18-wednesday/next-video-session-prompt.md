every point below yourself. Do not make him find any of these again.

**1. Finish the job; do not hand him approvals.**
"You need to stop making me approve and just fix this until it's done. I refuse to say "approve engine" ever again.
If you know that things need fixing, just fucking fix them and then pull from content engine main and file everything

**2. The hook is the first 3 seconds, and it moves.**
"considering that's the first few seconds of the video, it's not much of a hook. It looks quite dull for the first 3
seconds."

"Can't cut my head off and also ruin the visual above."

**5. The title gets a title treatment.**

**6. Captions stay off his face and stay readable.**
- Words over a busy picture or his take get the halo he approved ("Approve halo", "approve the lift"). No caption

"This piece needs to be hilarious and tongue-in-cheek ... use codex for all asset production, generation, sourcing."

**Before you show him a proof, run these checks:** the first 3 seconds move; his head is whole and, on the wide cut,
bottom right; the title is a card; no caption page is over his face; there are no page breaks mid-phrase; nothing
