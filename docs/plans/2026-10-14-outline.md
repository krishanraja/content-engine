# Wednesday 14 October, under.the.hood: three kinds of AI, and where Muse fits

Re-pivot, 2026-10-14. Krish: "we are comparing apples and oranges a little bit. Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini are not really personal assistants". Krish chose to record new videos to this outline ("Or we can record new", 2026-10-14). The article is rewritten to this argument.

1. Intro, his style: I've been having a little play around with Muse, Meta's new personal assistant, and everyone keeps comparing it with ChatGPT and Claude. That's apples and oranges.
2. There are now three kinds of AI.
   - Chatbots: you ask, it answers. The ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude apps.
   - Work agents: plugged into your work tools, given a job, left to run. Claude Cowork, ChatGPT dots, Gemini Spark. Built for people at a desk.
   - Ready-made assistants, the new kid: already on the phone you own, set up for you, running your errands. Muse, by Meta, and Siri AI, by Apple. Built for everyone else.
3. Which company is in which camp, and why: each follows its money. Meta (ads, billions of people in Instagram and WhatsApp) and Apple (iPhones) build ready-made assistants. OpenAI, Anthropic and Google sell monthly plans to people who work, so they build work agents.
4. Who uses each: a chatbot for anyone with a question; a work agent for someone who lives in documents, email and spreadsheets; a ready-made assistant for the person who has never opened a chatbot.
5. The scorecard, same six criteria, read in two bands. Among ready-made assistants, Muse and Siri tie, for opposite reasons. The work agents lose on price and reach because they were never built for that market.
6. Why it matters for businesses: they already do AEO so chatbots mention them. The new question is which kind of AI to plug into. Local, everyday businesses (restaurants, salons, trades) go with ready-made assistants, because their customers are already on WhatsApp and the iPhone. Software office workers use goes with work agents. Big brands and publishers keep chasing chatbot answers.
7. Prediction (Krish chose A, 2026-10-14), no percentage: by the end of 2027, a big consumer business (a supermarket, an airline or a food delivery app) lets Muse or Siri buy from it directly, and doesn't give ChatGPT the same deal.
8. Close: next Wednesday goes under the hood of the ready-made assistants (OpenClaw first, then Grok's assistant and Muse): what each says it can do against what it does.

# Wednesday 14 October, under.the.hood: Muse scored against its rivals

1. Intro: I've been having a little play around with Muse, Meta's new personal assistant.
2. I scored Muse against ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Siri on six things, flashed up as comparison cards.
3. Muse wins on what you notice: does the job, price, reach, asks first.
4. Muse loses on what you can't see: who can read your stuff, whose side it's on.
5. Why the last one matters: shops stop paying to be seen and start paying to be picked.
6. Muse and Siri tie, for opposite reasons.
7. Prediction, no percentage: by the end of 2027 businesses pay to be the one your AI assistant picks.
