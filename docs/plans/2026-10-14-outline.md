# Wednesday 14 October, under.the.hood: Muse scored against its rivals

1. Intro: I've been having a little play around with Muse, Meta's new personal assistant.
2. I scored Muse against ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Siri on six things, flashed up as comparison cards.
3. Muse wins on what you notice: does the job, price, reach, asks first.
4. Muse loses on what you can't see: who can read your stuff, whose side it's on.
5. Why the last one matters: shops stop paying to be seen and start paying to be picked.
6. Muse and Siri tie, for opposite reasons.
7. Prediction, no percentage: by the end of 2027 businesses pay to be the one your AI assistant picks.
