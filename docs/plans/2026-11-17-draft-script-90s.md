This month, America held 41 local votes on new AI data centres, and all but one went against them. So where do the builders put AI now?

People fear these giant buildings push up their own power bills. Someone has to pay for the new power lines. So the builders are looking for ways round, and there are three.

One is to bring your own power, with a power plant right next to the data centre. But a gas plant brings its own fight with the town. Two is to go small, with lots of little data centres beside solar farms. But a small site can't train a brand new AI. Three is to shrink the AI so it runs on your own phone or laptop. But a small AI gives simpler answers.

My call is that all three push AI the same way. It gets smaller, closer to you, and brings its own power.

If I ran a business, I'd ask my AI supplier one question this week. Where are you building the data centres my tools run on? If the town's fighting them, expect delays.
