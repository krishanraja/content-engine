## LinkedIn

I gave two AI assistants a few jobs from my actual life. One wrote a lovely reply to a scammer and made sending it my next job.

Customer service, for people trying to rob me.

Grok Bot caught it. Meta’s Muse didn’t. That would make a wonderfully tidy verdict, except another tester’s fake-business experiment had both resist the scam traps.

So I gave the wider research equal weight alongside my own tests. Flights, emails, the day plan, the things I keep meaning to cancel. Grok came out ahead on my accounts. The community evidence gave Muse a better case, and gave me a reason to test Grok for longer than an afternoon.

Here’s what I’d do with this info if I were you: hand one boring weekly job to one assistant. Let it look things up, sort things and draft. Keep spending and sending behind your approval. Check the dates, the number of nights and who an email is really from.
