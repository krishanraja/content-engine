## Who is who
- Imael Angel is the stage name of Bernadett Gango, a Hungarian-born Reiki master living in England. She writes the lyrics and ideas and makes the first versions with Suno and Udio, so the original vocals are AI-generated. Sources: hu.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imael_Angel; frequence3.com (catalogue made with Suno and Udio); Companies House, IMAEL ANGEL RECORDS LTD (17344041), incorporated 16 July 2026.
- HUGEL is a human DJ and producer. He rebuilt Movin' To The Sun by hand (MIDI programmed by hand, rebuilt instruments) with a new vocal by Ultra Nate, who is 58. HUGEL says the only AI left in the master is a layer of Angel's original AI vocal under Nate's. His team's "about 95% human" estimate is not independently verified (DJ Mag France). Source: weraveyou.com, Sept 2026; edm.com.
- Bad Times: the version on radio and streaming was re-sung by a human, Cali Bee, and reproduced by Robin Brandes, under exclusive licence to Universal Music GmbH (Qobuz credits); Official Charts lists EMI, UK peak 67. Broadcaster MistaJam: "completely reproduced and re-vocaled by humans".

## The numbers
- Movin' To The Sun: UK peak No. 2, 19 weeks on the chart (Official Charts); led Beatport's global chart for nearly 90 days, a record (Blikk, Hungarian tabloid: treat as reported).
- Imael Angel: 113 million Spotify plays and 11 million YouTube plays (Blikk: reported).
- Bad Times: No. 1 on Shazam in Greece.

## The pipeline (the layers the piece peels)
1. AI sketch: a non-musician writes lyrics and has Suno or Udio make a demo.
2. Short-form virality: the demo spreads as background audio on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
3. Shazam pull: viewers identify the song from someone else's video. About a third of Reddit mentions are people asking "what's this song?" under unrelated clips (cats, a father-daughter dance).
4. Human rebuild: a producer re-records it (HUGEL with Ultra Nate; Robin Brandes with Cali Bee).
5. Major-label licensing: Bad Times to Universal.
6. Radio and charts: the human-credited version charts.

## The Grammys split one song in two
- On 17 Oct 2026 The Australian reported the track ruled out of songwriting categories (the released version still uses material from Angel's Suno original) and eligible for performance categories. So one recording is "AI" for songwriting and "human" for performance at once. Nominations 16 Nov 2026.
