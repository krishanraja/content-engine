# Narrative lock: Wed 11 Nov under.the.hood - AI is already in the music you love

piece_id: 36886e0b-4b32-473b-be72-b0aef31fccef

## Surface story
Two songs people have danced to all autumn, Movin' To The Sun and Bad Times, are by Imael Angel, an AI-generated singer. HUGEL, a human DJ, remixed Movin' To The Sun with Ultra Nate and it went into the UK charts; Bad Times was re-sung by a human singer and licensed to Universal.

## Deeper story
"AI music or human music?" is already the wrong question. Peel back either song and you find people and AI at every step: a person with the song idea, an AI voice, human producers remaking it, a human DJ remixing it. AI is infiltrating everything at every layer, it is entering the mainstream fast, and it is becoming how humans create, so comparing the two to see who is better misses what is happening.
Krish: "the main goal is to get people who just think about things in binary terms (AI music versus human music) to realise that AI is infiltrating everything at every layer."

## Arc
- Open on the songs: he talks over the two clips people already know and like.
- The surprise: the singer does not exist.
- The twist: peel the layers of each song and people and AI swap places at every step.
- The turn: the binary breaks, because this is how music, and soon most work, is being made.
- Close on his line, back on the song the viewer was dancing to.

## The fun
The viewer is caught out: they liked the song before they knew. Each layer peeled is a small reveal. Music people know, and a story told like gossip.

## The use
No "3 things you can do" (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-03: "This piece doesn't need the three things you can do. It needs people to think differently and to be surprised and enjoy the piece."). The change is in how the viewer thinks: they stop asking "is this AI or human?" of a song, an ad or a piece of work, because the answer is already both.

## His close
The song you can't stop dancing to is already both, and that's how music gets made now.
Krish adopted this close: "I agree with your suggestion." (2026-11-03)
