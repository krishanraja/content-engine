# Narrative lock: Wed 11 Nov under.the.hood - A Reiki master wrote a No. 2 hit, and it is not AI versus human

piece_id: 36886e0b-4b32-473b-be72-b0aef31fccef
Publishes: 2026-11-11

## Surface story
A Reiki master with no music background, Bernadett Gango, typed her lyrics into Suno and released the songs as Imael Angel, with an AI voice. Movin' To The Sun reached No. 2 in the UK after HUGEL, a human DJ, rebuilt it with Ultra Nate singing; Bad Times was re-sung by a human, Cali Bee, and licensed to Universal. People have danced to both all autumn.

## Deeper story
AI tools let someone who would never have got in write a hit, and people carried it the rest of the way. So "AI music or human music?" is the wrong question: these songs are both at every layer, a person's idea, an AI voice, human producers, a human singer, a major label. The Grammys even split one song in two, AI for songwriting and human for performance. AI is entering the mainstream fast and becoming how humans create, so comparing the two to see who is better misses what is happening.
Krish: "the main goal is to get people who just think about things in binary terms (AI music versus human music) to realise that AI is infiltrating everything at every layer."
Krish, on the way in: "Perhaps this is more of a story about how people that wouldn't have gotten this level of success have done because of AI tools then?" and "Sounds good but I think there's still a lot of power in helping the user realise that it's not AI versus human." (2026-11-03)
