# Fri 13 Nov · mind.the.gap · Counting rat tails · storyboard

Creative direction (Krish, 9 Nov): take the viewer back to 1902 Hanoi with generated footage, then show, step by step,
how staff game an AI-use leaderboard. One idea runs through it: what gets counted versus what you want.

## Three looks, one grammar

| World | Look | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1902 Hanoi | Photoreal period film: warm faded autochrome colour, grain, soft vignette, slight gate weave | 9 generated clips + 9 stills (`fri-1902-generated.json`) |
| 2026 office | Isometric miniature diorama (house motion style `isometric-world`): tiny staff, desks, one glass leaderboard tower with crowned rank bars; coral = what is counted, mint = real work | 7 generated clips (`fri-office-generated` in `fri-1902-generated.json`); Codex device `office_floor` stays in the library |
| Krish | Full frame for the hook, the turn to "your company" and the close; everywhere else a small rounded window (about a fifth of frame height), bottom right on the wide cut, bottom left on the tall cut (clear of Instagram's right rail and above captions); off for one or two seconds on the tailless rat and the Token Legend slam (Krish, 9 Nov: face in the bottom corner) | `measuring-ai-long`, `measuring-ai-short` |

Evidence (the Shopify and Duolingo lines, the Fortune and CIO pages, the study's own two charts) appears as a screen on the
office wall that zooms to full frame, marked on its own words (`living-ui` rules), then zooms back into the office.

## Transitions (the same five everywhere)

1. **Into 1902 (the film gate).** His frame warms, grain rises over 12 frames, a projector flicker, and an ink rat
   silhouette runs across the frame as the wipe. Out of 1902 is the same in reverse.
2. **Inside 1902.** Cuts on his words; between two clips a 10-frame film dissolve with a little gate weave. Never a
   zoom or a slide.
3. **The match cut (the signature).** The rat farm clip ends on its high, near-isometric angle. The office floor enters
   in the same grid: crates become desks, cages become server racks, the warm grade drains to the house ink over
   18 frames, and the first coin drops. 1902 is now 2026.
4. **Office to evidence and back.** The camera glides to the wall screen; the screen fills the frame; the mark draws on
   the words; the screen shrinks back into the wall.
5. **Back to Krish.** A straight cut to his take on the word that turns to "you" or "I".
