# Narrative lock: Fri 13 Nov mind.the.gap - Companies ranked staff by how much AI they burn

piece_id: f3fcd5e1-573e-4692-a413-db7c5823a10e
Publishes: 2026-11-13

## Surface story
Companies told their staff to use AI and started counting it: in performance reviews, on dashboards and on leaderboards. Meta's internal "Claudeonomics" board ranked about 85,000 people by tokens burned, with titles like "Token Legend" and "Cache Wizard". Amazon's KiroRank did the same. Staff set AI agents running on junk tasks to climb them, and both boards were shut down.

## Deeper story
Whatever you count, people learn to produce. Business has made this mistake for over a century, and in 18 months the AI boom ran the whole cycle: mandates in 2025, leaderboards in spring 2026, quiet U-turns by autumn 2026. When one company cut its AI usage target in half, the junk fell away, real use held and sales rose 7%. The question is never how much AI your people use; it is what got done.

## Arc
- Open on Hanoi, 1902: a cent a rat tail, so people snipped the tails, let the rats go and farmed more.
- Act one, the mandates: Shopify, Duolingo, Microsoft and Coinbase make AI use something you are judged on.
- Act two, the leaderboards: Token Legend, Cache Wizard, KiroRank, and agents left running to climb them.
- Act three, the U-turns: Meta drops token counts from reviews, Amazon calls the metrics "deeply flawed" and says "tokenmaxxing is at odds with frugality".
- The proof: the company that halved its target and sold more.
- The punchline: the cobra story everyone tells about this is itself made up.
- Close on his line, back to the rat tails.

## The fun
Real leaderboard titles, an engineer who posted "Whenever my PM says something ridiculous I launch 10 sub-agents to analyse him" (read as one engineer's post), rat farms, and the twist that the most famous story about counting the wrong thing never happened.

## The use
3 things you can do tomorrow (Krish, 2026-11-03: "Agreed on those"):
- Scrap any target or ranking for how much AI people use.
- Make "AI didn't help here" a good answer: Amazon now calls it "a finding, not a failing"; say the same to your team so nobody fakes it.
- Judge AI on a number you already track, like sales, tickets closed or invoices sent: if it has not moved, the AI is not working yet, whatever the usage says.
