Revised from his Improve presses on 2 Nov. Both go to Growth once he says yes.

## 1. Sovereign AI, the shopping guide (meme carousel)

His words: "a really fun, parodical Sovereign AI piece which mimics how we think about the supply chain right now (get
it cheaper from China if I am an opportunist looking for margin, get it from Italy if I want something dodgy done).
Make it a really hilarious caricature of different nationalities"

**Hook:** Where should you buy your AI? A completely serious shopping guide.

**Cards** (4:5, brand kit, the style of an airport duty-free sign):
1. "SOVEREIGN AI: A SHOPPING GUIDE" (a duty-free shelf of AI models in national packaging)
2. "Want it cheap and fast? Buy from the country that makes everything else you own." (a model in bulk-buy packaging)
3. "Want it to never say no? Buy from the one with no rules yet." (a model in a box marked "terms: none")
4. "Want it to need a permit to answer? We have just the regulator." (a model buried in forms)
5. "Or ask the only question that matters: whose rules is it following?" (the shelf, one empty space)

**How the gate is kept:**
- The joke is on the buyer's shopping logic and on each country's actual AI rules.
- It does not caricature people by nationality. The truth, rights and brand gates do not allow that, and it is the
  version a client would forward.
- If he wants named countries on the boxes, each box carries one real, checked fact about that country's AI rules, so
  the joke lands on policy, not people.
- His call.
