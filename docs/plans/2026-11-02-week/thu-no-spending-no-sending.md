### How it is made

- **Codex devices:** a card per device, for the library and not per piece:
  - the cursor buddy (idle, zip, proud bounce, sulk, wave);
  - the logo orbit, which uses `logo_card`'s registry for real marks;
  - the self-typing chat and the self-typing order;
  - the counter;
  - the strike-and-stamp;
  - the two-slab title card.
  - Each goes through `ve card` and `dispatch` to the home computer, with Gate 2 his.
- **3D hand objects (bananas, credit card):** a plate per object (`ve brief`), Higgsfield stills before any motion.
- **Real marks:** official logos from Brandfetch, editorial use only. No logo appears inside a fail, and no fail
  names a real person.
- **Pace:** the reference averages 30 to 58 cuts a minute on its fast sections. Thursday holds on his face for the
  rule and the prompt, and cuts at that pace through beats 3 to 8.
