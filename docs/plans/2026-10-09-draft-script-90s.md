I've been watching what's coming with AI, before everyone notices. I'm Krish.

The AI apps I use every day now run lots of models, which are the AI engines underneath. So I wanted to break it down for you. What happens when the app picks your AI for you?

Claude Code alone lists more than a dozen. But that menu is going. OpenAI launched GPT-5 with a router that picks for you.

First, money. The app maker decides how much engine your money buys. On a busy day, I'd bet you get the cheaper one. Second, getting the job done. When OpenAI's router broke for part of a day, Sam Altman said GPT-5 "seemed way dumber". So when a task fails, you can't tell why.

If you build on AI, measure what a finished job costs, not the price per word.

My prediction. By the end of 2027, the big AI apps won't show you a list of models unless you ask. You'll get one box.
