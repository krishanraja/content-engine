I've been following how money is changing because of AI. I'm Krish.

So I've been reading the big AI firms' price pages. And I wanted to break it down for you. On 1 December, Google's newest AI doubles in price. So what should a sensible business do when AI prices keep moving?

Firms pay for every batch of about 700,000 words the AI reads. Google's price for that goes from $2 to $4.

And Google isn't alone. OpenAI cut its best AI's price, but only as a promotion with an end date. Anthropic, which makes Claude, cancelled a price rise. Now all three charge $4 a batch. And every one of those prices moved this year.

So I think three rules hold. Budget at the full price. Put the price in your contract, fixed until a date. And keep the freedom to switch.

On Monday, ask whoever runs AI where you work: was next year's budget built at the full price?
