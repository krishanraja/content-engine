## Take any song apart on your own laptop. $259 until 7 December.

- What it is: iZotope Photon pulls a finished song apart into its parts, and up to six voices out of one recording, on your own computer. It costs $259 until 7 December, against $349 after.
- Source: [Newsshooter, iZotope Photon: GPU-powered audio processing software (5 Nov 2026)](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/11/05/izotope-photon-gpu-powered-audio-processing-software/)

## A free music studio with 3 million sounds. The catch is the export button.

- What it is: Splice Create is a free Mac music app with more than three million licensed sounds built in and an assistant you can talk to. Making is free; exporting and licensing need the $9.99-a-month plan.
- Source: [The Fader, Splice launches its own DAW, Splice Create (6 Nov 2026)](https://www.thefader.com/2026/11/06/splice-launches-its-own-daw-splice-create)

## Spotify's AI DJ can finally stop talking.

- What it is: Spotify's AI DJ now has nine new voices and a setting that cuts most of the talking between songs. For an artist, that means the DJ that introduces your track can now talk less and play more.
- Source: [TechCrunch, Spotify's AI DJ gets new voices, personalities and an option for less chatter (10 Nov 2026)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/spotifys-ai-dj-gets-new-voices-personalities-and-an-option-for-less-chatter/)
