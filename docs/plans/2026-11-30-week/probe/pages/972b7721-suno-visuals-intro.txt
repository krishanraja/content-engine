Introduction to Suno Visuals
Edited
Ready to set your music in motion? Suno Visuals brings image and video creation together in Suno on web and mobile, giving you new ways to explore ideas, design artwork, and tell stories through your music.
With Suno Visuals, you can:
Create images and artwork using text prompts and reference images. Make song covers, original images, and new variations of things you've already created.
Bring your ideas into motion by animating images, creating video clips, and using Lip Sync to make characters sing or speak along with your audio.
Edit and build on your videos with prompt-based changes, customizable on-screen lyrics, and longer videos called Sequences, made by extending and combining clips in the Visuals Editor.
Choosing how you create
Every image and video is generated in one of a few modes, each with its own balance of speed, quality, and credit cost. Fast is great for quick drafts, Standard balances quality and cost, and Max (for images) gives you the highest quality.
Where everything lives
Your images, video clips, and Sequences are saved in your Visuals library, where you can view, download, share, or keep working on them
Ready to start?
Head to your Create page and select Visuals. For details on Visual credits, purchasing additional credit packs, or generation costs, visit our Visuals Pricing page.
