What are Visual Credits?
Edited
Alongside the launch of Suno Visuals, we’re introducing Visual credits to give you more ways to bring your music to life through images and videos.
Previously, credits could be used to generate songs as well as certain types of visuals. Going forward, those credits will be exclusively used for song generations and will be called Song credits. All image and video generations, including edits that generate new visual content, will now use Visual Credits.
Purchased Visual credits never expire, so you can use them whenever you like. You can check out your credit balance at any time on your Account page.
For details on Visual credits, purchasing additional credit packs, or generation costs, visit our Visuals Pricing page.
