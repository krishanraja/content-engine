Welcome to the Anthropic Copyright Settlement Website
You may be a Class Member if you are the legal or beneficial copyright owner of a book included in the Anthropic Copyright Settlement.

Current Status of the Settlement

On July 20, 2026, the Court granted final approval. A copy of the Court's order is available on the Important Documents page.

Next Steps

On November 13, 2026, you should have received a notice if at least one of your claimed Works is scheduled for payment. Please log in using the link below and carefully review the details of any Works for which payments will be issued. You will need the Claim Number and PIN included in your notice.
Payment Summary Portal
If you disagree with the allocation for any Work scheduled for payment, you must dispute the payment by November 20, 2026. The Work will then be removed from the first distribution, but will still be eligible for subsequent distributions after any discrepancies are resolved.
If you agree with the payment allocations, no further action is needed at this time.

Payments

We currently expect to send the first round of payments the week of November 23, 2026.
Payments will be issued in multiple installments. The first installment will be about 70% of the total award for each Work. The remainder will be paid after Anthropic makes its final payment into the Settlement Fund. See FAQ 11 for more details.
Payments will be issued consistent with the payment method selected on each validly submitted Claim Form. Please inform the Settlement Administrator of any updates to your payment information after the submission of your Claim Form, including your current address if you chose payment by check.

Works That Remain in Disagreement

Any works for which disputes are not resolved before the first distribution of Settlement payments will not be included in the first distribution and will instead be included in a subsequent distribution. 
For claims where all claimants do not agree on the appropriate allocation for a work, claimants may submit additional information supporting their proposed allocation.
You can still log into the portal to review and make updates to these Works. If you would like help reaching out to other rightsholders for your Works, please contact the Settlement Administrator at info@AnthropicCopyrightSettlement.com or 1-877-206-2314.
If rightsholders cannot resolve a disagreement by meeting and conferring, the disagreement can be submitted to the Court-appointed neutral Special Master for resolution. For more details about the Special Master, please review FAQ 26.
Please check this website for updates.

Your Legal Rights and Options

The deadline to submit a claim was March 30, 2026. The deadline to exclude yourself (opt-out) or object passed on February 9, 2026. If you previously excluded your works, you can no longer reinclude them. The deadline to do so was March 9, 2026. Please read the complete Court-approved Notice for more information.
