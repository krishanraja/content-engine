Ubisoft Reportedly Encouraging The Use Of Generative AI For All Projects
Michael Harradence / November 26, 2026
Ubisoft is apparently keen to have all its projects utilise generative AI in some capacity, to the point where if a developer doesn’t want to use the technology, they will have to justify why.
Vertical Slice’s Chris Kerr spoke to sources that claim Ubisoft wants its teams to use generative AI to speed up development times, although some people are reluctant to do so. “The attitude shift among our leaders [means] we all feel cornered by generative AI all the time,” one source commented.
Meanwhile, another source said that while the use of AI isn’t mandatory, it is highly encouraged due to the fact Ubisoft views AI development as “unavoidable.” They added, “if you planned on not using it, you would have to have a reason.”
Ubisoft announced earlier this month that it has set up a new studio, Motherbrain, which is dedicated to the use of generative AI in video games. It follows a major restructuring at the French publishing giant, which started earlier this year with the company establishing Creative Houses to lead its core franchises, as well as the cancellation of six titles.
Last Friday, Ubisoft announced that it had laid off 166 developers across its Montreal and Quebec studios.
[Source – Vertical Slice via VGC]
