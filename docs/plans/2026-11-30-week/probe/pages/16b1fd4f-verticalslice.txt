Update: Ubisoft's internal pitching process is becoming increasingly AI-pilled
Multiple Ubisoft workers have aired critical opinions of the publisher's generative AI strategy, including one who claimed staff are trapped in a "top-down nightmare of AI psychosis."
Nov 25, 2026
Image via Vertical Slice November 25 2026 2:55 PM 3 min read Share Share Share Share Share Email Copy Update [26/10/26]: Ubisoft has issued the following comment in response to our story:
We encourage our teams to explore where AI can be useful and bring value to their work. This doesn’t mean that projects are required to use generative AI. Teams need to determine whether, and how, these technologies make sense for the game they want to make and the experience they want to create for players.
Original story [25/10/26]: Ubisoft is requiring internal teams pitching projects to explain how they will leverage generative AI to accelerate and streamline production. Those staff who do not wish to deploy the divisive tech must comprehensively detail why generative AI is not suitable.
According to multiple current Ubisoft employees, speaking to Vertical Slice anonymously for fear of reprisal, teams who fail to do either of the above when pitching will not receive the go-ahead.
The information was disclosed during a presentation from company leadership to Ubisoft employees. One source tells me Ubisoft is not outright mandating the use of generative AI, but said the Assassin's Creed maker is all-in on the technology.
They explained Ubisoft has always toyed with new technology—just look at the experiments publicised by the company's 'La Forge' research and development group—but said there has been a paradigm shift of late. "The attitude shift among our leaders [means] we all feel cornered by generative AI all the time," they added.
