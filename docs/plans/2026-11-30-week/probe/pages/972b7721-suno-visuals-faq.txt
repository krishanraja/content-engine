Suno Visuals: FAQ
Edited
Who can use Suno Visuals?
Anybody can buy Visuals credits. For a limited time, paid plans will receive a grant of credits to try Visuals!
Is Suno Visuals available on mobile?
Yes. If you’re not seeing Visuals on iOS or Android, don’t forget to update to the latest app version!
What’s the difference between Song and Visual credits?
Song credits are used to generate songs, and Visual credits are used to generate videos and images. To view both your Visual and song credit balance, check out your account page.
If you’d like to purchase more, Visuals credit packs are available at www.suno.com/pricing/visuals.
How do I get access? What’s a credit pack?
For a limited time, Pro and Premier subscribers will have a limited number of Visual credits to try out the product.
Any user can purchase packs of Visual credits by going to www.suno.com/pricing/visuals.
Any Visual credits you purchase don’t expire and can be used at any time.
What happens when I run out of Visual credits?
To purchase additional Visual credits, please visit www.suno.com/pricing/visuals. Purchased packs do not expire and can be used at any time.
How many credits do I need to make an image or video?
Credit cost for a Visual is determined by a few variables including the duration (for videos) and quality you select. To check your Visual credit balance, check out your account page.
What types of content are allowed in Suno Visuals?
We encourage creativity and self-expression while maintaining a safe and welcoming environment for everyone. All content created, uploaded, or shared must follow our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
What models do you use?
The model is called H3 Move, based on H3 from MiniMax, which we optimized for our community including adding additional protections and safeguards. The model is self-hosted and we do not share data with MiniMax.
What’s the difference between Fast and Standard?
When creating a Visual video, you’ll have two options for quality, fast or standard. Fast is great for quick ideas and brainstorms. Standard is for more complex ideas. Fast costs less Visual credits than Standard.
