## sat/goodnews: AI in the hospital this week: five good things.

Five real stories from one week of AI in hospitals: catching PTSD risk early, giving a woman with ALS her voice back, reading 7,800 heart records, sparing lung cancer patients a rushed test, and giving nurses time back. Found on 13 Nov; each is checked against its source again before it is made, because it posts about two weeks later.

Source: [Your saved post asking for a positive ray of sunshine, and each story's own source](https://nyulangone.org/news/ai-predicts-ptsd-risk-based-stories-about-what-brought-patients-emergency-room)

- **AI spots who may get PTSD from what they say in A&E** (2026-11-09, health). Doctors at NYU Langone let an AI read the stories patients told about why they came to the emergency room, and it flagged every one of 30 people who later developed PTSD. [NYU Langone Health news, 9 Nov 2026](https://nyulangone.org/news/ai-predicts-ptsd-risk-based-stories-about-what-brought-patients-emergency-room); picture: https://engineering.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/square_large_default_1x/public/2024-04/Screen%20Shot%202024-04-24%20at%209.42.06%20AM_0.png?h=28926231&itok=3otiIhox
- **A woman with ALS speaks again in her own voice** (2026-11-11, people). Anne-Marie Poirier, who has ALS, types a few words into the Vocality app and it speaks a full sentence in a voice built from her own recordings. [Global News (The Canadian Press), 11 Nov 2026](https://globalnews.ca/news/12147285/quebec-woman-als-ai-voice-app); picture: https://globalnews.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/c75cdc637fd60095cac21aba396acbb56fe7325dd73fc5cc25f58f485e8ef89a.jpg?quality=65&strip=all
- **AI read 7,800 heart patients' records in a fraction of the time** (2026-11-13, health). Cleveland Clinic used an AI to review the records of more than 7,800 heart valve patients and predict whose new valve may wear out. [NVIDIA blog, 13 Nov 2026](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/how-ai-is-helping-care-teams/); picture: https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Screenshot-2026-11-12-at-9.25.28-AM-1280x592.png
- **AI could spare one in three lung cancer patients a rushed test** (2026-11-13, lab). Doctors at Memorial Sloan Kettering and Mount Sinai showed an AI reading tissue slides could have let 26 of 80 lung cancer patients skip a rushed gene test without missing anyone who needed it. [Clinical Lab Products, AMP 2026 report, 13 Nov 2026](https://clpmag.com/diagnostic-technologies/digital-pathology/ai-tools-cancer-classification-reporting-amp-2026/); picture: https://images.ctfassets.net/iujm5coq335o/nh8PTB9sk2mkJJRR4P889/e407c12bd35839440dfa8ecc7bc8f5a5/EGFR_AI_02.jpg
- **Sensors that give nurses time back raise $5.5 million** (2026-11-11, work). Akara, a Trinity College Dublin spinout, raised $5.5 million for sensors and AI that track how hospital rooms are used without filming anyone. [Trinity College Dublin news, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.tcd.ie/news_events/top-stories/featured/akara-funding-round/); picture: https://pxl01-tcdie.terminalfour.net/prod01/channel_3/media/tcd/news-images/Akara-Founders-RS-800X582.jpg
