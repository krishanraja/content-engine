## 6. The personality pass (house rule VOICE_ON_CAMERA, live since today)

Each script carries three of five. Two options for each, yours to cut:

- **Self-deprecating:** "This whole publication is made with Claude, so take the next ninety seconds with a pinch of salt" / "I pay for both of these. I am the product in this story."
- **Sarcastic, at the hype:** "'The wrong tradeoff.' Twice in one year. At some point it is just the tradeoff." / "Nuclear option is their phrase, not mine. I would have said 'Tuesday'."
- **Exaggeration, obvious by its size:** "Paying full price for a chatbot to tell you what day it is" / "A gym that lets you in five times as often and hides the door."
- **Analogy:** the ice cream counter (continuity) / the gym card / the buffet that quietly shrinks the plates.
- **Opinion as opinion:** "My bet: the five times is a gift, and gifts end." / "I think the meter is the real price, and I think they would rather you never looked at it."

Register choice, in order of how far you want to go: **dry** (one or two of
the above, delivered flat) / **warm** (three, with the ice cream callback) /
**full tilt** (all five, faster cut, the sound bed). The draft is warm.
