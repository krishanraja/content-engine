# Friday 9 October: fact check of Krish's seed research

The seed was "Why AI Model Names Are Proliferating, and Who Benefits", Perplexity
research Krish commissioned from his own idea. The engine treats it as raw material: every load-bearing
claim was checked against its primary source on 9 October 2026.

## Corrections to the seed
- OpenAI's ~$70bn annualised revenue (Reuters, 29 Sep) was revised: Reuters,
  8 Oct, says OpenAI told investors its September figure was almost $50bn.
- Gross vs net: Anthropic counts revenue gross; OpenAI nets out Microsoft's
  share (the seed said "hyperscaler shares").
- Claude Code lists 14 models, not 8; Haiku 5.5 ($0.10/$0.50) is the current
  Haiku and Haiku 4.5 is legacy.
- OpenRouter has 35 Anthropic IDs in its API (41 on its page), not 54.
- The OpenRouter "week ending 4 Oct" shares (1.8%, 1.0%) cannot be reproduced;
  live data runs to 8 Oct, Opus 5.5 is #9 at 3.83T tokens, Astra is outside the top 10.
- The 320x reasoning-token growth is year on year, not calendar 2025.
- Claude 3.5 Sonnet launched in June 2024, not August 2025.
- GPT-5 replaced five named models, not "about nine".
- Thinking cannot be turned off on Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1
  and Opus 5, not only Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1.
- The seed's ending ("not a conspiracy; it is...") breaks house rule R2.
