Your AI thinks it's the best. Guess who told it.

At school you don't mark your own test. Big companies now hold their AI to the same rule.

The Home Depot has Google's AI answer its customers, and OpenAI's AI check the answers. Microsoft has OpenAI's GPT write research reports, and Claude, from rival Anthropic, check them before anyone reads them.

Why bother? Scientists found that an AI gives its own writing better marks than people do. A different AI catches what the first one misses.

Try it tomorrow: paste your AI's answer into a different company's AI and ask it what is wrong.
