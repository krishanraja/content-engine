## Why AI works well in hindsight (the light lesson)
- The past sits still: the records are finished, the rules they should have followed are known, so every flag can be
  checked against the page. The future has no page to check against. AI is a poor fortune teller and a tireless
  accountant. (Our framing; consistent with every case below, where the win is a flag a person or checker confirmed.)
- Counterweight: chatbots on their own get tax returns wrong (NYT test and TaxCalcBench: refunds off by over $2,000 on
  average; Kiplinger on viral "AI found my refund" posts, one of them Grok). https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/can-ai-help-you-find-a-bigger-tax-refund
  To check on the primary test.

## Funny wins (money found in old paperwork)
1. The $195,000 hospital bill. A man's brother-in-law died after a heart attack; insurance had lapsed two months before;
   four hours of emergency care billed at about $195,000. He asked for the itemised bill and gave it to Claude, which
   flagged double billing and wrong codes; the hospital came down to about $33,000 (some outlets say $37,000).
   Source: Threads post by "nthmonkey" (Oct 2025); Tom's Hardware / TechSpot coverage
   https://www.techspot.com/news/110064-family-turns-ai-audit-195000-hospital-bill-reducing.html
   Status: his own account, widely reported; the hospital has not confirmed. Tell it as his account.
2. The Reddit tenant. Chris Maloney, 23, Amherst NY (Reddit u/monkeyincidentof93): his landlord kept money from the
   deposit; he pasted the lease into ChatGPT, which pointed out New York caps a deposit at one month's rent and he had
   paid a month and a half. The landlord countersued for $5,000. The judge awarded Maloney $1,180 and dismissed the
   counterclaim. https://scoop.upworthy.com/tenant-uses-chat-gpt-to-uncover-crucial-evidence-and-win-legal-battle-against-greedy-landlord
   Status: amounts vary by outlet; check his own Reddit update before using a number. Date likely late 2023.
3. The 20-year-old OpenSSL bug (Google OSS-Fuzz, 20 Nov 2024): "likely present for two decades". Checked 13 Nov.
4. Old mammograms (UCLA, JNCI, May 2025): AI flagged 90% of the visible-but-missed cancers. Checked 13 Nov.
5. A 40-year-old step in a maths proof (Axiom, 26 Oct 2026): maker's claim, no outside review yet. One line at most.

## The auditor in a business (it catches what nobody had time for)
6. Fake receipts, made with AI, caught by AI (AppZen data, vendor's own): AI-made receipts went from about 0% of the
   fakes it caught in March 2025 to 70.8% by May 2026; 1,471 AI-made receipts from 745 employees at 174 companies, $148,143
   claimed. Emburse survey: four in ten US employees have used AI to make a fake receipt.
   https://www.appzen.com/blog/catching-ai-generated-fake-receipts-with-ai ; https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3892615
   Status: vendor data, say so on screen.
7. Duplicate supplier payments: recovery-audit vendors (apexanalytix, PRGX) report AI finding duplicate payments and
   unused credits across millions of old invoices; McKinsey cites one company finding over $10m in duplicate payments.
   Status: vendor case studies; use as "the kind of thing it finds", never as a named number unless checked.

## Careful what you wish for (the skeletons)
8. Dana-Farber, Jan 2024. Blogger Sholto David used his eyes and ImageTwin (AI that compares a paper's images with
   about 50 million others) on papers from 1999 to 2017. Dana-Farber moved to retract six and correct 31. It has not
   concluded misconduct: "The presence of image discrepancies in a paper is not evidence of an author's intent to deceive."
   https://www.nbcnews.com/science/science-news/allegations-research-misconduct-roil-dana-farber-cancer-institute-rcna135521 ;
   https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/top-harvard-cancer-institute-will-retract-six-studies-correct-31-more-after-photoshop-claims-180983642/
9. Krafton, 16 Mar 2026 (Fortis Advisors v. Krafton, Delaware Court of Chancery). Krafton's CEO asked ChatGPT how to get
   out of a $250m earnout; it suggested a task force to renegotiate or a "Take Over". The court quoted his chats and found
   the firing of the founders pretextual; reports say he deleted the logs. The newest skeleton in the closet is your own
   AI chat history. https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/04/20/how-a-buyers-ai-conversations-sank-its-earnout-avoidance-strategy/
   Status: to read on the opinion itself.
