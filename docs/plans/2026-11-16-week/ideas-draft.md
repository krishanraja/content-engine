## WEDNESDAY 18 Nov: under.the.hood (4 options)

### W1. An AI found a hole in a 1986 maths proof. Here's why you can trust it. (RECOMMENDED)
- **Cue:** 5, "AI can help us backtrack on things that have not been true."
- **Hook:** "For forty years, one of the biggest proofs in maths had a step that didn't hold, and a team of AI agents found it in under a month."
- **Beats:**
  1. The proof: the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, about 15,000 pages in more than 500 articles by about 100 mathematicians. (Confirmed, Unite.AI.)
  2. What Axiom did: in 24 days, dozens of AI agents wrote about 43.1 million lines of Lean, a language a computer can check line by line. (Confirmed.)
  3. Why it's real: the AI can't bluff, because a separate checker accepts or rejects every step. That's the difference from a chatbot that sounds sure of itself.
  4. What it found: 34 noteworthy deviations from the published record, mostly small. One was a real gap in John Walter's 1986 work, where the agents built counterexamples to a step he assumed. The theorem itself still stands. (Confirmed.)
  5. The limit: per secondary coverage, Axiom's CEO says no one outside the company has rebuilt and checked it yet (search only). AI can backtrack well when there's a checker. Where there isn't one, as with health claims, it is much weaker.
- **One thing to do tomorrow:** Pick the one number your business repeats most, the "fact" in every deck, and ask for its original source plus one independent check.
- **Sources:**
  - "Axiom Math Releases 43.1M-Line Lean Proof of Group Classification", Unite.AI, 27 Oct 2026. https://www.unite.ai/?p=495307
  - Primary (not opened, but linked from Unite.AI): Axiom, "The Enormous Theorem, Formalized", 27 Oct 2026. https://axiommath.ai/research/the-enormous-theorem-formalized/
  - Search only, for the wider point: JMIR 2026 study finding that none of nine free AI tools reliably flagged retracted papers. https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e88766
- **Why now:** Announced 27 Oct. It's the cleanest recent case of AI correcting the record.
- **Honesty note:** The CEO's "not independently checked" remark is from search summaries only. Open Axiom's page before scripting.

### W2. "Twice as good as doctors." Real, or a staged exam?
- **Cue:** next to 5 (AI checking received wisdom in medicine).
- **Hook:** "A new study says an AI diagnosed hard cases nearly four times as well as doctors, so let's look at what the doctors were allowed to use."
- **Beats:**
  1. Microsoft's MAI-DxO, published in *Science*, was 83% accurate on 304 hard NEJM cases. The 40 doctors in the study were 22% accurate on their own knowledge. (Confirmed, SingularityHub.)
  2. On everyday emergency room cases it scored 80% against the doctors' 47%, at about $1,300 a case against more than $4,700. (Confirmed.)
  3. The theatre check: the cases were text simulations, with no scans, no rushed shift and no patient saying no to a test. The cost figures use 2023 price data. (Confirmed caveats.)
  4. A real-world test for contrast: Google's AMIE chatted with 98 real urgent-care patients at Beth Israel in Boston. Supervisors had to stop it zero times, and it put the right diagnosis first 56% of the time. A doctor watched every chat. (Confirmed, Tech Insider summary of the Lancet paper.)
- **One thing to do tomorrow:** When a vendor quotes a "better than humans" score, ask what the humans were allowed to use during the test.
- **Sources:**
  - "Microsoft's Medical AI Is Over Twice as Good Diagnosing Diseases as Doctors", SingularityHub, 4 Nov 2026. https://singularityhub.com/2026/11/04/microsofts-medical-ai-is-over-twice-as-good-diagnosing-diseases-as-doctors
  - "Patient AI in Primary Care: 114 Patients, 0 Stops", Tech Insider, Nov 2026. https://tech-insider.org/patient-facing-ai-primary-care-amie-114-patients-zero-safety-stops-2026/
- **Why now:** The *Science* paper is days old.
- **Honesty note:** AMIE was funded by Alphabet, had no control group and ran in 2025.

### W3. Claude can now build your ad campaigns. Who holds the button?
- **Cue:** none. It's adtech-native.
- **Hook:** "As of this week, an AI can build and change your ads on six platforms, and the only thing stopping it going live is one person pressing approve."

- **Sources:** Agile Brand Guide, 4 Nov 2026 (above).
- **Why now:** Launched 3 Nov.
- **Honesty note:** Possible overlap with 4 Nov "Claude Duet / AI acts first". All the figures are vendor-reported.

### F1. Most AI work is now done by AI, for AI (RECOMMENDED)
- **Cue:** none directly.
- **Hook:** "This year, AI agents started using more AI than people do, seven times more."
- **Beats:**

  5. Caveat on air: OpenRouter is one marketplace that leans towards developers, and Menlo is an investor in it.
- **One thing to do tomorrow:** List one repeat task in your team that a person does in a chat box today, and ask what it would take for an agent to run it overnight.
