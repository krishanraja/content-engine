## 1. End message

"The same question can get a different answer, or a refusal, depending on which country built the AI, so
test the one you rely on with the questions your business depends on."

## 2. Who it is for, and what they do on Monday

A business leader whose team relies on one AI for research, writing or customer answers. On Monday they
ask that AI, and one built in another country, the same three questions their business depends on, and
write down where the answers differ.
