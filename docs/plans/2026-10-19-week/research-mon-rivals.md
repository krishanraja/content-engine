# Research: how the big AI firms keep changing their short-term prices (Monday 19 Oct)

Asked by Krish on the board, 19 Oct: "Can we compare the equivalent Claude & ChatGPT Enterprise token pricing right
now, and see if they are making any similar discount moves? Also I think the point of this article is to get the
message across that the Big AI firms (call it that) are always trying to tweak their short term offers given how cut
throat their competitors are, and give business owners an idea of what actually is durable thinking amidst all this."

Read on 19 Oct 2026 at each firm's own page. Quotes are as the fetch reported them; each is filed verbatim on the
piece before the fact gate.

## Google (Gemini 4 Argon), already filed

- Pricing page (updated 16 Oct): Standard $2.00 in / $0.10 reused / $10.00 out through 30 Nov 2026; $4.00 / $0.20 /
  $20.00 from 1 Dec 2026. Batch and Flex half that, both periods.
- Launch post (13 Oct): "introductory price"; "After the introductory period expires on November 30, 2026, the price
  of $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens will apply."

## OpenAI, https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog

- 21 Aug 2026: "GPT-5.6 Sol now costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens" (the entry says
  input down 20% and output down 33%).
- 21 Aug 2026: "GPT-5.6 Sol's promotional pricing is available at least through November 21, 2026."
- openai.com/api/pricing (business plans page as fetched) shows seat prices only, and says "Credit-based pricing and
  token-based pricing are available for Enterprise plans" with no rates: Enterprise token prices are negotiated, not
  public. GPT-6 Astra $10 / $1 reused / $50 out is in the earlier corpus (OpenAI pricing page dated 13 Oct), still to
  re-read at the original.

## Anthropic, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/api

- 10 Aug 2026: "The introductory pricing for Claude Sonnet 5 ($2 / $10 per MTok) is now the standard price: the
  previously scheduled increase to $3 / $15 per MTok on September 1, 2026 will not occur."
- 1 Sep 2026: Claude Fable 5.1 "at $10 / $50 USD per MTok, the same as Claude Fable 5, with cache reads cut to $0.25
  per MTok."
- 22 Sep 2026: Claude Opus 5.5 "at $4 / $20 USD per MTok (Claude Opus 5 is $5 / $25)."
- 7 Oct 2026: "We've lowered the price of prompt cache reads on Claude Sonnet 5.5 from $0.20 USD to $0.10 USD per
  million tokens: 0.05x the base input price instead of 0.1x."
- 7 Oct 2026: "Claude Max and Team plans now include monthly API credits."
- Pricing page (filed): Claude Opus 5.5 $4 in / $0.20 reused / $20 out.

## What it shows (our read, labelled as inference in the piece)

- Three firms, ten weeks, at least six price moves: a launch price that ends (Google), a promotion with an end date
  (OpenAI), a cancelled rise and a lower launch price (Anthropic), and reuse prices cut twice (Anthropic).
- The top models are converging on $4 in and $20 out: Argon from 1 Dec, GPT-5.6 Sol (promotional), Claude Opus 5.5.
- Precedent for prediction A: Anthropic has already turned an introductory price into the standard price (10 Aug).
- "Enterprise" token prices are negotiated and unpublished at OpenAI; public rate cards are what the piece compares.
