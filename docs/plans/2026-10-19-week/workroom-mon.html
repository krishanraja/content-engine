Google's newest AI doubles in price on 1 December

Where the decisions for this piece live. Narrative signed, outline approved, both by Krish on 19 Oct.

The end message

On 1 December the price of Google's newest AI doubles for everyone paying the public rate, and the businesses that set it up to reuse what it has already read still pay about 95% less on that part of the bill, so find out which kind you are, and what your contract says, before December.
