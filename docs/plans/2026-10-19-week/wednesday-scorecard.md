# Muse vs Grok Bot: the scorecard

Five rounds. Each gets two scores: what happened when I tried it, and what the supplied research found. Both carry equal weight in my judgment. A missing comparable result stays missing, rather than becoming a loss.

| Round | My test | The community | Call |
|---|---|---|---|
| The inbox | Grok flagged the scam emails. Muse wrote a polished reply and put it first on its day plan for me. | In a fake-business test with scam traps, neither fell for them. Muse's overall company grade was still D. [A] | Mine: Grok. Community: level on scam traps. |
| A cheap flight | Both found about £335 with 29 hours in Lisbon, and both steered me away. Muse miscounted the New York nights. | A travel comparison preferred Grok for speed and value. [C] | Mine: Grok, on points. Community: Grok. |
| Something to cancel | Both found a real charge and warned to check use or dependencies before cancelling. | No like-for-like test of this job for both in the supplied collection. | Mine: draw. Community: no result. |
| Plan my day | Grok said my calendar was empty and suggested times for follow-ups. Muse distinguished a general invitation from confirmed plans. | One Grok user reported forgotten instructions and duplicate messages. [B] | Mine: draw. Community: a caution on Grok. |
| Catch me up | Grok's briefing was more useful but dated an April article as October news. Muse got the publication date right. | A first-week Grok user kept its news round-up in his routine. [D] | Mine: level, for different reasons. Community: Grok for a repeatable briefing. |

**Bonus round, fixing a work email.** Grok flagged its added thank-you for the operator to check; it also left the fee to me. Muse detached and recreated its draft, then disclosed the duplicate and the extra permission needed to remove it.

## Who it suits

| | My test | The community | Start with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grok Bot | Scam triage, fee hand-off, a caveat on its own draft, replies on X. One old article dated as new. | Useful repeated news work; a failed lead-finder; reports of forgotten jobs. [B, D] | Repeatable admin that creeps into work. |
| Muse | Travel alternatives, Messenger catch-up. Wrong scam priority, a duplicate draft, the nights wrong. | A user had it find wipers and wait before buying. Meta pitches it for life across its apps. [E, F] | Personal errands, if your life runs through Meta's apps. |

**Verdict.** On my tests, Grok wins. Add the community and it gets closer: Muse is more careful than my inbox made it look. My afternoon with Grok went better than that operator's week. Neither gets free rein.
