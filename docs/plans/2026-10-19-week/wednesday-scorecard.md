# Muse vs Grok Bot: what I'd actually hand over

For a leader trying a personal assistant on everyday life, with work creeping in. Observed personal runs: 20 October 2026. Research: the three supplied collections from last week, with selected originals read again on 20 October.

## How the evidence counts

**My tests: 50%. Research: 50%.** Each practical judgement must consider both columns. Agreement strengthens the case; disagreement stays visible. These are equal editorial weights, not task-success percentages. The cells remain plain, with no marks or invented numerical league table.

Research means the actual task and source behind a claim. A demonstrated test, an unverified user report and a vendor promise have different evidential strength. Repeated rows from the same source count once. If one half lacks relevant evidence, the combined judgement is **not established**; the missing half never turns into zero or an automatic pass.
