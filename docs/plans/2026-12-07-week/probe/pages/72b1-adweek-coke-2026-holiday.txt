AI Is Off the Festive Dinner Menu In Coke's 2026 Holiday Ad

After two years of AI-generated trucks and creatures, the soda brand that's synonymous with Christmas is putting humans back in the spotlight

 "Friends and family, strangers too" gather around the holiday table.

Key Takeaways

Coca-Cola's 2026 holiday spot ditches AI after backlash last year.

The brand resurrected its iconic 1971 jingle "I'd like to buy the world a Coke" with updated lyrics.

Coke's shift mirrors a broader industry trend of brands reassuring consumers their creative remains human-made.

Coca-Cola’s 2026 holiday ad is just as notable for the warm-and-fuzzies it brings as for what it’s lacking: AI (as well as its signature polar bears).

Titled “Enjoy Joy” and created by WPP Open X, led by Ogilvy, the 30-second spot is pure Norman Rockwell’s America. 

Friends and family toss a football around in the yard of a white country farmhouse as the men deep fry a turkey. In the cavernous dining room, it’s a potpourri of food, laughter, embraces, and two liter bottles of Coke. 

Trimmable to 15- and 6-second formats, the spot has both English and Spanish versions.
