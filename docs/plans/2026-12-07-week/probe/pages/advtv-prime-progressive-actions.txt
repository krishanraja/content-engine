Prime Video unveils Progressive Actions for Champions League
November 23, 2026
Prime Video has unveiled its Progressive Actions feature, an innovation for its UEFA Champions League coverage that highlights the passes and ball carries that drive a team up the pitch, and the often-unsung players behind them.
Developed by Prime Video’s team of producers, engineers, AI experts, and on-air analysts, Progressive Actions is the first Prime Insight built specifically for the Champions League.
Progressive Actions is built on proprietary AI-powered models that identify a Progressive Action based on how far the ball travels in each phase of the pitch, with the threshold becoming shorter closer to the opposition goal where defences are more compact and space is harder to find. The metric mirrors the data used behind the scenes at Europe’s elite clubs, yet has historically been difficult to show during a live broadcast. As a player makes a Progressive Action, an on-pitch graphic will show where the action started and ended, the player involved, and the distance the ball advanced, allowing fans to see the game as a coaching team would, in real time.
“Prime Vision is where we develop new features that shine a spotlight on the metrics, actions and players that impact a game most, yet aren’t always captured by traditional statistics or broadcast techniques,” commented Andrew Hornett, Director of Live Sports Production for Europe at Prime Video. “Every team has players whose influence goes far beyond the scoresheet, the ones doing the crucial work in the build-up, moving the ball forward two or three passes before an assist. Progressive Actions is designed to capture that impact, measuring and visualising these actions during a live broadcast. Fans will see them in real time as they happen, but we’ll also aggregate the data across the game, so you can see which players have had the biggest impact on the match.”
The feature will debut in the UK and Ireland on the evening of November 24th when Arsenal face Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium on Matchday 5 of the Champions League and will also be available for fans in Germany and Italy. Fans can access Prime Vision, with features including Progressive Actions, via their remote from the match detail page or the in-game menu during any live UCL fixture on Prime Video.
Progressive Actions is the first Prime Insight built from the ground up specifically for Prime Video’s UEFA Champions League coverage, and sits alongside Tactical Map, Momentum, Physical Insights, Advanced Analytics, Passing Options, and Player ID in Prime Vision’s growing suite of features designed to take football fans closer to the game, all available on the alternate feed during every UEFA Champions League match live on Prime Video.
