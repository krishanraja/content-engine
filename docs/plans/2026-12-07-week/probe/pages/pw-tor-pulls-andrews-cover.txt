 Tor Pulls Ilona Andrews Cover Over Generative AI Use
 By Natalie Chandler
 |
 Nov 24, 2026
 Loading the Elevenlabs Text to Speech AudioNative Player...
 Tor Publishing Group has withdrawn the cover art for This Crown Will Not Rule Me, the upcoming fantasy novel by husband-and-wife writing team Ilona and Andrew Gordon, after learning that generative AI was used in its creation without the knowledge of either the publisher or the authors.
In a November 20 statement, Tor said it would not use the imagery in any finished books. The publisher apologized to the authors and to readers, and said it would strengthen its internal review procedures to prevent similar incidents.
"Tor takes full responsibility for the breakdown in our review process," the publisher said, noting that its existing policies and contracts with cover artists prohibit the use of generative AI. Tor said it would share a replacement cover as soon as one is available. The publisher did not respond to requests for further comment. 
In a blog post, the authors, who use the pen name Ilona Andrews, said they also have a no-AI provision in their publishing contract. After readers raised concerns about the cover when it was revealed, the authors notified Tor and requested proof that the artwork was created by a human.
Andrews said the authors had been unable to identify potential problems during the initial review. The publisher had provided a sketch that appeared to be a conventional cover illustration, and the authors, who said they lack expertise in detecting AI-generated artwork, did not see cause for concern.
Andrews said they may begin requesting artists' project files and intend to review individual artworks rather than relying on an artist's reputation or assurances.
"We can no longer vet the artist. We have to vet every individual painting," she wrote.
Andrews also asked readers not to target the artist on social media, saying the authors did not know the circumstances surrounding the incident. 
Tor has previously faced scrutiny over AI-generated cover art. In February 2024, PW reported that readers had criticized Bramble, Tor's romance imprint, for using AI-generated stock images in the cover design for Gothikana by RuNyx. Tor also faced criticism in 2022 over the cover of Christopher Paolini's Fractal Noise, which used an AI-generated image.
The controversy comes amid broader concerns among authors and publishers about how to identify and prevent undisclosed generative AI use in creative work. Andrews described the challenge as particularly difficult because the technology is becoming more sophisticated.
She said the authors had terminated a separate contract with a vertical drama company after discovering that the level of generative AI used in the company's other productions did not align with their preferences. Andrews said the authors had specified that AI not be used and that only human actors and writers be involved, but acknowledged that they had not adequately researched the company's work before signing.
This Crown Will Not Rule Me is scheduled for publication on Sept. 21, 2027.
