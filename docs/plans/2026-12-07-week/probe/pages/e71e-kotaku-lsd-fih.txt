The Most Popular Game From Epic Games’ AI MegaJam Contest Is Pure Human-Made Slop
LSD: FIH is a big fish in a small, AI-slop filled pond

Epic Games’ 2026 Epic AI MegaJam, a game jam contest that allowed and encouraged developers to incorporate “any AI tools” into their four-day-long workflows, wrapped up on November 15, and voting is already underway. However, regardless of who takes home the bulk of the $50,000 cash pool, the popular vote has already been decided: PolyCube’s LSD: FIH–a fully human-crafted “fih aura-farming” experience.
While the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam urged participants to “vibe [their] way to $50k,” the game jam’s FAQ states developers didn’t actually have to use AI in any capacity to win any of the categories. As spotted by Automaton, one developer proudly took advantage of this loophole, and their human-made slop has already won the hearts of the public.
PolyCube’s LSD: FIH is currently the most popular game on the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam itch.io submissions page, and the developer proudly states in the game’s bio that “no AI was used to make this game.” In the game you play as a fish with a sniper rifle, and your job is to shoot balls with your rifle as quickly as possible in a bid to keep your combo counter going.
Of course, given the internet’s well-documented distaste for Epic Games’ new “AI first” pivot in the last few years, there is a concern that LSD: FIH may simply be a protest vote—an attempt to stick it to Tim Sweeney by crowning a non-AI game as the winner of his AI MegaJam. So, just in case, I played LSD: FIH, and lemme tell you…this game kinda slaps.
Even if the “fih aura-farming” aesthetic of LSD: FIH isn’t quite up your alley, the gameplay is weirdly satisfying. It’s essentially a quickscope simulator (a game that tries to capture the spirit of Call of Duty sniper kill montages from 2010), but with a combo mechanic that feels genuinely punchy. Honestly, this article would have been ready like 30 minutes earlier, but I got a little too invested in playing it.
However, according to the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam’s voting page, voting doesn’t close for over two more months, so we’ll have to wait a while to see if LSD: FIH emerges victorious. If it does, I can’t imagine Tim will advertise said victory particularly loudly, so I’ve set a reminder for January 29, 2027, just in case.
