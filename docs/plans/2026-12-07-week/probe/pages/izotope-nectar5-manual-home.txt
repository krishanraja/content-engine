Welcome to Nectar 5
New in this version
Moments: select lyrics and apply throws, reverse reverb swells, doubling, harmonies, pitch and formant shifts, and distortion to just those words. An industry first, in DAWs that support ARA.
Serial Compressor: stack up to 3 compressors, limiters, or clippers, set thresholds on the oscilloscope, and let Learn set the makeup gain.
Vocal Stabilizer: an adaptive EQ built for vocals, with style and register targets, Shape and Cut modes, and an Amount control for each band.
Overview page: the key controls of Nectar’s core modules in one place, with a one-click jump to each full module.
Custom Flow: guide Vocal Assistant with your style, register, key, modules, Intensity, Space, and an acapella reference.
Micro Pitch Shift: a new Dimension mode that spreads and subtly detunes the vocal for width and weight.
Wet Level mode, ducking, and Output EQ: send-style control and a 3-band Output EQ in Reverb, Delay, and Dimension, with ducking in Reverb and Delay.
Nectar 5 Elements: your whole vocal chain on one page, set up by Vocal Assistant.
A new look: a redesigned interface with a collapsible Pitch and Auto-Level side panel.
