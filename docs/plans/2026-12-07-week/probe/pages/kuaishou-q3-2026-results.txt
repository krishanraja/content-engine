Third Quarter 2026 Financial Review
Revenue from our online marketing services
decreased by 11.1% to RMB17.9 billion for the third quarter of 2026, from RMB20.1 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily attributable to the decline in e-commerce marketing spending due to the dynamic consumer environment and intensifying competition across the e-commerce sector.
Revenue from our live streaming business
decreased by 19.0% to RMB7.8 billion for the third quarter of 2026, from RMB9.6 billion for the same period of 2025, as a result of our continuous efforts to develop a rich and healthy live streaming ecosystem.
Revenue from our other services
increased by 1.5% to RMB6.0 billion for the third quarter of 2026, from RMB5.9 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily attributable to the increase in games and Kling AI revenue, partially offset by the decrease in e-commerce revenue.

Business Review
In the third quarter of 2026, the average DAUs on the Kuaishou App reached 417.3 million, representing a slight year-over-year increase. Due to market competition, the average daily time spent per DAU on the Kuaishou App in the same quarter declined year-over-year. In the third quarter of 2026, total revenues were RMB31.6 billion, adjusted net profit was RMB790 million and adjusted EBITDA was RMB3.9 billion, each of which experienced a year-over-year decrease.
Dynamic consumer environment and intensifying external competition exerted short-term pressure on our business during the period. Despite increased investment in our AI strategy this year, particularly in
Kling AI (

, we maintained prudent financial discipline to improve operational quality and efficiency. We also continued to honor our commitment to shareholder returns. In the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased a total of over 39 million shares for an aggregate consideration of over HK$1.4 billion. In the first three quarters of 2026, total shareholder returns, including dividends
(1)
and share repurchases, amounted to over HK$5.8 billion, reflecting Kuaishou's long-term confidence in its business and its endeavor to return value to the Shareholders.
AI business
In the third quarter of 2026, Kling AI continued to pursue its vision of "empowering everyone to craft captivating stories with AI". Through technological breakthroughs in its next-generation video generation models, enhanced professional filmmaking capabilities and the continued expansion of its global creator ecosystem, Kling AI further strengthened its global leadership among multimodal large video generation models. These advancements supported the evolution of AI video generation towards higher-quality outputs, greater controllability, and increasingly sophisticated professional creative applications.
At the model and product level, Kling AI announced its next-generation video generation model, Kling 4.0, in September 2026 and began rolling out Kling 4.0 Flash to a limited set of users. The new model delivers comprehensive upgrades in audiovisual performance, creative control and narrative completeness, with enhanced stability in complex dynamic motion and camera movements. It supports native video generation of up to 30 seconds, multi-keyframe control and multimodal reference inputs, thereby significantly enhancing the expressiveness and controllability of professional content creation. Meanwhile, Kling AI continued to refine its integrated creative platform, upgrading features including high-precision video editing, timeline-based creation and agent-assisted creation to further improve the efficiency of professional content creation.
In professional filmmaking and global creative ecosystem development, Kling AI continued to strengthen its partnerships with professional film and television production teams and creators worldwide, extending the use of AI video generation into professional film and television production pipelines. In the third quarter of 2026, Kling AI participated in the inaugural edition of TIFF: The Market, a marketplace officially launched by the Toronto International Film Festival, showcasing practical applications of AI in feature-length animation, film and television previsualization and hybrid production. One such example is the international animated feature MINIBOTS, which integrated Kling AI into its professional production workflow, exploring the application of generative AI in cinema-grade 4K animation production. In July 2026, Kling AI also hosted the Kling AI NEXTGEN Awards Ceremony in Seoul, South Korea. The competition attracted more than 10,000 submissions, further expanding Kling AI's presence and influence within the global professional filmmaking community.
In the third quarter of 2026, beyond Kling AI, we continued to deepen AI applications across our content ecosystem, commercial ecosystem and organizational infrastructure. We advanced the application of GoalRank, our next-generation, large-scale generative re-ranking architecture, in short-video recommendation scenarios.
GoalRank
is designed to optimize the overall user viewing experience across multiple short videos. Through model engineering optimizations, it increases user time spent on short-video content without requiring additional inference computing resources. In the third quarter of 2026, AI adoption across online marketing services and e-commerce business shifted from single-model efficiency gains to large-scale, system-level deployment. Generative recommendation, AIGC, smart bidding, and recommendation-model agents now work together, reshaping commercial content production, the marketing materials replacement, and the way of algorithms iterations. In the third quarter of 2026, we rolled out AI-native ways of working across the Group organization and launched MyFlicker 2.0. MyFlicker 2.0 consolidated AI assets such as team skills and knowledge, building team-level agents. At the end of September 2026, we established an enterprise AI productivity organization,

The platform empowers internal and external clients with more efficient end-to-end services for model customization, deployment and inference. These capabilities have supported rapid revenue growth of Kuaishou Vanchin. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue from Kuaishou Vanchin increased by nearly 90.0% quarter-over-quarter.
User and content ecosystem
In the third quarter of 2026, average DAUs on the Kuaishou App reached 417.3 million, and average MAUs reached 818.5 million, representing year-over-year growth of 0.3% and 12.0%, respectively. We continued to cultivate our content community with distinctive Kuaishou characteristics, while reinforcing the community core that is inclusive, trustworthy, and diverse. We iterated on our user retention mechanisms and leveraged refined operations to strengthen users' content consumption mindset, thereby enhancing user retention and stickiness. We also optimized the private messaging experience, introduced more social interaction features, and strengthened users' mutual connections. The average daily number of users with mutual followers engaging in private messaging increased by more than 7.0% year-over-year in the third quarter of 2026. Among them, the average daily number of users interacting through the social pet-raising feature

In the third quarter of 2026, we continued to enrich our high-quality content, with a focus on strengthening Kuaishou's distinctive community. We provided users with differentiated and heartwarming content. We once again partnered with Beijing Radio and Television Station (北京衛視) to present the 2026

Online marketing services
In the third quarter of 2026, revenue from online marketing services was RMB17.9 billion, representing a decrease of 11.1% year-over-year. Supported by year-over-year growth in marketing budgets in the content consumption sector, particularly in the AI-generated short plays vertical, our non-e-commerce marketing services revenue outperformed our overall revenue from online marketing services. Amid intensifying competition across the e-commerce sector, our e-commerce marketing services focused on improving merchant ROI by enhancing the synergy between our e-commerce and online marketing businesses, and through more granular merchant segmentation.
In the third quarter of 2026, within our non-e-commerce marketing services business, the content consumption sector performed strongly, and the lifestyle services sector remained solid. In the content consumption sector, we continued to expand the supply of AI-generated and live-action short plays with distinctive Kuaishou characteristics, driving sustained growth in high-quality short-play content. As of September 2026, the average daily number of active short plays increased by more than 350.0% compared with January 2026, further demonstrating the value of short plays within our content ecosystem. Underpinned by an abundant supply of short plays and rising user demand for such content, total spending on online marketing services in the content consumption sector increased by more than 100.0% year-over-year in the third quarter of 2026. In the lifestyle services sector, we continued to advance industry-specific refined operations, thereby capturing incremental growth opportunities in sub-verticals such as comprehensive services and local services. Meanwhile, across sectors including home renovation, travel and automotive, we cultivated high-quality vertical content to help our clients more effectively guide users from initial interest generation to marketing conversion, fostering a healthier and more sustainable marketing ecosystem.
In the third quarter of 2026, for e-commerce marketing services, we continued to enhance synergies and efficiencies between our e-commerce and online marketing businesses. Amid dynamic consumer environment and intensifying competition across the e-commerce sector, we realigned our organization around merchant segmentation, allocating more resources to merchant services and strengthening customer support. These initiatives began to yield initial results during the third quarter of 2026. Meanwhile, our operational initiatives helped boost merchant engagement, driving quarter-over-quarter improvements in merchants' marketing spend in the lifestyle consumption and daily necessities categories. During the third quarter of 2026, we continuously optimize our business ecosystem. We directed more traffic towards high-quality content and introduced a post-purchase traffic coordination strategy to reduce repetitive exposure to similar products after transactions. These measures improved marketing clients' ROI while safeguarding user experiences. In the third quarter of 2026, brand merchants' marketing spend continued to increase, supported by initiatives including supports for new branded stores in their early growth stage, targeted user management and long-term signal modeling for product recommendations. At the product level of e-commerce marketing services, our
Net Transaction ROI (

product continued to evolve. Through improvements to bidding adjustments, the introduction of mid-campaign switching capabilities, client incentive programs and refinements to the model's order cancellation and return features, the product's penetration rate in e-commerce marketing spending increased from 28.0% in the second quarter of 2026 to 34.7% in the third quarter of 2026. These enhancements further improved the certainty of transaction outcomes that e-commerce merchants derive from marketing placements.
In the third quarter of 2026, we continued to enhance the applications of AI across our online marketing services. Our
Universal Auto X (UAX,
全自動投放

placement solutions evolved from a single-point strategy into a multi-agent collaborative framework. The upgraded framework automatically diagnoses marketing placement performance issues within accounts and coordinates multiple placement capabilities to improve clients' marketing placement efficiency. We also enhanced our AIGC marketing materials solutions by incorporating user interest signals. When the existing library of marketing materials does not sufficiently cover user interests, our product will generate additional marketing materials better tailored to target audiences, thereby further improving the matching efficiency between marketing materials and user needs.
E-commerce
In the third quarter of 2026, our e-commerce business faced pressure from dynamic consumer environment and intensifying competition across the e-commerce sector, coupled with rising operating costs for merchants. We optimized our organizational structure around more granular merchant segmentation with more targeted merchant policies and resource allocation to support merchants in building sustainable businesses on our platform. During the third quarter of 2026, on the demand side, we focused on building a high-quality paying user base by improving how we identify and serve such users. We increased their engagement by broadening the range of product offerings and expanding the consumption scenarios. We also increased traffic allocation and subsidies for branded products to capture and serve high-value users effectively.
In the third quarter of 2026, on the supply side, we remained committed to onboarding new merchants, strengthening our systematic capabilities in brand merchant acquisition, and expanding merchant support initiatives to scale their businesses and improve merchant retention. In the third quarter of 2026, both the new merchant retention rate and the scaled growth rate of existing merchants improved steadily quarter-over-quarter. In the third quarter of 2026, for brand merchants, we continued to optimize the coordination between traffic strategies and supply structure to enhance brand operations and growth. On the brand supply front, brand matrix accounts expanded rapidly, with the number of newly opened brand stores increasing by more than 50.0% year-over-year. On the traffic front, we leveraged broader interest-based recommendations, targeted reach to high-value users and optimized omni-domain recommendation pathways to help brands engage high-value users more effectively on our platform. Together, improvements in brand supply and traffic strategies drove greater exposure for branded products and more effective conversion of high-value users. This, in turn, drove growth in both MAC and GMV among high-value users for brand merchants. Meanwhile, in the fourth quarter of last year, we launched T2000 Initiative, a dedicated program for top-tier brands, under which we implemented more granular, tiered operations tailored to the varying operational capabilities of participating brands. We also launched brand-week campaigns to boost in brands' self-operated live streaming activity, building stronger and more consistent brand awareness among users.
In the third quarter of 2026, in e-commerce content development and KOL operations, we focused on follower growth and live streaming frequency among human KOLs, continuously optimizing the KOL ecosystem and prioritizing the quality of content supply. During the third quarter of 2026, we optimized our traffic strategies and implemented targeted governance initiatives. Under our traffic distribution mechanism, we directed more traffic towards high-quality creators, thereby fostering healthier content fundamentals for e-commerce. We also continued to deepen our partnerships with top-tier KOLs. Through initiatives such as VIP services for top-tier KOLs and collaborative content development for major live streaming events, we maintained a stable supply of livestreams from our core KOLs. We also increased incentives for core KOLs, thereby driving a notable quarter-over-quarter increase in their streaming frequency in the third quarter of 2026. In addition, we continued to collaborate with external service providers to diversify the pool of e-commerce content creators and enrich the supply of our e-commerce content.
We continued to leverage AI tools across the e-commerce operational workflow to help merchants improve operational efficiency. During the third quarter of 2026, we launched a fully managed AI operations solution tailored to small and medium-sized e-commerce merchants. By integrating our end-to-end AI tools, the solution enables one-stop content creation and distribution, with intelligent support adapted to different consumption scenarios to help small and medium-sized e-commerce merchants improve their operational performance. The solution delivered initial business gains during the third quarter of 2026.
Live streaming
In the third quarter of 2026, live streaming revenue was RMB7.8 billion, representing a decrease of 19.0% year-over-year. We focused on strengthening refined operations on the supply side, fostering deeper relationships between streamers and users, and leveraging AI to drive innovation across content and user interaction, while remaining firmly committed to the long-term and healthy development of the live streaming ecosystem. On the supply side, we continued to advance the
Confluence Initiative (
百川計划
)
. Through acquisition incentives and operational support, we facilitated the recruitment and nurturing of talent agencies, reinforcing the fundamentals of our content supply. For independent streamers, we refined operations to enhance streamer services, and boost the activity of top-tier independent streamers. In terms of content ecosystem, we launched Kuaishou's first music-themed social reality show featuring streamers,
Singing in the Mountains (
向山而唱
)
. The show brought together top-tier Kuaishou-native streamers and celebrities for musical performances in outdoor mountain settings, exploring a new variety show format centered on streamers. By the finale, the live streams garnered 270 million views.
In the third quarter of 2026, we advanced the cultivation of streamer-user relationships by taking a coordinated approach across content supply, product and traffic. We encouraged streamers to foster long-term user engagement. Through innovative features such as
Fan Farm (
粉絲農場
)
, we drove conversion to paying users. We also refined our recommendation strategies to strengthen long-term matching between streamers and users, supporting the sustainable development of our live streaming ecosystem. On the product and technology front, we upgraded
AI Universe (AI
萬象
)
and launched AI-generated interactive features, bringing lightweight, variety-show-style interactive games into live streaming scenarios. These features offered users enjoyable and engaging interactive experiences, effectively expanding the range of interactions between viewers and streamers.
Overseas
In the third quarter of 2026, we continued to deepen our high-value user operations and strengthen localized ecosystems, balancing growth quality and profitability across our overseas business to lay a solid foundation for sustainable growth. In terms of traffic growth and the content ecosystem, we further improved user acquisition efficiency and our user growth structure. Centered on the needs of core users, we enriched locally relevant content, deepened content consumption and community engagement, and reinforced our high-value user base. For overseas online marketing services, we strengthened our AI-driven marketing product capabilities, exploring AI-assisted creation and localization of marketing materials. Through refined traffic operations and optimized placement strategies, we improved marketing placement efficiency and performance consistency. We remained focused on unlocking the monetization potential of short plays and other creative content formats, fostering synergies between our content ecosystem and monetization capabilities. Regarding overseas e-commerce, we maintained solid year-over- year growth in GMV and order volume in the third quarter of 2026. We leveraged our short video KOL ecosystem to drive transaction growth, optimizing merchant and product mix to meet local consumer demand better. High-quality supply also supported growth in average order value. While steadily scaling our overseas e-commerce business, we maintained operating efficiency and profitability.
Business Outlook
Looking ahead to the fourth quarter of 2026, the near-term operating environment remains challenging, with relatively conservative consumers' and marketers' spending sentiments and intense competition. In addition, we have experienced softness in our traffic trajectory, which will put further pressure on our monetization capabilities. Our ongoing strategic investments in Kling AI and our broader AI strategy will continue to carry cost implications and weigh on profitability in the near term. Therefore, our revenue and profitability may continue to be under pressure in the fourth quarter of 2026.
To cope with the challenges above, we are committed to a more disciplined approach to cost control and efficiency improvement in our core businesses. With confidence in our business model and long-term prospects, we will continue to return value to the Shareholders with our capital return initiatives in place. We believe that our financial discipline and strengthened AI capabilities will position us well to capture long-term growth opportunities and deliver sustainable values to the Shareholders.
