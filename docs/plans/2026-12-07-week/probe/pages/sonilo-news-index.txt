What's newSubscribe via RSS
Every update to Sonilo, all in one place. New models, features, workflows, and improvements.

Oct 15, 2026
Turn your lyrics into a song
Text to Music now sings. Write your lyrics, describe the genre, voice and language in Style, and get back a finished song. No lyrics yet? Press Write lyrics and Sonilo writes them for you.

Oct 9, 2026
Turn your photos into a video with music
Choose up to 100 photos in Photo to Music and Sonilo makes them into a video with music written for them. Pick an occasion or describe the music you want.

Oct 6, 2026
Organize your library into folders
Create folders in your Library and file music, sound effects, dubs and exported videos into them. Sounds you save from Discover can go in a folder too.

Oct 6, 2026
Put a Text to Music track on your video
Every finished Text to Music variation now has an "Add to video" button. The track goes onto the music lane of a new video project, ready for the video you add.

Oct 6, 2026
Restore deleted tracks from Trash
What you delete from your Library now goes to Trash first. Restore it within 30 days, or delete it for good.

Oct 6, 2026
Get listening notes on your music
Press Analyze under a Text to Music result and Sonilo listens to both variations. It writes how they differ, what plays, the rhythm and the mood, section by section, as notes you can copy.

Oct 5, 2026
Fade music and sound effects in and out
Press Fade in/out on a music or sound effects lane in the Studio, then drag the handles to set how the sound starts and ends. The fades carry into your audio downloads and MP4 export.

Oct 4, 2026
Join several videos into one
Pick up to 10 clips on Video to Music or Video to Sound Effects, drag them into order, and Sonilo joins them into one video before it adds sound.

Oct 3, 2026
Add music or sound effects to up to 10 videos at once
Choose Batch create, add up to 10 videos, and each one gets its own music or sound effects. Analyze every video in one click, and change one video's settings or give them to all.

Oct 3, 2026
Remove background noise from video and audio
Upload a video or an audio file and Sonilo takes out traffic, wind, hum and chatter while the voice stays. Set Strength to keep some of the room, then download an MP4 or MP3.

Oct 2, 2026
Add sound to a video by chatting with an agent
Upload a video and say what you want. The agent plans the music, sound effects and dubbing, shows you the cost, and builds everything on a timeline once you approve.

Sep 30, 2026
Add music only where your video needs it
Choose Sections and Sonilo analyzes your video to pick where music and sound effects belong. Adjust the sections on the timeline, then download each one or the full track.

Sep 29, 2026
Translate your video into other languages
Upload a video and get it back dubbed in up to 23 languages, in the original speaker's voice. Proofread the translation before it is dubbed, add lip sync, and export subtitles.

Sep 26, 2026Premium and up
Upload videos up to 30 minutes
Premium uploads now go up to 30 minutes, twice the previous limit. For videos longer than 8 minutes, the music is composed in sections that follow the picture.

Sep 4, 2026APIPro and up
Process videos up to 8 minutes with the API
Video-to-sound-effects now supports videos up to 8 minutes long via the Sonilo API. Send longer clips and full edits without splitting them first.

Aug 11, 2026Pro and up
Split any music track into stems
Separate any track into drums, bass, and other stems. Preview each stem individually and download only what you need.

Aug 4, 2026
Prompt writing helper
Analyze your video to automatically generate a sound direction based on what's happening on screen. Use it as-is, edit it, or start generating right away.

Jul 31, 2026
Sonilo is now available in 10 languages
Use the full Sonilo interface in 10 languages, including Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and German.
