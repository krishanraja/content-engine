 Pro vocal plugins for a polished sound
iZotope Nectar 5 is an intelligent suite of vocal plugins that takes performances from raw to release-ready. Get smooth dynamics, balanced tone, and professional creative effects in any workflow. Go all-in-one, or one-by-one with 14 component plugins to use in your DAW. Your vocal. Your chain. Your call.
 Introducing Nectar 5
Lead with speed
Get to a strong vocal fast with a retuned Vocal Assistant, the new Custom Flow, and the Overview page – every control within reach.
Pro know-how
Professional dynamics and tone control, minus the guesswork, from the new Serial Compressor and adaptive Vocal Stabilizer.
Total vocal mixing
A complete vocal production hub and 14 purpose-built modules, all able to load as solo plugins in your DAW.
Creative effects, lyric by lyric
Moments
Want a reverb throw on the last word of the chorus? The industry-first Moments transcribes your vocal into lyrics for word-by-word effects. Highlight words like any other text, then apply throws, swells, doubling, harmonies, pitch and formant shifts, or distortion – right where you want them. Runs in DAWs that support ARA, in dozens of languages.
Nectar, your way
However you want to build your chain, Nectar has the tools you need – from one click to full control.

Overview page

Full modules
Open any module to make fine-detail decisions, all inside one plugin.
Component plugins
Load modules as plugins, right next to the other favorites in your DAW.

Mix Engineers

Producers

Artists & Creators
Get to a finished-sounding vocal fast. Let Vocal Assistant set up your whole chain, then shape the essentials on the Overview page, and build out your sound from there.

Creative effects, lyric by lyric
Moments
Want a reverb throw on the last word of the chorus? The industry-first Moments transcribes your vocal into lyrics for word-by-word effects. Highlight words like any other text, then apply throws, swells, doubling, harmonies, pitch and formant shifts, or distortion – right where you want them. Runs in DAWs that support ARA, in dozens of languages.
7 effects
Exactly where you want them. All without managing multiple automation lanes.
7 effects
Exactly where you want them. All without managing automation lanes.
Doubler
A detuned double that makes any word sound bigger.
Delay
Echoes that lock to your BPM, with ping-pong that bounces side to side.
Reverb
Six spaces to send a word into, from tight rooms to vast cathedrals.
Preverb
The classic reverse reverb effect that swells into a word.
Distortion
Add grit with retro, tape, tube, or bitcrushed character.
Harmony
Stack up to four voices in key, for instant backing on any line.
Pitch
Shift up to an octave in either direction, with formant control.
Stacked compression, made simple
Serial Compressor
Get punch without the pump. Stack up to 3 compressors, limiters, or clippers. Easily dial in thresholds to level out vocals. Automatically set makeup gain with the Learn button, and try the classic FET-into-Opto chain.
Your essentials, all in one place
Overview page
The Overview page brings the key settings of Nectar's core modules into one central control hub. Shape several modules at once, hear the whole chain come together, and jump to any full module in one click for deeper edits.
Get your guide, or take the lead
Vocal Assistant
Trained on pro vocal standards and retuned for even better results, Vocal Assistant can set up your whole chain in one click. Or, if you’ve got a specific sound in mind, call the shots with the new Custom Flow. Then, dive into each module on the Overview page to fine-tune. However your chain starts, it ends your way.
One-Click
Skip the setup. Just like magic. Vocal Assistant analyses your sound and builds a tailored processing chain in seconds. Now including new modules from Nectar 5.
Custom Flow
Guide the Assistant on the style, register, key, Intensity, and Atmosphere you want – even pick which modules to use, and add an acapella reference to aim for.
Adaptive EQ, built for vocals
Vocal Stabilizer
Not sure if your vocal needs more air or a softer edge? Skip the guesswork with the Vocal Stabilizer, an adaptive EQ that guides every note toward pro-level tonal balance. Just pick a style and register, Shape or Cut, then fine-tune each band’s Amount.
Pro-style stereo widening
Micro Pitch Shift
Vocal sounding narrow and weak in the mix? Micro Pitch Shift, a new mode in the Dimension module, subtly spreads and detunes your vocal for the width and weight of modern mixes. One of the most popular widening techniques among pro mixers, now right in your chain.
Lift the vocal, lose the wash
Upgraded effects
Wet Level mode, ducking, and 3-band filters for cleaner, clearer mixes.
Wet Level mode
A new mode in Reverb, Delay, and Dimension that turns the Dry/Wet knob into a send-style control, adding the effect while keeping the full dry vocal.
FX Ducking
Easy automatic ducking for Reverb and Delay, helping make sure vocals don’t get drowned out in reverb tails and echoes.
Updated filtering
Reverb, Delay, and Dimension now include 3-band filters with adjustable high-pass and low-pass slopes to dial in each effect’s tone.

Workflow
NEW
Custom Flow
With the Vocal Assistant Custom Flow, you call the shots on style, register, key, modules, Intensity, Atmosphere, and an acapella to aim for.
NEW
Overview page
The Overview page puts the key controls of Nectar’s core modules in one central hub, with a 1-click jump to each full module for deeper edits.
IMPROVED
Vocal Assistant
Trained on pro vocal standards, Vocal Assistant sets up your whole chain in seconds – a tailored starting point to build from.
IMPROVED
Look and feel
A polished new interface that resizes to fit any screen and stays easy on the eyes for long sessions, with a collapsible Pitch and Auto-Level side panel.
Tone & dynamics
NEW
Serial Compressor
Stack up to 3 compressors, limiters, or clippers with automatic makeup gain – all the punch, none of the pump.
NEW
Vocal Stabilizer
Pick your style and register, then shape tone or cut resonances dynamically as the performance moves.
Equalizer
The Equalizer sculpts your vocal with per-node dynamic mode and harmonic tracking.
Compressor
The Compressor brings dynamic control and character in one stage.
Space & Effects
NEW
Moments ARA plugin
Moments transcribes your vocal, so you can highlight words to add reverb throws, harmonies, and more – right in your timeline via ARA.
NEW
Micro Pitch Shift
Micro Pitch Shift, a new Dimension mode, subtly spreads and detunes your vocal for the width and weight of modern mixes.
NEW
Wet Level mode
Wet Level mode in Reverb, Delay, and Dimension turns the Dry/Wet knob into a send-style control, adding space while keeping the full dry vocal.
Vocal Unmask
Vocal Unmask carves space for your vocal in the backing track, so every word cuts through.
IMPROVED
Reverb
Reverb goes from a subtle halo to a full wash, now with automatic ducking that keeps tails out of the vocal’s way, Wet Level mode, and a 3-band filter.
IMPROVED
Delay
Delay adds rhythmic echoes, now with automatic ducking, Wet Level mode, and a 3-band filter.
Dimension
Dimension adds chorus, flanger, and phaser, now with Micro Pitch Shift widening, Wet Level mode, and a 3-band filter.
Saturation
Saturation adds analog-style harmonic warmth and edge.
Pitch & Harmony
Pitch
The Pitch module corrects pitch in real time, from transparent to extreme, with creative color controls.
Melodyne essential
Melodyne essential ships with Nectar 5 for detailed, note-by-note pitch and timing edits.
Voices
Voices creates instant vocal layers and harmonies from presets, with auto-harmony styles and MIDI mode.
Backer
Backer generates background singers from your lead vocal, with 8 personas or your own acapella.
Clean-up
De-esser
The De-esser tames harsh, distracting sibilance so every line sounds smooth and polished.
Gate
The Gate removes room tone and bleed between phrases.
Breath Control
The Breath Control plugin reduces unwanted breaths without hurting audio quality.
Auto-Level
Auto-Level rides vocal level before processing, right in the side panel.

 Nectar 5 FAQ
 What's new in Nectar 5?
Nectar 5 adds Moments, an industry-first plugin that applies effects to individual words in your lyrics, plus two new modules (the Serial Compressor and Vocal Stabilizer), a new Overview page that puts your whole chain in one place, upgraded creative effects, and a rebuilt Vocal Assistant with Custom Flow. The new look is the cherry on top.
 What's the difference between Nectar 5 and Nectar 5 Elements?
Nectar 5 Elements is an all-in-one vocal plugin: Vocal Assistant, including the Custom Flow, and the Overview page, with core controls for Compressor, De-esser, Delay, Dimension, Equalizer, Pitch, and Reverb.
Nectar 5 is the complete vocal production suite: everything in Nectar 5 Elements, plus additional Overview controls, 12 full modules and 2 mini-modules, Moments, Breath Control, Melodyne essential, 400+ presets, and 14 component plugins to use in your own chains in your DAW.
 Can I upgrade from Nectar 4 or earlier?
Yes. Owners of Nectar 4 and earlier versions, including Nectar Elements, can get Nectar 5 at a loyalty price. Sign in at izotope.com to see yours.
 Can I use Nectar's modules in my own vocal chain?
Yes. Every Nectar 5 module and mini-module also loads as its own plugin. That's 14 component plugins, so you can drop the Serial Compressor, the Vocal Stabilizer, and more right between the plugins you already use. You can also run Nectar as an all-in-one vocal chain.
 Does Nectar 5 include pitch correction?
Yes. The Pitch module corrects pitch in real time, from transparent to extreme, and Melodyne essential comes with Nectar 5 for additional offline pitch correction.
 Which languages does Moments transcribe?
Moments detects the language of your vocal automatically and transcribes dozens of languages. It works best with English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese, French, Polish, Turkish, Dutch, and Indonesian. It also transcribes Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Ukrainian, Swedish, Vietnamese, Malay, Thai, and many others, though you may need to correct more words in those.
