Unreal Engine teams
You submit on itch.io.
1. Package your game:
Desktop or mobile: Windows 10 or 11, macOS, Android, or iOS. We recommend a development build for all platforms. Shipping builds are also accepted for Windows, macOS, and Android. iOS builds must be development builds.
Web (experimental): Package for Web with the Unreal Engine 5.9 MegaJam Preview using a Shipping build.
2. Name your .zip TeamName_GameName.
Desktop and mobile: keep it under 1 GB. For bigger builds, use butler or Blendo Itch Uploader.
Web: make sure index.html is at the top level of the .zip, and keep the game under 500 MB total and 200 MB per file. Mark it as playable in the browser on itch.io.
3. Upload it to your own itch.io account, then submit to this jam.
Include:
Team name, plus each member’s name and the email address linked to their Epic Games account
Game name
Gameplay video link (30–60 seconds)
Dev log link (optional, but required for the Glass Box modifier)
Tools you used (optional, but needed for partner prizes such as Hyper3D’s cash pool)
NVIDIA RTX Award (optional): check the NVIDIA features you used and describe how you used them
OpenAI Awards (optional): which OpenAI models or products you used, and how
UEFN teams
You submit on itch.io. with a private version code. That's a fixed snapshot of your island that can't be changed after you create it. Follow the Fortnite Developer Rules.
1) Your private version code must be the final, playable build you want judged, and you must create it before the deadline.
2) Live island codes and playtest codes aren't accepted, because they can be changed after the jam ends.
Include:
Team name, plus each member’s name and the email address linked to their Epic Games account
Island name and private version code
Gameplay video link (30–60 seconds)
Dev log link (optional, but required for Glass Box modifier)
Tools you used (optional, but needed for partner prizes such as Hyper3D’s cash pool).

Tips
Set up your agents before November 12. Getting MCP working on day one costs build time.
Scope small, then polish. A finished, fun game beats an ambitious broken one.
Package or publish early. Do a test build on day two so the last day isn’t a surprise.
Keep notes as you go. It makes your dev log easier to write for the Glass Box modifier category.
Make your video count. Show gameplay in the first five seconds.
Give it a shot anyway. Four days with agents on your side is a great way to try something you’d never have time for otherwise. Even if you don’t finish, you’ll learn a lot.
Find teammates or ask questions on the Unreal Engine Forums and UEFN Discord.
Do I have to use AI?
No.
Can I start before November 12?
You can set up your tools, agents, and MCP ahead of time, but don't start building your game until November 12. Declare any prompts, scripts, or assets you made before the jam.
Can my team enter with both Unreal Engine and UEFN?
No. Each team picks one and follows that submission process.
Do I need to come to San Francisco?
No. Online and in-person entries are judged the same way.
Can I keep working on my game after the jam?
Yes. Keep the jam version as your submitted build. Standout Unreal Engine and UEFN entries can also receive Development Continuation Grants of up to $50,000 to keep developing their game.
Who owns my game?
You do. See the Official Rules.
Can I include attribution required by a tool or asset license?
Yes. If a tool, plugin, SDK or asset you use requires attribution, you can include it in the way the rights owner describes.
