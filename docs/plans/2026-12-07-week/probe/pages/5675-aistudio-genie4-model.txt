# Genie 4
Genie 4 (`genie-4`) is a real-time interactive world generation model on
the [Interactions API](/docs/interactions-overview). Seed a world from a text
prompt, a 16:9 reference image, or both, then explore and steer the live 24 fps
video and 48 kHz stereo audio stream in real time using keyboard movement
controls (`WASD` and arrow keys) and mid-session text events.
For offline, fixed-duration clip generation, multi-turn video editing, and
upsampling up to 4K, use
[Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash](/docs/models/gemini-omni-flash) instead.

{% icon name="token_auto" /%}Stream & session limits
{% /section %}
*     **Output video** 1280x720 (720p, 16:9, 24 fps), 240 video output tokens per frame (5,760 tokens/sec)
**Max session duration** Up to 120 seconds per session (visual coherence and object permanence are strongest during the first 45-90 seconds)
**Output audio** 48 kHz stereo (16-bit PCM over WebSocket; Opus over WebRTC), bundled into video output tokens
**Reference image** Single 16:9 image in `world_setup` (keep compressed JPEG under 48 KB on WebRTC so the base64 JSON event stays within the 64 KB SCTP data channel limit)
**Serving regions** United States, Western Europe, and Japan (< 150 ms RTT in North America, Western/Central Europe, and East Asia; higher latency outside these regions delays steering and can reduce frame rate)

{% sup %}*{% /sup %} Billing is based on emitted video output tokens at `240`
tokens per frame (`5,760` tokens per second at `24 fps`), with `48 kHz` stereo
audio bundled into the video token rate. Under Standard pricing (`$17.50` per 1M
video output tokens), this equates to an effective rate of approximately `$0.10`
per second (`~$6.05` per minute). Always call `complete()` to end an active
session and stop billing. Genie 4 does not support the Free tier, Batch API,
Flex inference, or Priority inference.
