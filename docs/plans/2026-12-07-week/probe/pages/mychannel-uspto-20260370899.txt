(57) ABSTRACT

According to an aspect, a method may include obtaining, by
a server computer, data related to preferences of a user and
obtaining, by the server computer and from a computing
device, contextual information related to the user. The
method may include obtaining demographic data related to
the user, and generating, by the server computer, a prompt
for use by a large language model that is based on the data
related to preferences of the user, the contextual information
related to the user, and the demographic data related to the
user. The method may include sending the prompt to the
large language model, obtaining, by the server computer and
from a customized content repository, a customized media
content item for the user generated by the large language
model based on the prompt, and sending the customized
media content item to the computing device for playing on
the computing device.

BACKGROUND

[0001] A television (TV) application may present various
types of media content of interest to a user. The media
content may have different formats such as streaming video
and audio. The types of media content may include, but are
not limited to, movies, television shows, sporting events,
news items, short form videos, and music. In addition, or in
the alternative, a variety of media content providers may
deliver various types of media content for viewing by the
user. The TV application may deliver a customized viewing
experience to a user that spans the diverse types of media
content provided by the variety of media content providers.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

[0033] A television (TV) application may provide a user
with recommended media content for viewing by the user.
The TV application may interface with a media content
recommendation system that may offer suggestions for
media content that a user may be interested in viewing based
on, for example, the past viewing habits of the user. The
media content recommendation system may, however, fail to
provide an engaging viewing experience to the user that
could be provided by customizing the recommended media
content for the user. In addition, or in the alternative, the
media content recommendation system, when offering sug-
gestions for media content that a user may be interested in
viewing, may not fully consider that the user may watch
diverse media content across various genres. In addition, or
in the alternative, the media content recommendation sys-
tem, when offering suggestions for media content that a user
may be interested in viewing, may not fully consider that the
user may have fluctuating interests during a period of time
(e.g., a year). In general, a challenge for a media content
recommendation system may be how to synthesize the
diverse preferences of the user into a single, cohesive, and
customized entertainment offering while providing some
variety to the recommended media content.

[0034] Atechnical problem may be the implementation of
a media content recommendation system that provides cus-
tomized entertainment offerings to a user as a custom
channel in a TV application. In addition, or in the alternative,
a technical problem for the media content recommendation
system may be how to determine and then synthesize the
diverse preferences of the user into customized entertain-
ment offerings on the custom channel.

[0035] A technical solution may include a media content
recommendation system utilizing generative artificial intel-
ligence (AI) to create the custom channel of video experi-
ences tailored to the user. In some implementations, the
media content recommendation system may analyze a view-
ing history of a user while interacting with a TV application.
The media content recommendation system may determine
one or more preferences of the user for media content for

US 2026/0370899 Al

particular genres. The media content recommendation sys-
tem may determine patterns for media content watched by
the user, such as for example, the user may watch adult
movies on Saturday nights, and kid movies on Friday nights.

[0036] A technical solution may include the media content
recommendation system enhancing a viewing experience of
the user by generating customized media content. The
customized media content may be based on the previous
viewing behavior of the user. The media content recommen-
dation system may generate multiple customized media
content items (e.g., videos, short form videos, movies, etc.)
featuring the user as the protagonist. For example, a gener-
ated customized media content item may focus on different
combinations of the genres related to previous media content
watched by the user. In addition, or in the alternative, the
customized media content item may include an avatar of the
user and/or the voice of the user when including the user as
the protagonist or lead character in the customized media
content item.

[0037] A technical effect may include the media content
recommendation system leveraging the use of generative Al
to create a channel of video experiences for including in a
TV application that is customized and tailored to a user
based on the viewing history of the user over a period of time
when interacting with the TV application.
