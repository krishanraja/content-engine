Gameplay instructions
Windows 10 or 11. Unzip and run IlyaArbabi_ThisGameIsAISlop\Megajam.exe.
W A S D: walk. Shift: run. Space: jump (not everywhere). E: use; hold E on a floating commentary bubble for three seconds to hear it. 1, 2, 3: hold a relic once you have it; Q puts it away. Left mouse button: use the relic in your hand. Esc: pause and settings.
About 35 minutes, one sitting. Walk the path to the castle and do what the director asks; the current objective is on the plate at the top right. There are six cuts of the same walk; each ends by itself. A graphics card with 8 GB is comfortable; quality can be lowered in the settings (DLSS is used on RTX cards).
