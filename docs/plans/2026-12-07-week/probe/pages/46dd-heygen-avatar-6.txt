Motion prompt control
Gestures on cue.
Write the action next to the words. Avatar 6 performs it right on cue, in the person's own style.

“Hello”
thumbs up

“the counter on the left”
point to the side

“always happy to chat”
hand on heart

“some water”
wave
Prompt Friendly pharmacist. On ‘Hello', give a thumbs up. On ‘the counter on the left', point to the side with your index finger. On ‘always happy to chat', place your right hand on your heart. On ‘Take a seat', open both arms wide. On ‘some water', wave your right hand.

“Welcome”
arms open, palms up

“grandparents”
thumbs up

“front desk”
wave

“Thanks for trusting us”
hand on heart
Prompt Warm clinic host. On ‘Welcome', open both arms wide, palms up. On ‘grandparents', give a thumbs up. On ‘front desk', wave your right hand. On ‘Thanks for trusting us', place your right hand on your heart.

“not the melody”
wag index finger

“hear it”
cup an ear

“no, slower”
palm to the camera

“the taste, the choices and the feeling”
count to three
Prompt Playful storyteller. On ‘not the melody', wag your index finger. On ‘hear it', cup a hand behind your ear. On ‘no, slower', raise a flat palm to the camera. On ‘rain on a window', flutter your fingers downward. On ‘the taste, the choices and the feeling', count to three on your fingers.

Consent

Before an avatar created from a person's footage can generate any video, the person must record a short on-camera consent statement that includes a one-time code generated for that session, so footage recorded in advance or taken from elsewhere cannot satisfy the check; HeyGen then verifies that the person in the consent recording matches the person in the training footage, and the avatar cannot render until this check passes. All people shown in this report are HeyGen employees or contracted actors who consented to the use of their likeness in this report. The AI avatars in Figure 6 are synthetic characters, and the real-person avatars there are HeyGen public stock avatars.
