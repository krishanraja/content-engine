 iQIYI Q3 Earnings Call Highlights 
 Written by MarketBeat November 16, 2026 Add As Preferred Source Share Share Share This Article Link copied to clipboard. Close 
 Image from MarketBeat Media, LLC. 
 Key Points 
 iQIYI returned to non-GAAP operating profit in Q3, reporting CNY 25 million versus a CNY 30.3 million loss in Q2. Revenue fell 4% sequentially to CNY 6 billion, while content costs declined 8% to CNY 3.5 billion. 
 The company is expanding revenue-sharing and AI-generated content to broaden supply and lower production costs. Revenue-sharing titles launched rose more than 200% sequentially, and management said AI cost savings could be structural, though it gave no earnings or margin forecast. 
 Membership revenue slipped 1% sequentially to CNY 4 billion, while online advertising rose 2% to CNY 1.3 billion. iQIYI also recorded a fourth consecutive quarter of positive operating cash flow and ended the quarter with CNY 4 billion in cash, restricted cash and short-term investments.

 IQM's Nasdaq Debut Could Change the Quantum Race for D-Wave i QIYI NASDAQ: IQ returned to a non-GAAP operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 as content costs fell, even as total revenue declined from the preceding quarter.
 The video platform reported revenue of CNY 6 billion, down 4% sequentially, and non-GAAP operating income of CNY 25 million, compared with a CNY 30.3 million loss in the second quarter, CFO Ying Tian said on the earnings call. Content costs fell 8% sequentially to CNY 3.5 billion.

He said content costs also declined year over year.
 Revenue-sharing content expands 
 Gong said the number of revenue-sharing titles launched across long-form categories, including dramas, films and animations, rose more than 200% from the second quarter. Revenue from those titles grew by a double-digit percentage sequentially, he said.
 2 Tech Mid-Caps Under $10 With Big Upside The company’s approach combines premium in-house productions intended to attract and retain members with content from a wider pool of creators to serve diverse viewing preferences, Gong said during the question-and-answer session. He said revenue-sharing arrangements shift some spending from upfront investment to payments tied to performance.
 “In the future, the number of original titles will decrease,” Gong said through a translator, adding that he hoped premium originals would contribute a larger percentage. He said the company expects a broader revenue-sharing portfolio to improve its content cost structure and reduce reliance on individual hits over time.
 Uploads to iQIYI increased more than 360% year over year in the third quarter, Gong said. The company launched 58 revenue-sharing short-form dramas during the quarter and expects about 190 for the full year, with AI-generated titles accounting for roughly 30%.
 According to Enlightent data cited by Gong, iQIYI ranked first in quarterly market share for short-form drama for the first time. The company defines short-form dramas as series with episodes typically lasting 15 to 30 minutes.
 AI production moves into longer formats 
 Gong said AI-generated content is now the main source of supply for micro dramas and micro animations, while iQIYI is extending its use into internet feature films and longer dramas. In September, AI-generated micro dramas represented nearly 90% of the category’s content volume and nearly 80% of its video views, he said.
 The company launched eight AI-generated internet feature films in the third quarter. Three chapters of The Ferry Man generated more than CNY 14 million in revenue-sharing income, and each recouped its production cost within its first week of release, Gong said.
 IQIYI also launched 21 AI-generated short-form dramas in the quarter and has about 20 active long-form drama projects using a combination of live action and AI, according to management. Chief Content Officer Xiaohui Wang said the company expects its AI-generated internet film supply to increase substantially in the fourth quarter and continue growing next year.
 Wang said through a translator that AI-driven cost reductions could be “structural and sustainable,” though management did not provide a numerical forecast for their effect on future earnings or margins.
 Advertising grows as membership revenue dips 
 Third-quarter membership services revenue was CNY 4 billion, down 1% sequentially, while online advertising revenue rose 2% to CNY 1.3 billion, Tian said. Content distribution revenue fell 41% to CNY 400.9 million, primarily because of lower drama-related distribution revenue. Other revenue rose 4% to CNY 359.8 million.
 Gong said brand advertising revenue declined slightly year over year, while performance advertising returned to double-digit year-over-year growth. Connected TV accounted for more than half of total viewing time on the platform and became its largest traffic channel during the quarter, he said.
 Outside mainland China, membership revenue grew nearly 30% year over year, Gong said. He added that the company was expanding bundled membership offerings with local telecommunications operators in Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia.
 Cash flow and repurchases 
 Net cash provided by operating activities was CNY 165.1 million, marking a fourth consecutive quarter of positive operating cash flow, Tian said. IQIYI ended the quarter with CNY 4 billion in cash equivalents, restricted cash and short-term investments.
 As of Sept. 30, the company had repurchased approximately 64.2 million American depositary shares for $76.4 million under a program announced in March 2026. Tian also disclosed a $636.6 million loan principal receivable from PAG at quarter-end, included in prepayments and other assets on the balance sheet. About iQIYI (NASDAQ:IQ) iQIYI, Inc. is a Chinese online entertainment company that operates a video-streaming platform. Its service offers films, television dramas, variety shows, animation and other video content, including programs produced or commissioned by the company. The company generates business through services such as paid memberships, advertising and content distribution. Its platform is available through its website and mobile and connected-device applications, and iQIYI also offers an international service to viewers in selected markets outside China.
