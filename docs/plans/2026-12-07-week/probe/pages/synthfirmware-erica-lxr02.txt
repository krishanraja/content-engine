 LXR-02 IS NOW OPEN SOURCE! 

 The LXR-02 drum machine firmware is now open source and available on the Erica Synths GitHub!

 October 23, 2026 

 News

The LXR-02 has always been a great instrument on its own, with simple yet extensive sound design capabilities, but we realized it could also be a great platform for experimentation! We live in an era of "vibe hacking", where AI-assisted tools let musicians and developers alike reshape software in ways that were hard to imagine only a few months ago. With that in mind, we have decided to open the LXR-02 firmware to the community. We are curious to see what new ideas, features and approaches emerge, and where this experiment will take the instrument.

We invite you to explore the code, experiment with it, and share your results with the community.

Please note: Erica Synths officially supports only the original LXR-02 firmware. Modified or community-developed firmware is installed at the user's own risk, and our support team will not be able to assist with issues caused by custom code. The official firmware will remain available, and you can return to it at any time. However, if the bootloader is damaged as a result of installing custom firmware, the unit will need to be repaired by Erica Synths, and the repair will be charged.

Have fun!
