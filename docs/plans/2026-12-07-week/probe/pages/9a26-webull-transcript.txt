 RISKS 
 CFO Tian stated, "Membership services revenue was RMB 4 billion, down 1% sequentially... primarily due to a lighter content slate compared to the same period last year," indicating the negative impact of content timing on subscriber revenue. 
 Gong indicated that growth in regions outside of China fluctuated across quarters, reflecting a lighter Chinese drama content slate available for the international market. 
 SUMMARY 
 iQIYI, Inc. (NASDAQ:IQ) reported a return to non-GAAP operating profitability while managing a transition toward a decentralized, AI-driven content ecosystem. Management detailed a shift from a centralized long-form video platform to a social media model integrating premium dramas with creator-uploaded works. This strategy incorporates AI to reduce production costs and cycles across animations and short-form dramas. The company maintained leadership in domestic market share for long-form dramas and children's content while achieving its first quarterly lead in the short-form drama category. 
 Management introduced the FamilyScape marketing system to utilize historical data and AI analytics for improving campaign effectiveness on connected television devices. 
 CEO Gong reported that AI-generated content now accounts for nearly 90% of micro-drama volume and 80% of total video views on the platform. 
 The company launched NadouPro, a studio-grade production platform supporting transactions in digital assets and skills across multiple languages to build an international creator ecosystem. 
 Chief Content Officer Wang stated that AI integrates creative teams and intellectual property with advanced tools to shorten production cycles and manage capital investment. 
 Management noted that the retention rate for the new monthly auto-renewal joint membership for S-diamond subscribers was 10 percentage points higher than standard plans. 
 Chinese dramas demonstrated global influence, with the title Overdo ranking first on the international platform across 34 countries during its first week of release. 
 INDUSTRY GLOSSARY 
 AIGC : AI-Generated Content, referring to media such as video, text, or animation produced using artificial intelligence technologies. 
 CTV : Connected TV, which refers to television sets that can connect to the internet and access streaming services directly or via external devices. 
 Revenue-sharing content : A business model where content creators receive a portion of the revenue generated by their content based on performance metrics rather than a fixed upfront fee. 
 S-diamond : A premium membership tier offered by the company that provides enhanced benefits and higher subscription value. 
 Enlightent : A third-party provider of market data and analytics for the online video industry in China. 
 Popularity Index Score : A proprietary metric used by iQIYI to measure the reach, engagement, and popularity of its content.
