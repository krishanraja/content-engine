Commercial Rights for Sonilo Music and Sound Effects
Generated Sonilo music and sound effects can be used commercially when your generation-time plan and intended use qualify under the Terms of Service. Free and Creator outputs are non-commercial; Pro covers uses such as ads, client videos and monetized channels, while Premium adds film, TV, broadcast and studio games. Enterprise rights follow the applicable agreement. For API output, check the API terms and any applicable agreement rather than assuming a web subscription's rights. Sound Effects Library downloads follow the license note on the library page, not your generation plan. You remain responsible for required third-party permissions.
Sonilo describes its music models as trained on professionally licensed catalogs, including Shutterstock. Training-source rights and permission to use generated output are separate. Pro, Premium, Enterprise, and eligible API users have commercial-use rights under Sonilo's Terms of Service for licensed AI-generated music for YouTube, ads, social clips, client videos, and commercial video projects. Free is personal and non-commercial only. Developer teams can pair those rights with API access, video-to-sound-effects workflows, and pay-as-you-go-style credit accounting for licensed music for video. The page gives API and enterprise reviewers a clear answer for paid media usage, enterprise procurement, a commercial-video soundtrack workflow, commercial release checks, and projects that combine video-to-music with video-to-sound-effects. Commercial-use rights do not remove every copyright, platform, client, or broadcast review, so teams should review the current Terms before publishing and keep output records with the release channel.

Commercial rights are a ladder: Pro covers general commercial scoring, indie games included, Premium adds film, TV, broadcast, and studio games, and Enterprise adds custom terms. No plan includes releasing a generated track to Spotify, Apple Music, or other streaming services.
An indie game is a commercial game, on any platform, by an individual or a team with under US$200,000 in combined revenue and funding over the last 12 months, and it is covered from Pro upwards. At or above that line it is a studio game, which starts on Premium. Apps and websites are covered from Pro upwards.
No paid plan requires attribution. A Free-plan download that you share or publish must be credited "Music by Sonilo" (or "Sound effects by Sonilo"), per Section 6.2 of the Terms.
Free output is for personal tests, hobby projects, and non-commercial previews, not publishing commercial work.
