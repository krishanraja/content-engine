Game Name
Goon Patrol - Director's Cut
Gameplay instructions
Unzip and run GoonPatrol.exe (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; DirectX 12 or 11). Keyboard and mouse or gamepad. About 15 to 20 minutes.

Walk through the Stage 4 door on the backlot to start the film: three scenes and a boss. Fight the goons, and when the Footage meter has a segment, hold CUT to stop the scene and pick an edit (Smash Cut, Slow-Mo, Recast, Reshoot). Block as a white flash lands for a Perfect Take; dodge on a red flash. Dying just starts Take 2.

Keyboard and mouse: WASD move, mouse camera, left mouse light attack, right mouse heavy attack (hold to charge) and Finisher, Alt or C dodge, Q or Ctrl block, Space jump, Shift sprint, Tab lock-on, F CUT, E interact, T taunt, Esc pause.

Gamepad: left stick move, right stick camera, X light, Y heavy and Finisher, B dodge, LB block, A jump, L3 sprint, R3 lock-on, RB CUT, D-pad up interact, D-pad down taunt, Start pause.

Also on the title screen: Development Hell (endless mode). Two bonus scenes are behind their own stage doors on the backlot.
What platform is your game built for?

Windows 11
