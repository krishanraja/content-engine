Tencent Music (TME) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Ximalaya integration and pan-IP push shape the company's broader audio strategy.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – 
Nov 17, 2026 at 4:10PM EST

+Fool.comonShare

Summarize with AI

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026

CALL PARTICIPANTS

Executive Chairman - Cussion Pang

CEO - Ross Liang

CFO - Shirley Hu

Head of IR - Millicent Pu

TAKEAWAYS

Revenue -- RMB 8.9 billion ($1.3 billion), representing a 5.6% year-over-year increase driven by the consolidation of Ximalaya and momentum in IP-related services.

Music Related Services Revenue -- RMB 7.7 billion ($1.1 billion), growing 10.2% year over year due to robust artist merchandise sales and expanded offline performance services.

Membership Services Revenue -- RMB 5.0 billion ($744 million), increasing 9.2% reflecting the first full quarter of Ximalaya integration and SVIP program adoption.

Marketing and Consumption Services Revenue -- RMB 2.7 billion ($402 million), up 12.1% driven by physical album sales for artists Cai Xukun and Zhou Shen.

Social Entertainment Services Revenue -- RMB 1.3 billion ($188 million), representing a 15.7% decline from RMB 1.5 billion in the prior-year period.

Non-IFRS Net Profit -- RMB 2.5 billion ($365 million), compared to RMB 2.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA -- RMB 3.0 billion ($450 million), growing 2.7% year over year.

Non-IFRS Diluted EPS per ADS -- $0.23 (RMB 1.55), compared to $0.23 (RMB 1.54) in the same period last year.

Gross Margin -- 42.8%, down from 43.5% last year due to a revenue mix shift toward artist merchandise and offline services.

Operating Expenses -- RMB 1.5 billion ($229 million), rising 16.9% primarily due to the consolidation of Ximalaya and intangible asset amortization.

Cash and Investments -- RMB 51.5 billion ($7.7 billion), an increase from RMB 44.2 billion as of June 30, 2026.

Share Repurchases -- $300 million, consisting of 34.7 million ADSs repurchased at an average price of $8.60 per ADS during the quarter.

Debt Issuance -- $1.0 billion, in senior unsecured notes issued in September 2026 with tranches maturing in 2031 and 2036.

Concert Attendance -- 120,000 attendees, reached across four stadium concerts for the rapper GAI during the quarter.

AI Content Volume -- Negligible, with management noting a declining share of user listening time for AI-generated tracks.

SVIP Membership -- Robust growth, supported by premium sound quality, exclusive fan privileges, and long-form audio content.

Ximalaya Content -- 10,000 motion comics, added to the platform to capture demand for short-form audio-video entertainment.

Merchandise Sales -- Increasing momentum, with commemorative products for Jackson Yee's tour topping the 2026 bestseller charts.

Early Content Access -- 30 days, represents the early release window secured for K-pop tracks including JISOO's latest single.

Weixin Video Integration -- Targeted operations, which drove higher average daily video views and improved traffic diversion to core music apps.

RISKS

Hu stated, "In a challenging macro environment and a competitive market, our traditional advertising business is experiencing some headwinds," reflecting budget cuts by advertisers and intense traffic competition.

Hu warned that the addition of Ximalaya led to an increase in operating expenses to 17.1% of total revenue, up from 15.5% in the same period of 2025.

SUMMARY

Tencent Music (TME +6.87%) reported that its strategic integration of Ximalaya and the expansion of its pan-IP ecosystem drove steady revenue growth during the third quarter. Management stated that the company is evolving into a comprehensive music and audio platform by connecting streaming services with live entertainment, artist promotion, and merchandise. The company indicated that its core music business remains resilient despite a competitive landscape, with improved user retention and listening time per user. Management reported that it is focused on diversifying monetization through premium membership tiers and expanding its presence in international markets through global artist tours.

Cussion Pang stated, "User response to our Pan-IP experiences has been strong, and this momentum has continued to track ahead of our expectations," regarding the growth of non-streaming revenue streams.

The company was the first to optimize its applications for the iPhone Duo and has upgraded its HarmonyOS experience to support user growth across Huawei wearables.

Management indicated that Ximalaya's long-form audio content is highly penetrated into the QQ Music and Kugou Music user bases, acting as a key growth driver for membership.

The company has reached strategic agreements with top-tier K-pop labels for offline performances, supporting events for artists such as Song Yuqi and BIGBANG.

Ross Liang noted that the company is creating a seamless path from music discovery on Weixin to full-track listening on its primary applications to improve user acquisition.

Cussion Pang stated, "By using eco-friendly materials, resources efficient packaging and recycled and reused production materials, we aim to reduce our environmental impact," while expanding physical merchandise offerings.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

ADS: American Depositary Share, which represents the company's equity trading on U.S. exchanges.

ARPPU: Average Revenue Per Paying User, a metric used to track the monetization efficiency of subscribers.

IFRS: International Financial Reporting Standards, the primary accounting framework used for the company's financial reporting.

Mandopop: Mandarin-language popular music, a core content category for the company.

SVIP: Super VIP, a premium membership tier providing exclusive benefits, higher audio quality, and long-form content.
