Spine: If how hard your AI thinks matters more than its name, who will end up deciding how hard it thinks, and what does that mean for you?
Answer: The AI itself: thinking really does change the answer, the labs sell names while every extra thought costs them money on a flat-fee plan, so I think the decision is moving to software that picks for you, which helps the many who leave the default alone and costs anyone who can't see what it picked.

1. THE NAME GAME | proves: the labs keep the spotlight on new names, because a launch buys attention, users and money, so the setting that decides your answer gets chosen out of sight
2. THE SWITCH THAT MATTERS | proves: on the same model, more thinking gives better answers on hard questions, with limits (each extra thought helps less, and easy questions can be overthought)
3. WHAT THINKING COSTS THEM | proves: every extra thought is a cost on your flat-fee plan and a sale on the developer meter, which is why the app starts you in a low gear
4. WHO DECIDES NEXT | proves: in my view the choice is moving to the AI itself, even though OpenAI handed it back to Plus and Pro users in September, because the name sells and the gearbox decides the bill
5. THE TRADE-OFFS | proves: an AI that decides helps people who leave the gear where the app puts it, and wastes less, but hides what you got and lets the company's costs steer your answer
6. WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT | proves: until you can see the gear, pick it yourself for anything you will act on; in my view the car is going automatic next, and like a Formula 1 driver you want the paddles
7. OUR PREDICTION | proves: by 30 June 2027 ChatGPT starts Plus users on the automatic again (how sure we are: 85%), and what would prove us wrong
