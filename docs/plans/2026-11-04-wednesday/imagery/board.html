Claude Duet: what goes on screen

Every kind of picture in Wednesday's cuts, rendered from the real material. The header cards and charts you have already seen sit between these. Everything moves: nothing holds still for more than about a second and a half.

1 Your screen grabs, in your hand

Your 29 October Duet recording, set in the approved hand-held phone. Everything private sits under ink bars: your name, the K avatar, the job-hunt words, your ventures, the people, places and amounts in the to-dos, the morning brief's body, the Artifacts titles.

2 Video demo

Your recording playing inside the phone, sped up five times, with the camera pushing in. This is how every screen beat plays: video, never a still.

3 The real pages, the line marked

Screenshots of the live pages, the proving words marked in mint. Each one slides in and pushes towards its line as you say it.

Reddit refused the browser (a login wall on both reddit.com and old.reddit.com), so the two Reddit lines (long beats 24 and 25) are not shown here. Production uses a screenshot taken in a signed-in browser, username covered, or the drawn number card alone.

5 Custom art-directed object: The Queue mock for Codex

This piece's version of the passports: one real object that carries the idea. Paper work orders on a dispatch rail stop at the lilac line until the mint YES is stamped. Drawn by Claude to fix the idea and the timing; Codex makes the finished plates (codex-plates.md).

6 Motion test

The big moment, frame by frame at 30 fps with the brand's curves: orders slide in and sway, the camera pushes, the first stops at the line, YES stamps, it crosses and the queue moves up.
