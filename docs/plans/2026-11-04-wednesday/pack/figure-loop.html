Checks your apps
Gmail, Slack, your calendar, HubSpot, or any of the "over 6,000 connectors and plugins"
Each morning
Sends a brief
a short note on what matters today
In between
Reads, drafts, prepares
the work that's waiting, so it's ready when you look
Then it stops
