What makes the trip from Claude to Duet

Power users have built a lot around Claude. Not all of it comes along.

Comes with you
  • Your memory
  • The standard skills and connectors on your Claude account
  • The apps you connect: email, calendar, Slack, your CRM
  • Your past chats, which it can search
Stays behind
  • Files on your computer
  • Some of your Claude projects
  • Skills that live on your own computer
  • Custom connectors, in its scheduled runs
  • Claude Code sessions on your laptop
  • Microsoft 365, not connected yet

Claude Team and Enterprise plans: not yet. Anthropic says Duet comes to them later.
