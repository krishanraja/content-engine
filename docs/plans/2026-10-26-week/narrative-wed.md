## End message

Krish, 2026-10-26, on question 1: "yes, that's where Wednesday should land, but should acknowledge that this is another
horse in the race for proactive AI assistants rather than reactive chat bots - what does that actually mean for your
life and business (obviously everyone will cede responsibility to tools like this because they are lazy, but at what
cost?)."

Draft, with his addition: Claude Duet is the newest entrant in the race to build AI assistants that act before you
ask, where the chatbots of the last few years waited to be asked. Most people will hand more and more to tools like
this, because it is easier, so the question is what that costs. The useful ones read, draft and prepare on their own
and stop for your yes before anything leaves the building or cannot be undone; three questions tell you where that
line should sit for any assistant: does it reach outside, can it be undone, does it set the standard?

**Superseded the same day.** Krish, 2026-10-26: "I think for Wednesday's Claude piece we've already done that message

Draft 2, for his yes: if you already run your work through Claude, Duet is the part that starts without you. It
takes the memory, skills and connected apps you already have, checks them every few hours, briefs you each morning
and keeps working after you close the laptop, then stops for your yes before anything is sent, paid, signed or
deleted. Today that is for a personal Pro or Max account in the US only; Team and Enterprise accounts do not have it
yet, so a business that wants it has to decide whether staff may connect work apps to a personal account.
