Here's what's really inside the AI everyone's selling you. I'm Krish.

Millions of people just handed their boring jobs to a new app from Meta. It's called Muse. One person says a shop charged them $6,000 by mistake, for clothes that cost about $1,500. Muse wrote to the shop to get the money back. So what can Muse do that ChatGPT can't?

Muse is an AI helper that does jobs for you. It visits websites, fills in forms and argues for a better price.

Now the honest bit. Most of this isn't new. A CNN reporter found that ChatGPT and Google's Gemini can do similar jobs. Muse even suggested two places for a date night that had closed years ago. Very romantic.

Why is it so popular? It's easy, and free for most things. But I think the big reason is something else. Meta owns Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. Muse can turn a recipe video you saved on Instagram into a shopping list. ChatGPT can't see your saved videos. So Muse isn't cleverer than ChatGPT. It just gets to your photos, chats and saved videos more easily.

So there's a downside. The more apps you let Muse into, the more useful it gets. But Meta sees more of your life too. One reviewer let it read their email, then got worried. Their email holds the secret codes websites send to check it's really you. Anyone reading those could get into other accounts.

A security expert found a hole in the Mac version. A virus already on your Mac could secretly take charge of Muse. Then it could get into your email and anything else Muse can open. Meta fixed it the next day. But it shows how much power these apps have.

Meta says it won't use your Muse chats for its adverts. But there's a setting that lets Meta use your chats to teach its AI new things. Your chats become its homework. That setting starts switched on. I'd switch it off. Meta has also promised a locked mode. Not even Meta could read your stuff. Muse is free, but some people pay for extras. One privacy expert worries only paying users will get it.

So give it small jobs that are easy to fix if it gets them wrong. Getting money back from a shop? Great. Your bank account? Maybe not yet.

If you run a business, Muse can now tell you how your Instagram posts and adverts are doing.

My prediction. I don't think free users in the US will get that locked mode by 31 March 2027. I give it a 35% chance. If Meta can't read your stuff, it can't learn from it. And free apps usually pay their bills with what they know about you.
