I put Meta's Muse and Elon Musk's Grok Bot head to head on five everyday jobs. The new done-for-you personal AI assistants. One wrote a lovely reply to a scammer. So which one wins?

First, my inbox. An email wanted to buy six of my products, but never said which. Grok flagged the scam. Muse wrote a polished reply and put it first on my to-do list.

But Muse passed another tester's scam traps. So Grok wins my inbox. Other people's tests: a draw.

Now, a cheap New York flight. About three hundred and thirty-five pounds return, with twenty-nine hours in Lisbon. That's quite a lot of Portugal for a trip to New York. Both steered me away.

So, on my tests, Grok wins. Add other people's, and it's closer.

My call: Grok for repeatable admin. Muse for personal errands, if your friends live on WhatsApp and Instagram.
