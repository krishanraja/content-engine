I put two done-for-you AI assistants head to head. Meta's Muse and Elon Musk's Grok Bot. One wrote a lovely reply to a scammer. So which one wins?

First, my inbox. An email wanted six of my products, but never said which. Grok flagged the scam. Muse wrote a polished reply and made it my top job. Customer service, for people trying to rob me.

But Muse passed another tester's scam traps. So Grok wins my inbox. Other people's tests: a draw.

Then, a cheap New York flight. About three hundred and thirty-five pounds, with twenty-nine hours in Lisbon. That's quite a lot of Portugal for a trip to New York. Both steered me away. But Muse miscounted my nights.

So on my tests, Grok wins. Add other people's, and it's closer.

My call: Grok for repeatable admin. Muse for personal errands, if your friends all live on WhatsApp.
