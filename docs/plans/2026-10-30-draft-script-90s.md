Junior hiring at Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, has collapsed. So where will your senior staff come from in 2030?

Let me break down Anthropic's study of its own staff. Its technical hires have gone from one in four juniors to fewer than one in ten. And many of its managers say AI is why.

Machines have taken starter work before, like cash machines and spreadsheets. Both times, a new bottom rung appeared to learn on.

But I think this time is different. AI takes the first draft itself, the very work juniors learn on. And no one gets fired, because the junior job just never opens.

As a junior at Microsoft, I got a boring spreadsheet job. It taught me to spot work that repeats, and automate it.

Before you cut a junior role, ask two things. Who will your seniors be? And what are they learning on today?

Here's what I'd do with this info if I were you. Name each team's future seniors, and what they're learning on now. And give one junior a dull weekly job, by hand first, then with AI.
