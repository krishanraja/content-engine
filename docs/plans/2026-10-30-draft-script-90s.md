Junior hiring at Anthropic, who make Claude, has collapsed. So where will your senior staff come from in 2030?

I've read Anthropic's staff study, so let me break it down. In early 2025, one in four of its new technical hires was a junior. Now it's fewer than one in ten, and AI is a big part of why.

Now, machines have taken starter work before, like cash machines and spreadsheets. Both times, the jobs held up.

But I think this time is different. AI takes the very work juniors learn on. And no one gets fired, because the junior job just never opens.

As a junior at Microsoft, I got a boring spreadsheet job. Doing it is how I learned to spot work that repeats, and automate it.

Before you cut a junior role, ask two things. Who will be your senior in 2030? And what are they learning on today?

Here's what I'd do with this info if I were you. Name each team's 2030 seniors, and what they're learning on now. And give one junior a dull weekly job, by hand first, then with AI.
