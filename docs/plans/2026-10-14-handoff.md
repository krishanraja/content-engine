### 1. One spine per piece (the drift)

Why Wednesday sprawled: the piece began as "what will each AI company ask you
to hand over", became "Muse scored against its rivals", then "three kinds of
AI", then took on the money (ads), the shops (Amazon and Walmart), a new
prediction (airlines) and today the Siri section. Every re-angle was added as
new sections; none replaced the old spine. The engine checks facts, voice,
length and titles, but nothing checks that every section answers one
question. Today's Siri section was my own addition on top, without testing it
against a spine.

The guard to build:
- At the outline, one **spine**: a question and its one-line answer, which
  Krish approves. Stored on the piece.
- Each section of the outline names the part of the answer it proves. At
  most five sections.
- A judge in the engine reads the draft and asks, section by section, "does
  this answer the spine question?". Any "no" blocks the scripts and the
  publish page until the section is cut or Krish approves a new spine.
- A new angle means a new spine and a new outline from scratch: old sections
  are dropped, not kept. The house rule ONE_SPINE carries his words.
