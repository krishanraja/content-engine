# Monday: proposed narrative and visuals

Requested by Krish on 19 October. Proposal for presentation and future refinement;
the accepted recordings and checked article remain unchanged. Facts below come
from the current filed article, publish-body.md. Suggested spoken lines are new
copy, not quotations from the recording. No new paid research or rendering.

## The story in one sentence

The AI price is temporary; the work your business builds around it is much harder
to change. Take the savings, but build a budget and a working process that survive
the next offer.

## What makes it entertaining without making it childish

The reveal does the work: a small footnote, a large bill; a real discount, a bill
that still doubles; three apparently different pricing stories, one budgeting
problem. Use one dry aside at most. Keep the audience as leaders of real businesses,
not fictional shopkeepers. Do not add jokes at the expense of a person's competence.

as rendered, watched or included in the accepted MP4s. There is no need to delay
publication of the existing accepted cuts for these additions.
