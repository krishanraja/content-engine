I've been looking at what's really inside the AI everyone's selling us. I'm Krish.

So I've been having a little play around with Muse, Meta's new personal assistant. It's an important evolution in how AI is going to come into our personal lives. So I wanted to break it down for you. And the big question is: what do we have to hand over to get one?

So why not just use ChatGPT or Claude? You ask those a question and they answer. Muse goes off and does the job. Meta gives Muse its own computer in the cloud. From there, Muse opens websites, fills in forms and sends emails for you.

ChatGPT and Claude can do jobs like this too. But you usually pay. Muse is free, and it's built into apps you already use, like Instagram and WhatsApp. That's the real difference.

And Meta isn't alone. OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Apple all have assistants like this now. Each one wants a different part of your life. Meta wants your personal life. OpenAI wants your work. Google wants what's already in your Gmail. Anthropic wants to be the careful one at work. Apple wants to be the private one.

Same prize, though. Your assistant visits websites for you, so whoever runs it sits between you and the internet.

Here's the catch. To do your errands, an assistant needs your emails, your passwords and your bank card. All of that has to be kept somewhere. There are three ways.

One. Your details stay on your own phone. Apple does a lot of Siri's work this way. When the phone isn't enough, Apple uses its own servers, and says even Apple can't see your data there.

Two. Your details sit on Meta's servers, and Meta can look at them if it needs to. That's how Muse works today. Meta's own safety page says so.

Three. Your details sit on Meta's or Google's servers, but scrambled so only you can read them. Not even Meta or Google. Both say this is coming. Neither has done it yet.

And yes, the ads. Meta makes almost all its money from ads. I think one day, shops will pay to be the one Muse recommends. The bigger question is who can see your life.

So which one should you use? If you live on Instagram and WhatsApp, Muse is free. Turn off the setting that lets Meta train its AI on your chats. If you live in Gmail, try Google's. For work, Claude. And if privacy matters most, Siri does less, but keeps more to itself.

My prediction. By 30 June 2027, OpenAI, Google or Anthropic will launch an assistant that shops for you the third way. Your details scrambled, so even they can't read them. I'd put that at 55%.
