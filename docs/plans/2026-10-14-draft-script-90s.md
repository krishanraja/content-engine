Meta just launched Muse, an AI assistant that actually does things for you. And OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Apple are all building their own. So what are we giving up to get one?

To do your errands, an assistant needs your email, your calendar and your bank card. That lives in one of three places. On your phone. On the company's servers, where the company can get to it. Or on their servers but encrypted, so even the company can't read it.

Muse runs on Meta's servers, and Meta says that doesn't stop Meta getting to your data. Apple does a lot of Siri's work in a private cloud it says even Apple can't see. Meta and Google say encrypted versions are coming.

And the ads? Meta makes almost all its money from ads. So I think shops will one day pay to be the one Muse recommends. The bigger question is who can see your life.

My prediction. By 30 June 2027, OpenAI, Google or Anthropic will launch an assistant that shops for you. And your data will stay encrypted, even from them. I'm 55% sure.
