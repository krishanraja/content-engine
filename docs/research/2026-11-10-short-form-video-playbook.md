# Short-Form Video Playbook

> Filed 10 Nov 2026 as an observation source for `config/video-styles.json`, never taste proof: Krish shared it with the request to decide video style before the script. Its five formats are the catalogue's `playbook_formats`.

## What this is built on

No playbook makes every video go viral. In this set, 1 video in 7 broke out past 10x its creator's normal views, and that is the realistic target. What can be bulletproof is the process: every video passes the same checklist, and you learn which format works for you inside 30 posts.

- **Source:** the 100 most recent videos in your [Sandcastles workspace](https://app.sandcastles.ai/videos) (228 tracked), 19 creators, all AI and tech, posted roughly early October to 10 November 2026.
- **Spread:** 28 videos under 2x, 40 at 2 to 5x, 18 at 5 to 10x, 14 at 10x or more. Outlier score is views against the creator's own baseline.
- **What it cannot see:** captions and stats only, not the footage. Opening frames, pacing and visuals are inferred, so treat hook rules as hypotheses until checked against the videos.
- **Two distortions:** small accounts inflate the score (34.5x on 73K views), and the second-highest outlier (323.9x) was a paid Apple partnership, so its reach may be bought. Neither is used as evidence below.

## What the outliers have in common

The breakouts show a scene, put the stake on the viewer, and arrive while the news is fresh. Concept explainers sat at baseline: a clear transformer-architecture video scored 1x.

1. **A scene beats a concept.** The top organic video (593.7x, 8.4M views) is two AI agents left alone in a job interview, stuck saying goodbye on a loop. Others: 80s action stars in an AI police lineup (18.7x), a camera tracking restaurant tables (12.6x, 1.5M).
2. **The stake lands on the viewer.** "ChatGPT remembers you" hit 25.3x, the highest for that creator. "Your AI text is now watermarked in the EU" hit 21.3x and 2.7M views.
3. **Access only the creator has.** Sitting in Toto Wolff's chair in the Mercedes F1 garage hit 51.1x. Her 30 videos in the set have a median of 3.3x.
4. **A repeatable format plus daily volume.** The creator posting daily (day 484) has the highest median of anyone with 10+ posts, 7x, and posted his biggest video twice for 217x and 233x.
5. **Comment-keyword CTAs are table stakes.** "Comment X and I'll DM you" appears on videos from 1x to 25x. The topic decides whether a video breaks out.
6. **Engagement is a different game from reach.** The outrage-led ethics creator averages 9% engagement but a 2.4x median. Anger buys comments from existing followers, not new viewers.

Use these five and nothing else until one of them hits 5x twice for you. Your strongest bet is Insider access: you run a 14-agent OS in production, and real numbers from inside it are something no one else in this set can film.

## Production rules for every video

Each video carries one idea, opens on the scene, and closes on one ask. The hook rules come from captions, so check them against the footage before treating them as settled.

1. **Hook, first two seconds.** Open on the thing itself: the screen, the room, the result. Say the stake in the viewer's terms in one plain sentence, with a number if you have one. Working examples from the set: "ChatGPT remembers you." "I'm in Toto's seat."
2. **Body, one idea.** Teaching goes in three to five numbered steps. Stories get one setup and one turn. Cut anything that serves a second idea; it is the next video.
3. **The why line.** Close the body with one sentence on why it matters to the viewer. The top resource-drop creators end every video this way.
4. **CTA, one ask.** Follow, then comment one keyword for a resource that already exists. Never promise a guide you have not built.
5. **Length.** Your explainer slot is 15 to 60 seconds. Stories land under 30.
6. **Caption.** First line repeats the keyword ask. Then restate the steps, add two questions people would type into search ("Who can see my ChatGPT chats?"), and finish with three to five hashtags.
7. **Claims.** Every number on screen has a source you can name. The set shows self-relevant fear works; outrage does not grow reach, so leave it out.

## Pre-publish checklist

A video ships only when every box is ticked.

- [ ] It uses one of the five formats, named before filming
- [ ] The first frame shows the thing, not your face explaining it
- [ ] The first sentence names the viewer's stake, with a number where one exists
- [ ] It is dated or exclusive: a change from this week, or access only you have
- [ ] It carries one idea; teaching is three to five numbered steps
- [ ] It ends on the why line, then one keyword ask
- [ ] The resource behind the keyword exists and the DM is ready to send
- [ ] Every on-screen number has a named source
- [ ] Caption: keyword line, steps, two search questions, three to five hashtags
- [ ] Runs under 60 seconds, under 30 for a story

## How to run it

Post 30 videos in 30 days across the five formats, score each against your own baseline at 72 hours, and keep whatever wins.

- **Score:** views at 72 hours divided by your median. Under 2x is normal, 5x is a signal, 10x is a breakout.
- **Double down:** a format that hits 5x twice gets half the next 30 slots.
- **Repost winners:** re-cut a 10x video with a new first line and post it again within three weeks. It worked twice in this set.
- **Kill rule:** a format at under 2x after six tries is dropped.

**Next 72 hours:** open the top ten outliers in Sandcastles and run Analyze on them, so the hook rules can be checked against real footage. If Analyze spends credits, that is your call before it runs. Then film three Insider access videos from inside mind/make OS.

**Counterpoints.** Daily volume is the one input the top creator has that a checklist cannot copy; at three posts a week, expect the 30-video test to take ten weeks. And this set is consumer AI. Senior commercial leaders may reward Changed this week over spectacle, which only your own numbers will show.
