Prepared 2026-10-14. Read-only research. Nothing was posted, uploaded or changed.

## 0. The three facts that decide the plan

1. **Substack already makes podcast episodes, but its feed is blocked.** Each
   "VIDEO:" post is a Substack podcast item with an MP3 enclosure. The feed at
   https://home.makeyourmindup.ai/feed carries `<itunes:block>Yes</itunes:block>`.
   Channel title: "makeyourmind/up". No Spotify or Apple listing exists from it.
   Episode art on all four is Substack's generic default
   (`podcast_episode_image_info.isDefault: true`).
2. **The 16:9 long cuts are short.** All four are 1920x1080, 152 to 188 seconds
   (about 2.5 to 3 minutes). The full articles are 1,233 to 2,130 words, which
   is 8 to 14 minutes read aloud.
3. **Video from a non-partner host cannot earn on Spotify.** Substack is not on
   Spotify's video-partner host list. Video added on top of a Substack feed
   "can't be monetized". The Spotify Partner Program needs the show hosted on
   Spotify for Creators.

## 1. Spotify for Creators requirements (primary sources)

### Show cover art
- Size and ratio: "aim for dimensions between 1400x1400 to 3000x3000 pixels,
  keeping a ratio of 1:1." https://creators.spotify.com/resources/create/dos-donts-showart
  (published 2019-08-02).
- Newer guide says "3,000 x 3,000 pixels", square, and a podcast name that stays
  readable small. https://creators.spotify.com/resources/grow/podcast-seo
  (published 2026-08-26).
- Format and file size: no Spotify for Creators page states them. [UNCERTAIN]
  Industry norm (Apple's rule, which most hosts mirror): JPEG or PNG, RGB,
  under 512 KB to 1 MB. Make it 3000x3000 JPEG, sRGB, under 1 MB to be safe.
- Upload: "Changes usually appear within 24 hours" (paraphrase of the page).
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/uploading-cover-art/

### Episode art
- Episodes use show art by default. Custom art can be uploaded per episode, web
  only. Once added it "can't" be removed, only replaced by re-uploading show art.
  No dimensions given. https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/uploading-cover-art/
- For video episodes the image that matters is the thumbnail: "Horizontal",
  "16:9 aspect ratio", "Suggested resolution of 1920 x 1080". No format or file
  size given. https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/thumbnails/

### Audio files
- "We support MP3, M4A, and WAV file types." Mono or stereo.
- "We don't have a file size limit."
- Tip: keep "embedded artwork, ID3v2 tags, and other metadata as small as possible."
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/publishing-audio-episodes/
- Bitrate and loudness: no official Spotify podcast figure found. [UNCERTAIN]
  Use the industry norm: MP3 128 to 192 kbps stereo (or WAV master), loudness
  -16 LUFS integrated stereo, true peak -1 dBTP.

### Video episodes
Spotify video specs page, https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/video-specs/
- File size: "Less than 10 GB" recommended; "Up to 60 GB" compatible.
- Duration: "Less than 4 hours" recommended; "Up to 12 hours" compatible.
- Container: "MP4" recommended; "MOV" compatible; "We discourage FLV and AVI".
- "One video track", "One audio track", "Audio and video tracks must be the same duration".
- Codec: "H.264" and "H.265 (HEVC)".
- Resolution: "1080p or higher" recommended; "180p or higher" compatible.
- Aspect: "16:9 widescreen" recommended; "Any aspect ratio" compatible.
- Bitrate: "1080p source: 25 Mbit/second CBR".
- Frame rate: "24, 25, 30, 50, or 60 FPS".
- Keyframes: "Approximately one keyframe per second recommended".
- Colour: "Rec.709", SDR, "Progressive scan (no interlacing)".
- Audio: "AAC-LC", "128 Kbps or higher", "Stereo".

Publishing videos page, https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/publishing-videos/
- "We support MOV, MPG and MP4 video files."
- "The duration of your file cannot be longer than 12 hours."
- Audio "Bitrate of 192 Kbps or higher" (conflicts with 128 above; use 192). 
- "You need to verify your account before you can publish your first video or episode."
- "You can only upload video content on Spotify for Creators web."
- "Videos and video episodes are only available on Spotify." Other apps get audio.
- "audio from your video will be available through your RSS feed."

### Same video as YouTube
- "If you publish video podcasts on other platforms like YouTube, you can upload
  the same files to Spotify for Creators."
  https://creators.spotify.com/resources/create/how-to-start-a-video-podcast-on-spotify
- Only rule: you must own it. "It's against our Terms of Service to upload video
  content that belongs to someone else." (publishing-videos page). Music beds must
  be licensed for podcast use too; check the ledger licence covers Spotify.

### Titles and descriptions
- No hard character limit for show name, show description, episode title or
  episode description was found on any Spotify page. [UNCERTAIN]
  Safe ceilings from Apple's spec, which Spotify feeds also travel through:
  show/episode description up to 4,000 characters; keep titles under about 60.
- Show name: "Keep it short (three to four words)." "Avoid abbreviations,
  punctuation, and misspelled words that confuse the algorithm."
  https://creators.spotify.com/resources/grow/podcast-seo
- Description: the first 250 characters are "what's visible in the Spotify app
  before a listener has to tap to expand." Same source.
- Episode description: "Three to four sentences are usually enough." Same source.
- Episode titles: "think about search intent." Same source.
- Formatting: bold, italic, lists, links; "Only HTTPS links". No length limit stated.
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/formatting-your-show-notes/

### Categories
- Set from "The <itunes:categories> field in your RSS feed", or chosen "when you
  submit your podcast or show" on Spotify for Creators, plus "Our editorial team
  curates categories your show may appear in." No count stated.
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/changing-podcast-show-categories-on-spotify/
- Suggested: Technology (primary), Business (secondary), News > Tech News if offered.

### Chapters
https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/episode-chapters/
- Timestamps in the description, one per line, e.g. "(00:00) Intro".
- "You need at least 3 chapters". "Your first chapter should start at 00:00".
- "at least 30 seconds between each chapter's start time."
- "Aim for chapter titles under 40 characters." Plain text, no emojis.
- Auto chapters exist, English only, "aren't available to all shows yet."
- Effect: a 3-minute episode can hold at most about 5 chapters. Worth it from
  8 minutes up.

### Clips (vertical promo)
https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/clips/
- "between 15-90 seconds long", "Isn't bigger than 1GB", ".MP4 or .MOV", must have sound.
- Vertical preferred (the page says "Vertical (16x9) preferred", a typo; read as 9:16).
- One clip per episode. Shown on the show page, Home, Podcast feeds, Browse, Now Playing.
- The 90-second 9:16 short cut Krish already makes fits this exactly.

### RSS import versus hosting on Spotify
https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/adding-a-new-show/
- "Create a new show": "Your episodes are automatically published to Spotify."
  Audio, video or both.
- "Find an existing show": for shows hosted elsewhere; "You'll need your
  podcast's RSS feed to claim or submit your podcast."
https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/video-episodes-for-shows-not-hosted-with-spotify/
- Video-partner hosts: Audioboom, Audiomeans, Buzzsprout, Flightcast, Libsyn,
  Podigee, Podspace. Substack is not listed.
- Others "can still replace existing audio episodes with video, directly on
  Spotify for Creators", but those "can't be monetized".

### Can the Substack podcast feed go to Spotify?
- Yes, for audio. Substack's own page: "Publish video to YouTube automatically,
  and add to Spotify for Creators from your dashboard." https://substack.com/podcasts
- Substack help article (403 to my fetch; content from search snippet,
  [UNCERTAIN] wording): connect under podcast settings > distribution > "Connect
  to Spotify"; only the audio of a video episode goes automatically; video must be
  uploaded by hand in Spotify for Creators; Private Mode blocks the feed; do not
  also submit the feed yourself or you get a duplicate show.
  https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/24766453298324-How-can-I-distribute-my-paid-Substack-podcast-on-Spotify
- Today the feed has itunes:block = Yes, so nothing would list until that changes.

### Monetisation gate (for later)
https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/spotify-partner-program/
- "Host your show on Spotify for Creators"
- "Have published at least 3 episodes"
- "Hit at least 2,000 consumption hours on Spotify in the last 30 days"
- "Hit at least 1,000 audience count on Spotify in the last 30 days"
- "You can't earn Premium video revenue for any video episodes without ad breaks."
- More markets from "October 20th, 2026". "Eligibility criteria may change at any time."
- Maths: 2,000 hours from 3-minute episodes needs about 40,000 full views a month.
  From 10-minute episodes, about 12,000. Longer episodes reach the bar 3x faster.

## 2. What grows a new show on Spotify (2025-2026)

- Video: "More than 500 million users have streamed a video podcast", up nearly
  50% year on year. Spotify Newsroom, 2026-05-21,
  https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-05-21/investor-day-podcast-features-updates/
- Video for new shows: "40% of Spotify users (2 in 5) who streamed a new show in
  2024 chose a video show." https://creators.spotify.com/resources/create/how-to-start-a-video-podcast-on-spotify
- Clips: recommended across Home, Browse and Now Playing (clips page above). This
  is the main discovery surface for a show with no audience.
- Comments replaced Q&A. "Q&A is replaced by Comments".
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/how-to-use-q-a/
  Creators approve each comment and get "a single reply to each listener comment".
  https://creators.spotify.com/resources/grow/audience-engagement (2024-07-09)
- Polls: 2 to 7 options, one poll per episode.
  https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/how-to-use-polls/
  Fits mind.the.gap's dated Call and the week's one question.
- Call to action: "build a call to action into your episode scripts, preferably
  in both the episode intro and outro". Same engagement article.
- 73% of listeners "are interested in finding more opportunities to interact
  with their favorite podcast hosts." Same article.
- Consistency: no Spotify primary source found that quantifies it. [UNCERTAIN]
  Treat a fixed Mon/Wed/Fri slot as good practice, not a proven Spotify signal.
- Discovery via search: short, keyword-led show name and the first 250
  characters of the description (podcast-seo guide).

## 4. Recommendation

### Where to host
Create a **new show on Spotify for Creators** ("Create a new show"), and upload
video directly. Do not connect the Substack feed to Spotify.
- Why: native video, clips, polls, comments, and the Partner Program all need
  Spotify hosting. Substack-to-Spotify gives audio by default, video by hand,
  and that video "can't be monetized".
- Spotify for Creators can then distribute its own RSS to Apple and others
  (audio only there).
- Keep the Substack VIDEO posts as they are. Leave the Substack feed blocked to
  avoid a duplicate show.
- [UNCERTAIN] Check the music-bed licence covers Spotify before the first upload.

### Format: combination of (b) now, then (a) as a video read
- **Now (this week): (b) the 16:9 long cut as a video episode.** Zero new
  recording. Backfill the four existing long cuts (10-05, 10-07, 10-12, 10-14) so
  the show opens with 4 episodes, past the 3-episode bar. Attach the 9:16 short
  as the Spotify clip.
- **From week 3: the "full read" video episode, 8 to 12 minutes.** Krish reads
  the article body on the teleprompter in the same session as the long cut. The
  body is already written and fact-checked, so it costs one extra read of about
  10 minutes, not a new script. At about 150 words a minute: 1,200 words is 8
  minutes, 2,000 words is 13 minutes. Trim bodies over 1,800 words to the spoken
  core. Cut it with the same intro sting, bed and B-roll as the long cut; add
  chapters from the article's section headings.
- This is option (a) done as video, not audio only. On Spotify, audio-only is the
  weaker product: new listeners lean to video (40% stat), and the same file can
  go to YouTube as a longer watch.
- **Not yet: (c) conversational episodes.** Highest effort (guests, scheduling,
  editing), no teleprompter, no pipeline in the engine. Revisit after 8 to 12
  weeks of data. A monthly "Calls" review (mind.the.gap rulings) is the natural
  first one.

### Length and cadence
- Length: 8 to 12 minutes for the full-read episode. Keep the 3-minute long cut
  as YouTube's main video and as the Spotify episode only until the full read starts.
- Cadence: 3 a week, same day as the Substack post: Monday follow.the.money,
  Wednesday under.the.hood, Friday mind.the.gap. Publish at the same hour each day.
- Number episodes and put the series line at the start of each title, e.g.
  "follow.the.money: Warner sued Suno. Who else gets paid?" [UNCERTAIN] Spotify
  advises against punctuation in the show name, not in episode titles.

### Show name
- Spotify search already holds near neighbours: "Make Your Mind" (Jess Leondiou,
  makeyourmind.co), "Make Up Your Mind With Jahleane Dolne", "What Makes Up Your
  Mind" (Stanford Psychiatry), "Make Up Your Mindset". (Spotify search, 2026-10-14.)
- Spotify advice: three to four words, a keyword, no punctuation.
- Recommended: **"Make Your Mind Up on AI"** (5 words, keyword, distinct from
  the neighbours). Keep "makeyourmind/up" as the wordmark on the art, not in the
  name field, because the slash is punctuation.
- Alternative: "Make Your Mind Up AI". Avoid plain "makeyourmindup" (one
  unsearchable token) and plain "Make Your Mind Up" (crowded).
- This is Krish's call.
