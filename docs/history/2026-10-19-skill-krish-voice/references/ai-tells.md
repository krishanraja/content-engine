# AI-tell QA

Remove:

- generic warm-ups and throat-clearing;
- symmetrical three-part lists used for rhythm rather than meaning;
- inflated claims such as game-changing, revolutionary, or the future is here;
- fake quotations, invented scenes, and unsupported certainty;
- excessive headings, labels, parentheticals, and explanatory summaries;
- generic closers asking the audience what they think;
- em dashes;
- prose that sounds smoother than the underlying evidence deserves.

Do not make the copy artificially rough. Specificity, compression, and an honest point of view create the voice.
