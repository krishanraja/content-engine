# ve producing runbook

Target: <1 hour after recording; S0-S8 total **55 engine minutes**, fixes included. Prepare direction/devices before/during recording. Gates/Krish-wait are separate. `ve` means `python scripts/ve.py`; replace <fields>. No --proof/--all flags exist: the proof is its own verb, `ve proof`.

**One piece, one session** (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-04: "Yes, I would assume one piece and one session would be good, with Claude orchestrating and Codex doing asset production."): the session that starts a piece finishes it or brings him the cut or one question. Claude orchestrates; during the piece Codex is dispatched only for that piece's named plates, and engine or library cards wait until it is filed. A new look is tried carefully, first in the lab (`ve lab render`: every style and caption look on one window of his pinned take, outside any piece, `ve lab verdict` with his words), then on the proof of his own take, never by producing whole cuts to see. After any change to the renderer or the render script, run `ve lab render` again and `ve lab diff --against <the last lab folder>`: the house look must not move (Krish, 2026-11-04: "we need to experiment carefully, as opposed to just producing videos experimentally and then seeing that they're shit"). Before questioning a name or line he used, read what he said on the take, not the Teleprompter script (he paraphrases on camera): `ve said <piece> --take <id> --teleprompter <script.txt>` lists every difference and proposes the words the cut should carry, spelled as the script spells them. One production round (plan 4 Nov 2026): each decision is made on his own footage before the whole cut renders, one press each on the board's day tab: Sign storyboard (with `ve expect`'s sheet), Pick a style (`ve styles`), Approve proof (`ve proof`), Approve cut (the packet). Never ask in chat what a press asks; an overnight run stops at the next unpressed gate and never renders past it.

stills returns 2 today. Stop unless approved existing stills cover the stage; quick-edit is fallback. This procedure does not certify a complete ve path. In shadow (8 Nov), run S3 to S7 on the same takes as quick-edit and stop before S8: shadow cuts are watched side by side, never filed.

At boundaries: `ve time <piece> status` (0 within the engine-minutes cap, 1 above; the cap is `engine_minutes_cap` in `config/ve/budget.json`, 300 today), `ve ledger <piece>` (OK/WARN/OVER; 1 above any cap). Log actual usage: `ve ledger <piece> add claude_tokens <n>`, `ve ledger <piece> add codex_tokens <n>`; likewise allowance_points/credits, optionally --note <text>. Finite non-negative amounts only.

At >=80% stop the clock, put an ask in the gate packet and end. Refuse past caps (`config/ve/budget.json`): 300 engine minutes, 3.3M Claude, 2.1M Codex, 12.6M total tokens, 1.2 allowance points. Ledger only warns; dispatch checks recorded caps only with --piece; time start checks engine minutes only. Live time appears in time status until stopped. Stop/log before dispatch. Never read big files, individual images or raw logs: use QC JSON and one contact sheet. Human playback/listening still applies. **Maximum 2 fix loops total**: change spec, lint, compile, render, QC; then stop if blocked.

1. **S0; Claude/scripts; 3 min.** Preflight first (from 9 Nov `ve brief` and `ve render` refuse without it): `python3 scripts/check_failures.py --piece <piece>`, `python3 scripts/floor.py --format long_video --piece <piece>` and `--format short_video`, `python3 scripts/preflight.py <piece> --skill ve`. In Bash `. scripts/ve-env.sh` pins ffmpeg n8.1.2/tesseract. `ve doctor`: PASS/WARN/INFO, exit 0; hard FAIL exits 1, stop. `ve ledger <piece>`, `ve time <piece> start S0-S1` (started phase, 0), `ve start <piece>` (wrote session-pack.md <=36 KB, 0). Read ENGINE.md, this runbook, pack/spec. start needs an existing spec.

2. **S1; Director/Claude; 5 min.** Read `docs/ve/WORLD_CLASS.md` first (what the board references actually do and how a cut reaches them). Two treatments; `ve lint <spec>` after changes (clean, 0). `ve stills <piece>` exits 2: stop unless approved stills exist. `ve expect <spec>` writes `<ve dir>/expect.md`, at most six plain lines of what this cut will and won't do (only the ones that apply: no sfx kit, safe zones listed not held, wide cut with no locked key frames, outside clips muted, a format with no frozen lock, a style with no device, a hero device or style repeated from the last three filed pieces); it goes beside the storyboard, and `ve expect` records the engine it was written on (`<ve dir>/engine.json`). From 9 Nov (the Thursday guards, `docs/learning/2026-11-04-thursday-killed.md`, `scripts/velib/guards.py`): `ve lint` refuses a spec without `style.idea` (`object`, `moves` [{move, means}], `never`, and `real_artifacts` [{asset, text}] or `real_none`; the bar is `docs/plans/2026-11-02-monday/motion-system.md`) and any device that re-types a named real artifact's words; `ve proof` and `ve render` refuse a whole cut drawn on an engine changed since `engine.json` unless Krish's exception approval binds the new hash, and `ve proof` lists the cut's type treatments for him to judge moving (never refused by count); `ve render` refuses a third full render of a cut since his last press. Gate 1: Krish signs treatment/stills/bed/credits in storyboard (the Sign storyboard press, bound to the sha256 of the storyboard and expectations he saw). `ve time <piece> stop` prints recorded seconds/totals (0 unless over cap); end at gate. Log actual wait: `ve ledger <piece> add krish_wait_seconds <n>`.

3. **S2; Claude dispatches/verifies, Codex builds; 3 min.** New hero only; otherwise skip. The moment a card is queued, start `python scripts/ve.py lane wait <id>` in the background (a Monitor) and act on its first built or failed line; never a timed check-in (F22, Krish 2026-11-02). `ve lane queue` refuses a device card whose verbs are not in EngineEventSchema: add them to `apps/renderer/src/engine/props.ts` first. Never ask Krish to start Codex by hand. The lane needs a Control Center booking (`reserve_credit_spend`, pool codex) passed as `--spend-id`; the building computer reads no credit file. A built card's branch (`ve-card/<card>`) is pushed from the home computer, so WSL needs git access to GitHub once: point Ubuntu's git at the Git Credential Manager that ships with Git for Windows (`git config --global credential.helper` set to its `git-credential-manager.exe` under `/mnt/c/Program Files/Git`). Heartside and Full Time queue their own cards in their repositories (`codex/queue/<id>.md`, config/codex-lanes.json) and the same pass builds them. Log device-work engine_seconds, Codex tokens/allowance; avoid double-counting. Device work shares the cap. Dispatch reserves against the credit caps Control Center places on the publication (`ve budget status`) and refuses over it; a card needing a tool the Codex profile does not allow is held. Runway/Higgsfield/ElevenLabs: `ve ledger <piece> reserve <provider> <amount>` before each generation, `settle` after. The booking itself is Control Center's: the session running the dispatch or generation calls `reserve_credit_spend` before it (refused as over_share, held, unknown or allowance_first: stop), `settle_credit_spend` after with the actual cost and job ids (0 voids it), then `record_credit_balance` (service `codex`, unit `percent`, the percent left; service `codex-credits`, unit `credits`, the paid balance). Without fresh balances Control Center keeps codex-credits shut. The local cap is Control Center's read: monthly runway/higgsfield shares are spread over the weeks before `use_by`. `ve verify-card <card.md> --cwd <worktree>` must exit 0; verifier renders golden stills/reviews one contact sheet. No per-piece code/Codex production edits.

4. **S3; scripts; 6 min.** `ve time <piece> start S3-S5` (0). Per take: `ve ingest <piece> --take <id> --media <file> --aligner-a <name> --aligner-b <name> [--clock-a <json>] [--clock-b <json>]` (0; two different aligners; no paid aligner (Krish, 2026-10-29: "do not use eleven labs for this")). Captions are timed by forced alignment, not whisper (Thursday 5 Nov: whisper's word times ran 3 to 19 frames off his voice; MMS and wav2vec2 agreed within 2 frames on all 148 words): write both clocks with `python scripts/velib/forced_align.py <take> <whisper-words.json> mms-fa <a.json>` and `... wav2vec2-960h <b.json>`, then ingest with `--clock-a <a.json> --aligner-a torchaudio:mms-fa --clock-b <b.json> --aligner-b torchaudio:wav2vec2-base-960h`; QC hears the render the same way (`--heard mms=... --heard w2v=... --captions-drawn-by mms`). Without torch, ingest runs two local faster-whisper models itself (`--aligner-a faster-whisper:small.en --aligner-b faster-whisper:medium.en`, no clock files). The signed script words go to `<piece>/ve/script.words.json`. Per take, after ingest: `ve faces <piece> --take <id>` (0; advisory, needs mediapipe) names where he is off the lens, has his eyes closed or turns his head, and conform then lists those moments inside the cut in `cover.<cut>.json` for a graphic to cover; it never changes the take chosen. Per take, before ingest (Krish, 2026-11-05: eye gaze correction "for all post-processing of my makeyourmindup videos"): `ve eyeline [<piece>] --media <take> --backend <model>` (0: `<take>.eyeline.mp4` with the take's own sound copied and every frame time kept, checked before it is kept; 1: refused, the reason named, nothing kept) turns his eyes toward the lens only where they are off it, never on a blink, a turned head or a lost face, and writes before/after strips and an eye loop for the Critic; ingest the corrected file. With `--backend maxine --maxine-copy <copy>` it uses only where NVIDIA Maxine Eye Contact aims the lens point, never its pixels; the copy comes from one GitHub Actions run of `maxine-trial.yml` with `image=1.5.0` on a rented GPU (about $0.04 for a 96 s take, a spend Krish approves each time; see STATUS.md). One Maxine run calibrates a recording session: the session's other takes run with `--lens <that run's eyeline receipt>` and no pod (refused when his head sits more than 4 degrees from where it sat). It corrects only the drift of reading the prompter, by a push of his own eye; hard glances, looks up and down, squints and closing eyes are left as filmed. Before ingest, `python scripts/velib/eyeline_review.py <take> <corrected> --out <dir> --lens <receipt>` writes the blind before/after sheets, the key and the numbers for a fresh reviewer. `flx` (WangWilly/Hsu) is evaluation only and writes only to `.cache/eyeline-eval/` (velib/eyeline.py). Per take, after ingest, on the machine that holds the media: `ve hear <piece> --take <id> --ctc --whisper large-v3` (0; the listen pass, velib/hear.py; `--ctc` needs torch and torchaudio, `--whisper` faster-whisper) hears the hesitations, broken-off words and stutters the transcript left out, the voice no word covers and the quiet a join may sit in, measures every word, and stores it in the take record. conform refuses a take without it unless `--without-listen` is given, and then cuts the unwritten fumbles out like any flub, puts every join where the sound is quiet, and prefers the reading that sounded right (Krish, 5 Nov 2026: "automatically post-edit errors out"). After `ve said`: `ve lexicon learn <piece> --take <id> --teleprompter <script.txt>` (0) adds the take's names, mishearings, hesitations and added words to his lexicon (`config/voice/lexicon.json`), which every later transcription hears with; taste is never learned, only proposed. Once several takes have a listen pass: `ve voice profile <piece> [<piece>...]` writes his voice in numbers to `config/voice/profile.json`. Per cut: `ve conform <piece> --cut <cut> --render --assets-dir <assets> --joins-reel` (0: EDL, words, conformed picture and dialogue from one graph, joins reel; 1: the F09 lint or a line no take read, named). Read its "kept" lines: an unscripted word no agreed gap lets go is for his ear at Gate 3; so is every "for his ear" line, a word that sounded wrong (both clocks unsure of it, a hesitation one ear heard inside it, or far longer than his usual), which conform never cuts. Premix with `ve premix <spec> --cut <cut> --dialogue <assets>/dialogue.<cut>.wav --words <words> --props <props> --out <assets>/audio.<cut>.wav [--lift FROM-TO per chapter card]` (0; needs `MINDMAKE_MUSIC_ROOT`, which `python scripts/quick-edit/music.py fetch` prints). It writes `audio.<cut>.bed.json` and `audio.<cut>.music.wav` beside the stem for QC. `--lift` windows are on the cut's own clock; on a wide cut that opens on the intro sting, premix takes the sting's frames from the props only and delays his voice, his first and last word and every lift by exactly those frames, with the bed from the first sample (the sidecar records `lead_frames`), so premix from the props the cut renders from. Never retime picture alone.

5. **S4; fresh Director/scripts; 5 min.** `ve start <piece>` rebuilds pack (0); preserve running clock. `ve lint <spec>` must be clean (0). Per cut: `ve compile <spec> --cut <cut> --edl <piece>/ve/edl.<cut>.json --assets-dir <assets>` (after conform; or `--words <words.json>` for an already cut-ready master) prints props hash/path (0); QC then takes the same `--edl` so it measures the conformed joins, on the finished file's clock (each join moved by the sting's frames on a cut that opens on it). The opening (house rule HOOK_IS_THE_INTRO; Krish, 2026-11-05: "I think it should go at the start of the longer wide videos 100%,"): a 16:9 whole cut of a series piece on its series' day compiles with the series' approved intro sting in front (`props.sting`; the report's `opening` is `sting` with the clip's sha256), so his face and the hook come in on the frame after it; a 9:16 cut and an off-day piece put the hook on frame 0 (`opening: hook`). While `config/intro-sting.json` says `candidate` the wide cut stays on its hook and the report says why (`opening_why`); once approved, compile fetches a missing clip from the library (`python scripts/quick-edit/intro.py fetch`) and refuses a wide series cut that cannot have it. compile.<cut>.json needs lint clean, props_sha256, sfx_missing empty. Absent kits report referenced SFX/block status. Unsupported verbs refuse; test_grid draws carry.

6. **S5; scripts/Krish; 7 min. Every piece proves its hardest beat moving (F04, F13).** The style first, after he records: `ve styles <spec> --cut <cut> [--top 3]` takes the top three from `creative_direction.pick_motion` for the spec's points and channel, leaves out (and names) any style whose device is not built, and renders the same 8 to 15 s window once per style to `<ve dir>/styles/<module>.mp4` with `styles.json` (0). No take yet: it uses his last recorded take of the same format, labelled as such, never stock; none at all, it refuses. Only styles that look different from the house look and from each other are offered (`config/ve/style-distinct.json`; a look-alike gives way to the next ranked style); with fewer than two it refuses: pick a window with more graphic motion, or show the options as named styles with their rules. His Pick a style press records the module he picked (`gates.style` on the making record); set `style.motion` in the spec to it and write his verdict on each style shown (`python scripts/post-pack/creative_direction.py verdict --module M --verdict picked|seen|not-for-us --said "<his words>" --on <date>`; picked twice becomes tested, not-for-us is never offered again). Then the proof: `ve proof <spec> --cut <cut> [--beat <id>]` (0) renders only the hardest beat (`style.proof.beat` if named; else most distinct devices; ties to a SPLIT beat with a card, then the longest), grown to 8 s and cut to 15 s, never with the sting in front, from his real take through the same render path, and runs `ve qc` on the clip (`--no-qc` skips). It writes `<ve dir>/proof/<cut>.mp4` and `proof/<cut>.json` (beat, window, sha256, the spec's structural hash, style). `ve time <piece> stop`; end at Gate 2: Krish presses Approve proof or Reject (with his words) on the board. From 9 Nov `ve render` refuses a whole cut until that press is bound to the clip's sha256 and the spec's style, devices and beat plan are unchanged since; a caption edit or a retime under 0.5 s keeps the proof, anything structural needs a new one. Nothing skips proof.

7. **S6; scripts; 14 min.** After acceptance: `ve time <piece> start S6-S7` (0). Each cut: `ve render <props> <cut.mp4> --spec <spec> --assets-dir <assets>` (0, lag/residual/path). Same assets as compile; staged hashes must match. It refuses a sting that is not the approved clip, a wide series cut compiled without the approved sting, and a stem whose premix lead is not the sting's frames. Default scale 1; budget all cuts together.

8. **S7; scripts, fresh Critic/Claude, Krish; 10 min.** Each cut: `ve qc <cut.mp4> --format <format> --props <props> --out <ve-dir>/qc.<cut>.json` (0 measured pass, 1 failure). Require complete; absent OCR is not_run. QC reads the file on its own clock: on a cut that opens on the sting every beat, caption, join and the plate offset sit the sting's frames later, and its `opening` row passes only when the file's first frames are the approved clip, frame for frame, his cut starts on the next frame and the file holds the sting plus the cut; any other cut must hold exactly its own frames with no sting clip in front (the row needs the clip on this machine). The Critic checks the wide cut has one opening: the sting, then his face, with nothing between. Use QC JSON/one contact sheet, <=2 fix loops. Before the Critic: `ve refs <piece> <cut>` (0) puts the cut's frames above each inspiration-board reference named in `brand.references` (a reference not on this machine names its Drive id to download), and writes the cut in motion for the Critic (`beats`, `events` and `cuts.<cut>.jpg`, `numbers.<cut>.json`; it needs `props.<cut>.json`). The Critic scores the cut against each card and the scores go to `ve verdict write ... --references <scores.json>`; under 8/10 against any card the cut is NOT READY (`docs/ve/MOTION_STANDARD.md`, F26). From 9 Nov `ve refs` also writes `reference.<cut>.proof.jpg` (the cut above the proof he approved) and the Critic scores it as card `proof`, the same 8/10 rule; no approved proof is itself a hold. `ve packet` opens with "In short", at most ten plain lines (the round, the proof and what changed since, each advisory, each Critic note, the expectations), and counts the round in `<ve dir>/packet.record.json` (a new whole-cut file sent to him is one more; the same file again is not). Critic follows pack card, supplies verdict.<cut>.json. `ve status <piece>` exits 1 until checks/verdicts/exact-file approval exist. A Gate 2 or 3 packet is made only by `ve packet <piece>` (QC, composition, key frames, frozen design lock, and a Critic verdict from `ve verdict write` for that exact file); a new format first needs `ve lock freeze` (`docs/ve/design/TEMPLATE.md`). Log tokens, `ve time <piece> stop`; end at Gate 3 with cuts/joins reel for Krish. Never fabricate passes/approvals.

9. **S8; Claude/scripts; 2 min.** After Gate 3: `ve time <piece> start S8` (0), `ve status <piece>` (every cut READY; it prints `delivered: NO (not filed: run ve file)` and exits 1 until the cut is filed and read back from its 3 Posts folder). `ve file <piece> --subject <post subject> --dry-run` (0, prints where each cut goes), then without `--dry-run` on a home computer with `MINDMAKE_ARCHIVE_ROOT` and `MINDMAKE_LIBRARY_ROOT` set (`. scripts/ve-env.sh` clears `MINDMAKE_ARCHIVE_ROOT`: set it again or pass `--archive`) (0; refuses a cut that is not READY, a cut without the bed-shape receipt `ve qc` writes for its bytes, a file changed since approval, or anything already there). It copies each cut into its post's `3 Posts` folder; `ve status` then prints `delivered: DONE <path>` and exits 0 only when that copy reads back (F27). Tell Krish "Done" with the link. Before the cut goes on its making record (`making.py video`, which warns on a cut that leaves out a clip Krish linked to the piece and asked to keep the sound of: `making.py <piece> clips`) or into the library (`send.py`), write its validation receipt: `ve receipt <cut.mp4> --piece <piece> --cut <cut> --piece-id <making id> --library-path '3 Posts/...' --script <signed script> --voice-master <dialogue.wav> [--clarity <dir>]` (0 ready; 1 names what holds it). Its opening check measures a stinged cut from the frame after the sting (`opening.lead_frames`), and delivery holds a result measured from any other frame. It hears the cut and the voice master with the same transcriber (Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-31), binds QC, the Critic verdict, his final approval and the words gate to these bytes, and reads his rulings from `<piece>/ve/rulings.<cut>.json` (heard_as, removals, visual_clarity, each in his words). Sync and motion stand as not applicable only when the props hold no picture segments (Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-31). Always `ve time <piece> stop`, even on failure, then `ve ledger <piece>`; retain actual totals. Filing needs an approved route. Never claim unavailable delivery; never publish/send/schedule.
