## 1. Vertical rhythm: groups touch
Headlines butt against the line under them: the landing's insight runs straight into its italic question; the good-news cover's date touches the headline; the good-news item's Fraunces line touches its Anton headline; the cover's headline touches V4. Set one spacing scale, measured ink to ink, scaled from 1080 px wide:
- within a group (label to headline, headline line to line): the font's own leading;
- headline to its dek or date: 40 px;
- between groups (a text group and a logo, a wordmark or a CTA): 64 px;
- V4 under a headline: the existing clearance (half the fitted cap height), never less.

## 2. Fill by anchoring, not by parking at the top
Many tall zones put everything at the top and leave the rest empty: the Story hook and proof, the statement close, the meme tall and meme video, the chart plates, the document plate's colour block, the title frame. In any zone taller than its content, anchor the primary group (label and headline) to the zone's top and the secondary group (dek, date, source, CTA or wordmark) to the zone's bottom safe line, so the layout fills the zone the way a poster does. The bottom safe line is the top of the caption band on video, the platform's control line on Stories, Reels and TikTok, and the frame's safe margin elsewhere.

## 3. Show the platform as it will be seen (sheets and sequences only)
On every vertical video and Story preview on a sheet, draw a quiet ghost of the platform's own interface at 20% cream: the Reels and TikTok right-hand action column and caption lines at the bottom, the Story reply bar and the link-sticker position. Then the reserved space reads as reserved. Never in an item export.

## 4. Captions
- The house caption is Fraunces 600 at 72 px per 1080 px of width on vertical, 64 px on 1920 wide, sentence case, two lines at most, the current word in mint.
- On a photo or on ink: cream, over a soft burn that is a full-width horizontal band feathered top and bottom (at most 60% ink at its centre), never a blob hugging the words. The blob reads as a stain.
- On a colour block: ink text, no burn; the current word in ink at weight 800, the rest at 600.
- video-vertical-captions and video-wide-captions are the caption templates: their exports must show the captions.
