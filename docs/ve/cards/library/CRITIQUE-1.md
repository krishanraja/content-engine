# Creative-director critique of Library v5 round 3 (25 Nov 2026)

Read at true size from the host renders of the 122 items and the eleven family sheets. The Library is mechanically sound: the grid, the fitter, the one-wordmark count and the safe zones all hold. As design it is not ready to show. These are the defects, family by family, and the fix each one needs. Krish's standards that each defect breaks are cited by their floor number in brackets.

## What is wrong across the whole set

1. **Dead space everywhere.** The vertical evidence plates leave their bottom 40% empty cream. The step cards leave the bottom third of each colour block empty. Every cover and story pushes the photo into the top quarter and leaves a slab of empty ink under the headline. The closing cards still split into a mint top and a dead ink bottom with a small S3h in the corner, which is the exact card Krish called "ridiculous". (87, 94, 258: never leave dead space; fill the frame deliberately.)
2. **Type is too small for the canvas.** Captions are a 40 px Fraunces line on a 1080 px frame. Lower thirds, callout tags, strength tags and source lines are near illegible at phone size. The YouTube thumbnail fails at 168 px. (12: captions big, bold and engaging; 259: evidence legible.)
3. **The photos are crushed.** The burn eats 60 to 70% of every photo, so the operating-table world reads as a black rectangle with a lamp. Burn only the area that carries type.
4. **Homogeneous and joyless.** Almost every social item is the same dark photo with the same cream headline. Krish on 24 Nov: "we've lost some element of joy ... I didn't want to make this completely homogenuos". The climb colours appear only on step cards. Each frame should use one colour family, and use it big.
5. **No real faces or footage.** The hero video is Krish on camera, and the Library has no talking-head frame, no split screen, no face band and no headshot thumbnail. (122: thumbnails are never just a face on black; 254: the face is never obstructed; 150: the face band is never see-through.)
6. **No sound.** There is no intro sting, outro sequence or music bed spec, although every cut must carry a licensed bed (130, 155) and a colourful sting that moves from frame one (201, 249).
7. **Motion is described, never shown.** Every video item carries a one-line motion note. Nothing plays.
8. **Stretched photo edges.** Where an aperture is wider than the photo, the code stretches the edge pixels (newsletter third piece, wide place card). That is visible and must go: crop to the focal box, or change the aperture.
9. **Placeholders read as dead copy.** "[What changed for the better]" in Fraunces at headline size looks like a broken card. Off-day examples need a real, sourced example or a smaller, clearly marked slot label.

## Family by family

### Video, vertical and wide
- **Title.** Headline sits mid-frame with a tiny V4 under it; the photo is a dark band. Fix: photo fills the frame with a light burn at the bottom, headline larger, no wordmark (one mark per video, on the close).
- **Why 1 to 3.** Good bones. The question sits at the top of the block and leaves 35% of the block empty. Fix: question set larger and centred in the block's vertical space, with the caption band reserved below it.
- **Why 2.** The white document box hangs off the top with a large white void under the text. Fix: crop the document to its text and place it on ink at legible size; never pad a document with blank paper.
- **Evidence plates (document, chart, place, creative work, quote, documented).** Bottom 40% empty cream; note and source lines small. Fix: the evidence fills the top 55 to 60%, the note in Anton fills its zone, the strength tag at a legible size, and the caption band is reserved, not empty.
- **Callout.** The leader runs into the tag box and the tag text crowds its top-left corner. Fix: the leader ends on the box edge; the tag is DM Mono at a legible size with even padding.
- **Challenger.** A small dashed box floating mid-photo. Fix: a full-width parked panel on ink with the claim in Fraunces italic and the OPEN tag, photo above it.
- **Lower third.** A tiny logo chip and a DM Mono line; illegible on a phone. Fix: real logo at legible size, source in DM Mono at a minimum of 28 px per 1080 px, on a solid ink plate that clears the caption band.
- **Captions.** "at 30 seconds" at about 40 px. Fix: house captions at a minimum of 64 px per 1080 px width, Fraunces 600, current word in mint, in a fixed caption band clear of platform UI.
- **Rail.** A black strip with four tiny labels. Fix: the rail as a thin progress line along the frame edge with the current rung in its colour; labels only where legible.
- **Recap.** Fine on vertical. On wide, the rows are cramped. Fix: two-column layout on wide, evidence grid left and ruled list right.
- **Landing.** Lots of empty mint below the insight. Fix: the photo above, the mint block sized to its words, insight set larger.
- **Close.** The card Krish rejected, unchanged in its problem. Fix: rebuild from scratch (below).
- **Missing:** talking head full frame, split screen (evidence over face), face band, wide side-by-side, intro sting, outro sequence, chapter card for the 5-minute cut, sponsor or partner read, and the end screen as a moving sequence.

### Closing card, rebuilt
One card, no dead area. Ink with grain, full bleed. S3h set large, filling the width of the content box. "Follow for more" in Anton or Fraunces at a size that reads at arm's length. The subscribe route in DM Mono under it. In video it is the last frame of a six-second outro: the coral, butter and lilac blocks push up in turn (the climb), mint lands with the takeaway, then the ink card holds for two seconds (72, 165, 168, 208). The takeaway lives on its own mint landing card before it, never split on one card.

### Carousel
- **Cover.** Photo cropped to a band at the top; dead ink below. Fix: photo full bleed with the burn only where the headline sits.
- **Why cards.** Good, but carry the same empty-block problem as video.
- **Document card.** The source line sits on the card; Krish wants carousel sources in the post description (186). Fix: drop the source line from carousel cards, keep the logo crop.
- **Close.** Same rejected split. Rebuild as above.
- **Good news and simple advice.** Cover, item and close only, with dead placeholders. Fix: real example content; an item card where the photo or logo leads; a numbered list card for simple advice that is not top-left numbered (280); text centred on story cards only where 198 applies.

### Social
- **Story, Reels and TikTok covers.** Photo in the top quarter, 40% dead ink. The Reels cover ignores the 3:4 grid crop Instagram shows on the profile. Fix: full-frame photo, the headline inside the 3:4 grid-safe centre, platform UI zones respected.
- **YouTube thumbnail.** Fails at 168 px. Fix: three to five words, Anton at least 150 px tall on 1280 x 720, a real logo, a headshot variant and a no-face variant (143: a wide and a vertical thumbnail with every pack).
- **Meme.** It is a feed post, not a meme. Fix: a real meme format: a real photo or screenshot, a plain caption, and only a small wordmark bottom-right under the picture (227, 37).
- **Threads/X text card.** Same as the feed post. Fix: a text-led card, one sentence in Fraunces large on cream or a climb colour, with the source.
- **Quote, number, versus, poll.** Workable; tighten spacing and make numbers huge.

### Profile
- **Avatars.** Strong. Keep.
- **Banners.** A tiny S3h in a large ink field over four flat steps. Fix: S3h large on the left inside the all-device safe area, the four climb steps rising from bottom right, nothing small.
- **End screen.** Cramped mint and ink stripes. Fix: rebuild on ink with the platform's element zones honoured and S3h placed clear of them.
- **Highlight covers.** Fine; check the photo fills the circle crop.

### Podcast
- **Show art.** Strong. Keep.
- **Episode art.** S3h plus the question crowd each other and the question is illegible at 56 px. Fix: one of the two leads: either the question in Anton large with D small, or S3h alone with a number. Test at 56 px.
- **Audiogram.** Waveform and quote are small on a large dark photo. Fix: quote in Fraunces large, waveform full width, real excerpt length.

### Newsletter
- **Weekly edition.** Right structure. The third piece's photo is stretched. Fix: true crops, larger summaries, a real "what else happened" example from a verified run.
- **Email header.** Mint bars above and below read as a leftover border. Fix: ink header with V9 alone.

### Web, email and future
- **Desktop cover.** Content small in a large page with an empty bottom. Fix: compose to the 1440 x 900 fold.
- **Article.** Large gap between dek and body. Fix: tighten.
- **OG card.** Coral is a rung colour, not a cover colour. Fix: ink with the photo, or mint only when it carries an answer.
- **Future.** Workable as a starting set. The press kit crowds; the event card mixes three type sizes in one block.
