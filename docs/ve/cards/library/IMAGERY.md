## The direction (26 Nov, replaces the dark grade)
- **Bright, clean and now.** Contemporary places and objects in good light: daylight, clean studio light, or a vivid colour light from the palette. Never decay, dust, ruins, abandoned rooms, worn-out vintage kit, horror or "haunted" moods. Old things (records, jukeboxes, TVs) appear only in good condition and good light.
- **Colour you can see at thumbnail size.** Each image carries one strong palette colour (coral #FF6A4D, butter #FFD84D, lilac #B7A6FF or mint #7EF0C0) as real light, a backdrop or the hero object, so the feed is colourful.
- **One encapsulating hero visual per cover.** The image is the single punchy object or moment that sums up the story at a glance (for "A fan's AI song. Who gets paid?": a gold record split three ways, or a phone playing a song with three hands reaching for the coins). It is big, sharp and readable at 160 px wide. A lone company logo is never the visual.
- **Whole heads, with headroom.** Never crop through a face or a head. Generate each image in the shape of the slot it fills, with the people whole inside the slot's bright area.
- **Faces beside objects.** On face-led covers his approved headshot sits beside the hero visual (the face plus object pattern), on the series colour.
- The cover standard still binds: the headline leads, at most three parts besides it, bright and high-contrast over dark and muddy.

## Templates aspire; sessions generate (Krish, 26 Nov 2026)
Krish: "I think that the goal is to have aspirational templates but then leave the actual generation to the session so it generates a high-quality image that we know looks and feels like the template aspiration. I don't think we need to generate hordes and hordes of imagery to just keep on file, just in case we'll use them one day."

- **No stock bank.** We do not generate imagery to keep on file. Each image slot in a template carries an aspiration: one exemplar image that shows the bar, plus the slot's style, prompt shape, framing and aspect.
- **Each piece generates its own image** at production time, in the session making that piece, to match the exemplar's look and feel. It is checked against the exemplar and these rules before Krish sees it, and its prompt, model, date and cost go in the piece's record.
- **Exemplars are few:** one per image role (hero visual, story scene, face-and-object), made once, kept only as the reference for that template.

## What the current covers that work have in common (read from @makeyourmind.up, 25 Nov)
- **A scene that acts out the headline, with wit.** A crocodile crawling under an office desk; a cat flying a plane with an "AI" badge; a man recoiling as hands shove a vacuum, a card and a mattress at him; a robot being told off at a desk. One idea, true to that story, readable in a second.

- **A two-tone Anton headline.** Cream, then the payoff line in mint. One face.
- **The small wordmark bottom-right.**
- **Inside slides are dense and useful:** a slide counter, the logos of the tools it works in, a large real screenshot, the prompt quoted in mint, numbered steps.

## The five house styles
Every generated image is one of these. Each post picks one style for its cover and may use a second for inside slides.

| Style | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Story scene | A cinematic, photoreal moment that acts out the headline with dry wit: a fictional person or an animal in a real workplace or creative setting (studio, stage, newsroom, stadium tunnel, gallery, office) | Covers, thumbnails, the hook |
| 2. Operating table | Real objects laid out and dissected on teal cloth under a surgical lamp | Why cards and step cards: taking a claim apart |
| 3. Analogue world | Records, jukeboxes, old TVs, timers, tape, in real rooms in daylight or lamp light | Context, landings, closes, the colour-bar motif's world |
| 4. Screen composite | A dark, softly lit desk or room as the ground, built to hold real interface screenshots floated on top | How-to and tools carousels, evidence slides |
| 5. Sector sets | Empty but lived-in places in the six industries: a recording booth, a film set between takes, a dressing room, an empty stadium at night, a gallery after hours, a print room | Off-day posts, stock bank, backgrounds |

## Rules for every generated image
1. **Acts out this story.** The image makes this headline's point; never a generic "AI" image, glowing brain, robot hand or circuit board.
2. **No text, no logos, no interface inside the image.** Real logos and screenshots are added as layers on top, unaltered.
3. **No real people's likeness.** Generated people are fictional, adults, and never resemble a real person; Krish appears only through his approved headshots. Never the felt robot (retired by the v5 lock).
4. **Composed for the type.** The subject sits clear of the headline zone of the template it is made for, inside the 3:4 grid crop on tall covers; nothing important in the platform's control zones. Where type sits on the image, the template adds its own colour field or burn; the image itself stays bright.
5. **One grade.** Bright, clean, contemporary light with true colour; one strong palette colour (coral, butter, lilac or mint) as light, backdrop or object. Slight film grain at most. No low-key gloom.
6. **Sharp at size.** Generate at the target aspect (4:5 for carousels and feed, 9:16 for Stories and Reels covers, 16:9 for thumbnails) at 2k, and reject anything soft, mangled (hands, faces, objects) or uncanny.
7. **Fresh for hero pieces.** Each Monday, Wednesday and Friday piece gets its own new images (Krish's standard: never reuse a previous piece's assets). The stock bank serves off-day posts, fallbacks and backgrounds.
8. **Reported events.** Generated images illustrate an idea. A reported event uses a credited real photograph.
9. **Recorded.** Each image's prompt, model, date, style number and credit cost go in the piece's record, so it can be traced and never mistaken for a real photograph of a real event.

**Hero visual (covers and thumbnails):** "A bright, crisp photoreal product-style still of [the one object or moment that sums up the story], centred and large, on a clean [coral | butter | lilac | mint] backdrop, soft studio light with a clear shadow, modern and new, high contrast, sharp edges that read at thumbnail size. No text, no logos, no screens with readable text. [Aspect]."

**Story scene (carousel covers, inside slides):** "A bright, contemporary photoreal still: [the one moment that acts out the headline, with dry wit]. [Modern setting] in clean daylight or vivid [palette colour] light, true colour, everything in good condition, the subject in the upper two thirds. No text, no logos, no readable screens. [Aspect]."
