# The standard every makeyourmindup frame must meet (25 Nov 2026)

The bar is the full look Krish locked on 25 Nov (`locked-full-look-where-it-goes.jpg`, and `.scratch/full-look.png`): colour in every grid, one tight Anton headline per card, the real document, photos burning into ink. Krish confirmed that bar and flagged two of its own frames as unacceptable: the closing video frame (a mint takeaway over a thin ink band with "Follow for more" and a small wordmark crammed bottom-left, the rest dead) and a tiny "for Artists" logo chip floating on a dark photo. His words: "your standards need to be tighter". A frame fails if it breaks any line below. There is no partial pass.

## Type
1. **One face per headline.** A headline is one typeface, never a sentence split across Anton, Fraunces and DM Mono.
2. **Anton headlines are tight:** line height 0.90 to 0.95 of the font size, no added tracking, at most three lines, sized so the block spans at least 70% of the content width.
3. **Supporting text** is one Fraunces line or short paragraph at 36 to 44 px per 1080 px of width. Labels are DM Mono at 26 to 30 px. Nothing a reader must read is smaller than 33 px per 1080 px, except the site line.
4. **No floating lines.** Inside a headline, lines touch their leading; between groups use the scale (40 px headline to its line, 64 px between groups).

## Marks and logos
5. **No floating chips.** A company logo is never a small chip dropped on a photo. It sits on a solid field (ink, cream or the card's colour), at least 56 px tall per 1080 px of width, on the type's left edge, as part of the type group, or it is left off the card.
6. **One wordmark,** on ink, sized as a deliberate element of the composition: V4 at least 40% of the content width under a feed headline; S3h at least 60% of the content width on a close.

## Composition
7. **No crammed corners and no dead bands.** No element is pushed into a corner with empty field beside it. No band thinner than 20% of the frame carries type. Every field the eye lands on is either content or a deliberate colour field balanced by the type.
8. **One composition per frame.** A close is one composition: S3h large, "Follow for more" set as a line of the group, the site under it, centred in the safe area. The takeaway belongs on its own landing frame, never squeezed above the close.
9. **One edge.** Every element aligns by its visible ink to one left edge (2 px tolerance), or the frame is a single centred lockup by design.

## Pictures and evidence
10. **Relevant pictures only,** sharp at their displayed size; the burn is the only gradient; on carousels the photo burns into the card's colour.
11. **The real document,** cropped to the lines that matter and legible at phone size. Never retyped, never boxed in a system font.

## Colour
12. **Colour carries the system.** Any nine consecutive feed posts include the climb colours and mint as full-colour cards, as in the locked grid. The homepage carries colour the same way: never an all-ink page with one mint button.

## The system: the poster and the evidence (26 Nov 2026)
Chosen by blind judging after Krish rejected generic photoreal people ("They are all huge failures in so many ways"), old-school music artefacts, dark imagery, a card on his face and a floating head. Three independent directions (evidence, poster, film still) were built blind to each other and judged blind by two reviewers against his full rejection history. A third, re-shuffled reviewer then picked the synthesis over both originals. The film-still direction (generated people) ranked last with every judge.
- **Type is the picture.** Giant Anton set edge to edge on flat house colours, two-tone where the payoff is the answer (mint).
- **The colour bars are the data.** One bar is one second: the 1985 intro as twenty bars against 2015's five, a 30-bar play meter with the 30th second lit in mint and labelled "0:30 = counted", and the key number striped coral, butter, lilac and mint. Never a tilted or decorative block.
- **One piece of real evidence per frame:** a straight cream card cut from the real source to one sentence, with the words that matter marked in mint. Never a wall of document, never a word clipped at an edge.
- **Why cards:** the rung colour full bleed, the giant "WHY n", his question, and one data device (timeline, meter or source card), with the play meter filling the foot.
- **Covers:** four families. The data cover (the striped number, at most 2 words, the source card) leads the brand accounts and YouTube. Receipt (his headline, the named companies with one verdict chip each, and the colour bars as the old-TV test card) is the face-free alternative. Face (his approved headshot cut out on the series colour, at most 4 words, nothing on his face) is for his own accounts and the YouTube A/B arm. Carousel covers (his headline, the mint payoff, the data, the wordmark) are the fourth.
- **People:** only Krish's own approved headshot. No generated or stock people anywhere in the system.
- **Pictures:** where a template has a picture slot, the piece supplies the specific real thing in the story (a real place, product, screen or document), never a mood shot.
- **Words are his:** at most 4 words beside a YouTube title and at most 7 elsewhere, in his shapes (TITLES.md).
- **Safe zones:** on 9:16, words and logos sit inside x 120 to 960 and y 330 to 1180. Data devices may run below, where captions and buttons sit, because they carry meaning without being critical.
- **Verdict chips stay** (his own device). Proof tags and series pills stay off.

## The test before anyone sees it
13. Look at the frame at true size and at phone size (a 1080 px card at 360 px wide). If any line above fails, or if it would look like a slide, a template or a stock social post, it is not shown.

14. An approved template is locked. A revision brief must name its exact ID before it can change. Record the brief when unlocking, then lock the new render after Krish approves it. Run the lock check every round. See [LOCKING.md](LOCKING.md).

## What Krish likes, to build on
- **The colour bars.** Of the locked banner (coral, butter, lilac and mint bars rising like steps on ink), Krish, 25 Nov: "I do enjoy this, it looks like an old TV which goes nicely with the records and jukeboxes". Treat the bars as a house motif from the same analogue world as the records and jukeboxes: use them where a frame needs colour without a photo (banners, the homepage, the close, chapter and transition frames), always flat with grain, in climb order, never as decoration on a frame that already has a colour block. The line on that banner, "WE TAKE AI CLAIMS APART, ONE WHY AT A TIME.", is retired; the bars are not.
- **No icon beside its own wordmark.** Krish, 25 Nov, on the homepage: "I dont think we need the icon separately where the wordmark exists if it has the icon inside it." Where S3h (which carries the D as the O in YOUR) is on a frame or page, the separate D icon does not appear on that frame or page.
