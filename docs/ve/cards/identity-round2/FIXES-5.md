## 1. S3g show art: a ghost D that reads as the icon
- Draw the large D behind the type in its own colours, held back:
  - the cream half in cream at 16% opacity on the ink;
  - the mint half in mint at 20% opacity;
  - the computed fills are about #2D332F and #23412F (use the exact blends).
- Size it at about 85% of the tile, centred slightly right, so the off-register gap between the halves shows through between the letters.
- The cream type and mint UP stay on top at full strength.
- Check that the cream type keeps at least 7:1 against the lightest ghost fill. Record the ratios in NOTES.md.

## 2. Avatar: a ghost name behind D

- Inside the clip, set the three S3g lines (MAKE / YOUR / MIND/UP) huge behind the D:

  - UP in mint at 20%;
  - cropped by the circle edge;
  - the letters large enough that MAKE and YOUR are recognisable at 110 px.
- The cream and mint D sits on top at full strength, at its current size.
- Nothing spills outside the circle.
