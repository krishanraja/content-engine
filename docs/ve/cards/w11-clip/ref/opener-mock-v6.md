# The approved opener mock, as the clip device must reproduce it

Krish saw six mocks of Wednesday 11 Nov's opening on 9 Nov 2026 and shaped this one (the mp4 is media and is not in
the repository; it is `wed11-opener-mock-v6.mp4`, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 50 s, made from his short take). The timing below
is the short cut's own clock; the long cut follows the same plan on its own words.

| Time | Picture | What the clip device does |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 3.4 s | the wedding-toast clip, full frame, its own sound | `clip` on a STAGE beat, `full: true`, entered with `toState: "cut"` on frame 0 |
| 3.4 to 3.8 s | his intro: the clip rises into the top half while his picture slides up into the bottom half | the SPLIT layout; the clip now fills the top slot with a channel-colour `edge` along its bottom |
| 17.75 to 19.6 s | the episode card on ink (HUMAN MUSIC / OR AI MUSIC?) | the clip has exited (`cut`) |
| 29.3 to 29.75 s | the cat clip drops in from the top while his picture moves down to the bottom half, one matched move | `clip` enters (13 frames, every frame) |
| 41.5 to 41.95 s | the cat clip slides up and out while he rises to full frame, ending on a beat of the cat song | `clip` exits (13 frames, every frame) |

second copy of him showed at another size while the split dissolved, and the move was stepped on twos.
