## follow_the_money

> Work out where the money moves and who ends up better or worse off, one Why at a time. That is the standing question and it leads every piece. THE LADDER: Why 1 asks who pays, and why the price moved. Each Why is answered from evidence on two domains, a filing, a contract, a pricing page, a royalty split, or it is marked as an open question and the ladder stops there; a Why is never climbed past without its evidence. Each rung joins the last with "because" or "but", never "and then", and ends on the next question. Three to seven rungs, five as the target, never padded. The channel covers the whole money system, from any side of the invoice: what a business can charge for AI-powered work, what AI actually costs to run and whether it pays back, how the labs themselves make money, and when it is worth owning the thing rather than renting it. A piece that traces one chain end to end is the best version of this channel. THE READER is anyone standing at the invoice, on either side of it: the leader of a five to fifty million pound business who has just seen the renewal quote, the operator setting a price for work a model now does, and the founder deciding whether to build the thing or keep renting it. SUBJECTS are giants, and after that whoever leaves the best paper trail. Product surfaces are evidence for the money question and are never a follow.the.money subject in their own right. THE BOUNDARY, and it decides every contested subject: what does the reader change next? If they would go and change a price, a budget or a contract, it is follow.the.money. If they would change what they build or buy, it is under.the.hood. If they would change how they think or what they expect, it is mind.the.gap. The question decides, never the surface. NAMING: name whoever the number came from. A public decision by a named executive is fair game; their character is not. THE LANDING: what the ladder shows about who ends up better or worse off, and the strongest version names something already fixed that has not yet shown up in the price (a lease, a depreciation schedule, a liability cap), then hands the reader one open question. Never a forecast, a date to check, odds or a confidence number. Diagram language: the money map, where the dollars enter and where they leave, one rung at a time. HARD GATES, applied before scoring. NOT US: the subject is never Krish, mind/make, CTRL or his own accounts, however available the material. MATERIAL EXISTS: if the evidence has to be created rather than found, it is not commissioned, however good the idea.

## under_the_hood

> Work out what actually goes together, and why this one worked, one Why at a time. Take a shipped thing apart to show what is really in it. THE LADDER: Why 1 asks how it is built, and what broke. Each Why is answered from evidence on two domains, a changelog, the docs, a pricing page, a release note, an error state, or it is marked as an open question and the ladder stops there; a Why is never climbed past without its evidence. Each rung joins the last with "because" or "but", never "and then", and ends on the next question. Three to seven rungs, five as the target, never padded. THE STANDING QUESTION, and the one the opening artifact asks the reader: what would you build differently having seen inside this? Second question, and it decides the harder cases: does this make its user sharper, or dependent? SUBJECTS: the stack and the surface together. Which pieces go together and which quietly do not; the architectures behind builds that are working and the reason they are; what actually ships against what was demoed; and the gap between what a keynote claimed and what a normal account can reach on day one. Big-company launches first, because the distribution makes the stakes legible; after that, whoever leaves the best paper trail. THE READER is choosing what to build or buy: someone with a team, a budget and a decision in front of them about which pieces to put together. THE BOUNDARY, and it decides every contested subject: what does the reader change next? If they would change what they build or buy, it is under.the.hood. If they would change a price, a budget or a contract, it is follow.the.money. If they would change how they think or what they expect, it is mind.the.gap. The question decides, never the surface. Diagram language: the mechanism diagram, with Real or Theatre as the annotation on the evidence it judges. THE LANDING: what is really inside, said as the ladder found it, then one open question handed to the reader. Never a forecast, a date to check, odds or a confidence number. CADENCE: standing subchannel with no fixed day. It publishes when a subject earns it. HARD GATES, applied before scoring. NOT US: the subject is never Krish, mind/make, CTRL or his own builds, however available the material. MATERIAL EXISTS: if the evidence has to be created rather than found, it is not commissioned, however good the idea. A product that must be bought, installed and run to be inspected fails this gate; a product whose changelog, docs and pricing page are public passes it. TIMESTAMP EVERYTHING: pricing pages and changelogs change, so archive the surface before recording or the piece rots in a week. NO PREACHING: no closing moral, no lesson for leaders, no sentence telling the reader what to conclude. The conclusion is the reader's to reach.

## mind_the_gap

> Notice the pattern, and trace why it is there, one Why at a time. Take a shift that is visible across several threads and resolve it into one observation the reader has half-noticed and never named. THE LADDER: Why 1 asks where the claim and the reality part. Each Why is answered from evidence on two domains, or it is marked as an open question and the ladder stops there; a Why is never climbed past without its evidence. Each rung joins the last with "because" or "but", never "and then", and ends on the next question. Three to seven rungs, five as the target, never padded. When the evidence splits, follow the better-evidenced branch and park the other; when a Why lands on a person, ask how it made sense to them, never who is to blame; when a Why leaves the subject, stop and name who owns that question. The gap is any of three, and the piece says which one it is working: between what is claimed and what is actually happening; between what people believe and what the evidence shows; or no gap at all, just a pattern worth naming and what it shows. SEVERAL THREADS, ONE SPINE. A piece may and should draw on several subjects, shifts or themes. What it may not do is survey them: they resolve into one ladder with one spine, or it is a roundup. Covers thinking, usage, habit, technology and application; public decisions and what they say about the wider picture; historical shifts; and the human stories underneath the technology. The question the opening artifact asks the reader: what is the pattern here, and why is it there? THE BOUNDARY, and it decides every contested subject: what does the reader change next? If they would change how they think or what they expect, it is mind.the.gap. If they would change a price, a budget or a contract, it is follow.the.money. If they would change what they build or buy, it is under.the.hood. The question decides, never the surface. Diagram language: two columns, belief against evidence, and the pattern timeline. Register is human, observant and connected to the real world. Strongest subjects invert a well-covered story rather than restating it. THE LANDING: the pattern, said as the ladder found it, then one open question handed to the reader. Never a forecast, a date to check, odds or a confidence number, and never what is coming next. HARD RULE, and the one that overrides every other instruction in this mandate: no preaching. No closing moral, no lesson for leaders, no sentence telling the reader what to conclude or become. The not-us gate applies here too: the subject is never Krish, mind/make, CTRL or his own operating loop, however available the material.
