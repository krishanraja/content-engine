# Four-week pilot

The first eight approved Shorts establish a balanced baseline:

| Week | The Money of AI | Built With AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Extracted | Short-native |
| 2 | Short-native | Extracted |
| 3 | Extracted with one controlled change | Short-native with one controlled change |
| 4 | Short-native with one controlled change | Extracted with one controlled change |

This yields two observations in every series and source-mode cell. It is a baseline, not enough evidence to activate a performance rule. Eligibility still requires three comparable directional tests.

For every video:

1. Approve angle, claims, hook, and exact candidate hash.
2. Approve a new treatment manifest, or use a treatment already approved in versioned config.
3. Approve the final master hash and platform package.
4. Re-import any post-engine edit before attaching analytics.
5. Record one primary experiment variable and unavoidable confounds.

Import platform CSVs without filling unavailable fields. Review distribution, resonance, and qualification together. Views alone never justify a creative rule. Qualified actions include relevant inbound responses and Mindmaker Live UTM actions; keep their source notes alongside the CSV.

Calibration precedes the pilot: produce three low-resolution solo treatment manifests and two conversation treatment manifests, compare them pairwise in Codex, then render no more than two full variants unless testing a genuinely new treatment.
