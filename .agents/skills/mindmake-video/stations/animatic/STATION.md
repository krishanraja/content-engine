# Animatic station

## Responsibility
Test whether the visual argument works in time before full rendering. Own timing and attention flow, not final polish.

## Inputs
Use approved styleframes, transcript timing, exact assets and the visual plan's beat boundaries.

## Outputs
Produce a low-resolution timed review artifact with readable proof, caption and presenter transitions.

## Quality gates
Hard-block meaning loss, rushed evidence, asset drift and unsafe overlaps. Soft-block monotonous pacing, excessive device density and transitions that call attention to themselves.

## Failure and fallback
Return to the exact failed frame or beat. Do not solve a structural problem with more motion.

## Handoff
Treatment compiles only an approved animatic lineage into platform manifests.

## Learning boundary
Record timing corrections as observations at the narrowest scope. Do not infer a universal pacing rule from one video.

## Change control
Timing heuristics require comparable examples, regression cases and a version bump.
