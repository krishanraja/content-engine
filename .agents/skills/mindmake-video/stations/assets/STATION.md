# Assets station

## Responsibility
Acquire, classify and approve exact visual material. Own provenance and asset quality, not beat timing or final composition.

## Inputs
Use the visual plan, source shortlist, approved owned artifacts, rights information and brand asset registry.

## Outputs
Produce exact asset files, an asset ledger and an approval packet containing the pixels that may enter production.

## Quality gates
Hard-block unknown rights, hash drift, privacy exposure, generated evidence and fake wordmarks. Soft-block bland headlines, stale proof, weak authority and assets that will not read on a phone.

## Failure and fallback
Return to the approved source shortlist or use an explicitly approved presenter-only plan. Never fill the gap with decoration.

## Handoff
Styleframes can use only assets whose exact hashes and truth roles appear in this station's artifact.

## Learning boundary
Source quality feedback may inform future shortlists. It cannot turn a specific approved screenshot into a durable global preference.

## Change control
Rights classes, source tiers or brand assets require new fixtures, exact hashes and a version bump.
