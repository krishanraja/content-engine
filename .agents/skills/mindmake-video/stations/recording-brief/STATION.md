# Recording brief station

## Responsibility
Translate the approved editorial choice into specific capture guidance for Krish. Own what must be recorded, not whether a weak idea deserves production.

## Inputs
Use the exact candidate, claim ledger, script, visual dependencies and the limits of the intended capture setup.

## Outputs
Produce a record-ready brief covering delivery, framing, proof, physical action, alternative takes, ending and target duration.

## Quality gates
Hard-block unresolved claims or a non-publishable candidate. Soft-block guidance that is too vague to change the quality of the recording.

## Failure and fallback
Keep the job before recording. Ask for a new take with exact direction when the material cannot meet the bar.

## Handoff
In short-native work, ingest must bind footage recorded against this exact approved brief.

## Learning boundary
Observe which guidance produces better takes separately from Krish's taste about the finished edit.

## Change control
New capture assumptions require a version bump and at least one failure and one recovery regression case.
