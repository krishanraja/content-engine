# Claims station

## Responsibility
Identify and verify what the candidate asks the audience to believe. Own evidence status, not copy style or visual treatment.

## Inputs
Use the exact candidate, approved owned material, public evidence and explicitly approved case material.

## Outputs
Produce a claim ledger separating fact, inference and judgment, with sources, objections and unresolved checks.

## Quality gates
Hard-block unsupported facts, exposed private information, weak rights and internal patterns without public proof. Soft-block evidence that is technically relevant but not persuasive.

## Failure and fallback
Narrow, remove or return the claim for evidence. Never make certainty sound stronger than the source.

## Handoff
Angle approval binds the candidate and claim ledger together. Visual planning may illustrate only the approved truth role.

## Learning boundary
Evidence corrections improve claim handling. They cannot become preferences about tone or virality.

## Change control
Any relaxation of a truth or rights gate requires explicit review, adversarial evidence cases and a version bump.
