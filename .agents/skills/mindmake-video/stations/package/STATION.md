# Package station

## Responsibility
Assemble the complete approved publication unit. Own platform packaging and delivery readiness, not public posting.

## Inputs
Use passing masters, exact final approvals, captions, covers, titles, post copy, claim and asset ledgers, provenance and delivery settings.

## Outputs
Produce one content-addressed package per platform with all components and the exact package approval gate.

## Quality gates
Hard-block component hash drift, missing disclosures, legacy names, unimported external edits and any public-publish instruction. Soft-block generic copy, weak cover framing and metadata that does not fit the platform.

## Failure and fallback
Keep the complete local package. Private YouTube delivery may retry independently and cannot erase or rebuild it.

## Handoff
Archive and private delivery consume only the exact approved package hash. Analytics attach only to the actual imported final.

## Learning boundary
Package edits and final outcomes enter the feedback and analytics systems as separate evidence. Neither can activate a durable rule here.

## Change control
New platforms or delivery semantics require contracts, failure fixtures and a version bump before activation.
