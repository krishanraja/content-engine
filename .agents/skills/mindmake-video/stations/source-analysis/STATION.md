# Source analysis station

## Responsibility
Describe the visual source as measurements. Own tracks, cuts, gestures, gaze, speakers and safe regions, not the story.

## Inputs
Use normalized media, the encrypted Krish identity capability and job-local guest consent references.

## Outputs
Produce a source analysis artifact with confidence, capability downgrades and conservative fallbacks.

## Quality gates
Hard-block guessed identity and hidden capability failure. Soft-block unstable tracks that cannot support confident camera motion.

## Failure and fallback
Label uncertainty and use static framing. Do not convert low confidence into false precision.

## Handoff
Visual planning combines these measurements with the candidate and claims. Measurements never choose the editorial emphasis alone.

## Learning boundary
Detection failures may improve technical fixtures. Performance or taste cannot relabel identity evidence.

## Change control
New detection capabilities require confidence thresholds, negative fixtures and a version bump.
