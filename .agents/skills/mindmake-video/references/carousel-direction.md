# Carousel visual direction

## Boundary

The carousel route begins with a completed content idea whose argument, claims, sequence, audience payoff and ending are already set. It produces the visual expression of that material. It does not decide what Krish should discuss, record or believe.

The director may challenge visualizability, request evidence or flag ambiguity. If the missing input would require a new topic, argument, claim, payoff or recording brief, return it to the content owner. Do not fill the gap by inventing content.

Load and validate `config/carousel-visual-direction.json` before visual work. Use its default standard mode unless Krish explicitly selects fast or exploratory.

## Standard collaboration

1. **Meaning map.** Translate the locked content into semantic beats. For each beat, state the audience task, factual payload, emotional job, proof need and visual opportunity. Ask Krish to confirm meaning, not aesthetics.
2. **Visual casting.** Present concrete image ingredients across real-world evidence, infographic, physical analogy, cultural restage, handmade and surreal wildcard lanes. Explain the exact semantic mapping, comprehension risk, rights path and production burden. Draft first so Krish can select, mix, reject or contribute an association.
3. **Territory audition.** Show three materially different two-frame auditions using the same locked content: evidence-led, explanatory and unexpected. Each audition includes the cover and the decisive proof or reveal frame. Do not present superficial style variants as different territories.
4. **Storyboard and asset gate.** Expand the selected territory into one low-resolution complete contact sheet. Then present every exact screenshot, photograph, illustration, meme restage, sketch or generated element for hash-bound approval with source, rights and truth role.
5. **Final.** Produce one polished candidate. Produce a second only when one material visual decision remains unresolved.

Standard mode has four human decision points: meaning; combined casting and territory selection; storyboard and assets; final. Fast mode combines the early work into a visual-route decision and final approval. Exploratory mode separates casting from territory selection.

Keep one screen and one decision at each review. When Krish responds, record both the choice and the reason. Allow ingredient-level mixing, such as one route's image with another route's composition.

## Defensibility

The moat is contextual visual judgment, not a fixed house style. Keep infographic, real-world, physical, cultural, handmade, cinematic and surreal treatments available. The content determines the treatment.

Penalise generic gradients, floating cards, anonymous AI imagery, decorative technology motifs and familiar agency tropes unless they carry exact story meaning. An analogy must reduce explanation. Shock must carry meaning. A walrus is useful only when the audience can retell the walrus and the point together.

For a famous meme, propose an owned restage, a clearly transformative reference or a licensed source. Never assume the circulating image is free to publish. Keep evidence visually and contractually separate from illustration.

Store contextual learning. Do not infer that Krish likes an object, animal, colour or style in isolation. Record why a pairing worked or failed, its content context, its rights path and whether it improved comprehension or recall. Check novelty against the private motif ledger and require approval before activating a durable rule.

## Carousel branding

Use the official series wordmark once in the top-left as the channel signpost. Use the official Mindmake wordmark once in the bottom-right as the publisher signature. Keep both phone-legible, inside safe margins and on a contrast-safe plate. Do not stack them, recreate them as text or repeat either identity elsewhere on the card.
