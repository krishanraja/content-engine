# Asset safety and provenance

## Truth role and media type

Classify truth role separately from file type:

- `evidence`: authentic material that directly substantiates or documents the point;
- `illustration`: an authored or generated interpretation that helps explain or evoke it;
- `decoration`: atmosphere without evidentiary value.

A screenshot, photograph, clip, diagram, animation, sketch, or generated shot can never inherit a truth role from its format. Generated media is always illustration, never evidence. Show authentic evidence before visualising its implication.

Every used asset records its exact hash, source, owner, rights basis, claim link, editorial purpose, truth role, approval, transformation history, and disclosure decision. Third-party evidence must retain attribution and enough source identity to be credible. Prefer primary authorities, original data, reputable reporting, and visually specific headlines over generic service copy or SEO pages.

## Exact approval boundary

Review the exact candidate frame or clip that will enter the timeline. A crop, cleanup, animation, AI transformation, provider rerun, or regenerated frame creates a new asset hash and requires a new approval. Never treat a prompt, URL, or approximate contact-sheet thumbnail as approval of a different output.

Only the exact approved excerpt may leave the machine for cloud processing. Record provider, model and version, prompt, uploaded input hash, output hash, cost, provenance, and deletion or retention status. Local analysis remains the default.

Adobe Firefly is the default generated-asset adapter. Runway source-footage transformation is reserved for exact-shot approval. The default cloud ceiling is GBP 15 per Short; above it, propose a local alternative or request an explicit exception.

## Krish, guests, and synthetic media

- A stylised alteration of Krish requires exact shot approval and must not create a false factual impression.
- Synthetic speech, fabricated documentary footage, or a fake quotation is prohibited.
- Do not upload guest likeness or voice to a generative provider without separate approval and a recorded rights basis.
- Apply platform altered-content disclosure when realistic, meaningfully altered, or generated media could be mistaken for reality. A disclosure label does not convert illustration into evidence.

## Owned sketches

Preserve Krish's exact sketch as the first option. Cleanup, isolation, colour treatment, stroke animation, and deterministic camera movement may retain the original geometry. Reinterpretation, AI extension, 3D conversion, or newly invented marks are separate derivative assets and require separate approval. Keep the original and derivative hashes linked in provenance.

## Failure behaviour

Fail closed on a missing asset, hash drift, unclear rights, broken attribution, unapproved transformation, misleading crop, undisclosed realistic generation, or an illustration used as proof. If cloud access, budget, or provider output fails, use the recorded local fallback rather than silently substituting another asset.
