# Visual direction

## Doctrine

Use evidence-led authentic maximalism. Krish, guests, genuine artefacts, and verified sources are the trust spine. Rich direction is welcome only when it proves, explains, orients, compares, evokes, or deliberately delights. Content for content's sake and effects for effects' sake fail the editorial bar.

For every narrative beat, declare:

- the claim or spoken idea;
- the viewer's immediate task;
- one primary attention target;
- the visual's narrative job;
- reading and cognitive load;
- the intended entry, hold, and exit;
- a deterministic low-confidence fallback.

Do not introduce a critical source, major camera move, and new caption phrase on the same frame unless the collision is the exact approved experiment. Alternate dense beats with visual rests. Finish on a strong idea and a deliberate final image.

## Treatment lanes

- `restrained`: proven film grammar, stable framing, and minimal compositing.
- `premium`: richer evidence, motion, and sound with one established signature device.
- `experimental`: one principal unproven hero technique supported by familiar grammar.

Choose the lane from the story, source quality, and available proof, not automatically from the series. A previously approved treatment is reusable only inside its recorded scope. Do not broaden the Built With AI solo evidence-ribbon approval to conversations, another series, or a new visual technique.

## Virtual camera

Frame for the meaning of the moment, not merely the largest face. Consider identity, active speaker, eyes, torso, hands, gaze, pointing direction, movement, guest reactions, evidence focal regions, captions, branding, platform UI, source resolution, and digital-zoom headroom.

- Give lead room in the direction of a look, movement, or gesture.
- Reset tracking at source-shot boundaries.
- Bound crop velocity, acceleration, zoom, easing, and minimum hold time.
- Use a punch-in only when the semantic beat earns emphasis.
- Keep important hands or pointed-to objects when they carry meaning.
- Fall back to a stable crop when analysis confidence is weak.
- Permit a full-frame visual beat when inspection or comprehension is the viewer's primary task. Preserve audio continuity and design the return to Krish or the conversation.

## Technique registry

Every technique must have a stable ID, version, narrative purpose, required inputs, tunable parameters, accessibility constraints, cost class, fallback, and experimental status.

The foundation includes shot-size progression, inserts, cutaways, reactions, montage, cut-on-action, match, smash and jump cuts, J/L cuts, sound bridges, motivated camera moves, freeze frames, and controlled speed changes.

The first Mindmake signature pack is evidence-led: living evidence theatre, gesture-directed composition, guided screenshot pans and magnifiers, animated owned sketches, vertical evidence descent, and evidence-to-illustration handoffs. More experimental devices such as depth layering, portals, camera-motion relays, stylised duplicates, or reality ruptures remain exact-shot approval decisions.

Use `config/techniques.json` as the single repertoire authority. Before authoring production shot directives, run `studio v2 repertoire propose` for every beat and persist its `DeviceSelectionTraceV1` in `device_selection_traces`. Hard eligibility precedes scoring. Use one primary device and at most two supports. Equal scores resolve by stable device ID. Never select a device whose required governed inputs are missing.

The registry also contains recipes and analysis-only reference observations. A recipe is a recurring narrative sequence, not a house style. A reference observation may teach a principle but never grants permission to copy, trace or publish the reference media.

The current device pack adds noun-to-proof cut, tracked object label, progressive value reveal and embodied closing action. The Evidence Walkthrough recipe combines these with guided inspection and a clean return to Krish. Tracked labels require ordered layer tracking keyframes. Progressive reveals require at least two staged reveal moments. Embodied closing actions belong only in the final payoff or ending beat.

If no existing device clears the repertoire threshold, do not lower the threshold or choose decorative filler. The sharp alternative is exactly one job-local `DeviceInventionProposalV1`. It must use the experimental lane, name a conservative existing fallback, receive phone-size styleframe and timed animatic review, and receive exact approval from Krish before treatment. It cannot promote itself into the registry.

## Review and rejection

Review new work through phone-size entry, middle, and exit styleframes, then a timed low-resolution animatic. Premium and experimental work always requires the animatic gate. A proven restrained treatment may skip it only when risk and confidence checks pass.

Reject or request a precise pickup or rerecord when the argument, performance, ending, proof, source resolution, or visual coverage cannot support a premium result. Never use motion or generated media to disguise weak content.

Reject unsafe flashes, unreadable evidence, motion that risks discomfort, attention collisions, damaged facial detail, or a crop that changes the apparent meaning of a gesture.
