# Feedback and controlled learning

Capture explicit comments, approval actions, exact structured diffs, and re-imported external finals. For video finals, compare duration, framing, cut structure, caption treatment, transcript alignment, and audio characteristics where available.

State the inferred reason in one sentence. Do not present uncertain intent as fact. Low-confidence changes remain observations until the user explains or confirms them.

Scope memory in this order: global, series, source mode, treatment, platform, job. Start at the narrowest credible scope. Broaden only after the same preference is confirmed three times across at least two jobs, survives regression cases, and receives explicit approval.

Keep taste and performance separate. A performance rule becomes eligible after three comparable directional replications with no qualified-action or editorial degradation. Views alone never define taste and never promote a rule.
