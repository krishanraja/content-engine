# Radar evidence diagnostics

Control Center owns discovery, series lenses, opportunity review and editorial approval. The Studio may pull the latest completed mm-ctrl public pool and Control Center sanitized feed only for provider diagnostics, offline import and provenance checks. Never duplicate their scraping or query their databases directly.

Merge by canonical source URL first and normalized headline fingerprint second. Keep the more corroborated record. Mark source age and provider failures.

The evidence quality gate rejects generic guides, listicles, service headlines, URL-like or title-only summaries, personnel appointments without a specific operating consequence, aggregator and discussion surfaces, news older than 72 hours, and single-source secondary reporting. A single-source primary authority may remain in the signal pool when its summary states a concrete consequence.

Do not assign a raw signal to a series and do not create a hook from its headline. A signal can seed a Money angle, a Built angle, both or neither after Control Center runs the two lenses independently.

Production starts only when Control Center supplies a schema-valid `ProductionBriefV1` with the completed idea, approved text, claims, evidence, visual opportunities and exact Krish approval.

An internal sanitized pattern is inspiration only. It needs public evidence or explicitly approved case material before it can support a factual public claim.
