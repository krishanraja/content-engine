# Editorial channels

## Shared audience

AI-native business decision-makers. The content should help someone make a consequential decision, understand a mechanism, or improve a real build.

## The Money of AI

- Primary audience: enterprise leaders, founders, transformation buyers.
- Required question: what changes economically or organisationally?
- Useful proof: pricing, workflow economics, adoption evidence, decision rights, incentives, coordination, credibility, or operator experience.
- Avoid: generic market-size narration, funding news without mechanism, and model launches without business consequence.

## Built With AI

- Primary audience: operators and builders.
- Required question: what was built, changed, tested, or learned?
- Useful proof: interfaces, code, prompts, workflows, diagrams, before/after behaviour, constraints, and failure modes.
- Avoid: generic tool lists, tutorials without a real outcome, and simulated expertise.

## Source modes

- Extract when the source already contains a self-contained tension and payoff.
- Solo when the recording is direct-to-camera and needs framing, compression, or proof support.
- Short-native when the idea needs a designed hook, a controlled experiment, or an artifact recorded specifically for the format.

## Reuse

One approved argument may be adapted across platforms, but do not paste identical openings or add platform-specific claims. Preserve the same claim ledger and meaning.
