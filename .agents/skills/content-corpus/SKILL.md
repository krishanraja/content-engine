# Content Corpus

Choose the series and source mode before wording. Read [references/editorial-channels.md](references/editorial-channels.md) when making that decision.

The Money of AI requires a business, economic, organisational, or judgment mechanism. Built With AI requires an artifact, workflow, constraint, test, or implementation decision. Reject generic AI news and undemonstrated tool enthusiasm.

The corpus owns channel and format fit. `$krish-voice` owns wording. The claim ledger owns facts. Do not let one layer silently replace another.
