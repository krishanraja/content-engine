## 2. The additive unit chart: the Mon 2 Nov jobs piece

His words: "this visual was really nice in how it explained additively what I was saying in the Anthropic jobs video."

**When.** Any share or count on a follow.the.money piece: `unit_field` first, before a bar or a stat
(`config/ve/recipes/data-story.json` `protected_default`).

**The device.** `unit_field` with `mark: "badge"`, `columns: 10`, `plane: true` (rotateX 24, rotateZ -6, skewX -4),
`greyScope: "striped"` and `counter: {of: "striped", suffix: "%"}`, its dataset the rows the claims name. One event per
spoken claim, each on the word that names it: `enter` on "out of every hundred", `stripe` on "27" (the subset carries the
series-colour stripe, scattered through the grid), `grey` on "9" (the subset greys from the end of the grid to 9 left).
The numeral is bound to the grid, so it reads the same row in the same frame. 9:16 in the band panel (grid about 60%,
numeral 230 px, top left); 16:9 in the pillar panel (grid about 72%, numeral 260 px).

**Not carried over.** His screenshot shows the series pill and a wordmark in the body; a short's body carries neither
since 16 Nov (`config/brand-palette.json` `logo_rule`), unless he says otherwise.
