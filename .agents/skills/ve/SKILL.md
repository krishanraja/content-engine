# ve

1. Read docs/ve/ENGINE.md and docs/ve/RUNBOOK.md, then only the signed spec/session pack. No big files, images or raw logs: QC JSON, one contact sheet.
2. Preflight: `python3 scripts/check_failures.py --piece <piece>`, `python3 scripts/floor.py --format <long|short>_video --piece <piece>`, `python3 scripts/preflight.py <piece> --skill ve`; brief and render refuse without them from 9 Nov. In Bash `. scripts/ve-env.sh` pins ffmpeg n8.1.2/tesseract. `python scripts/ve.py doctor`: stop on hard blockers. `python scripts/ve.py start <piece>` writes the <=36 KB pack. Below, ve means python scripts/ve.py.
3. Follow S0-S8 (55 min envelope). `ve time <piece> start <phase>`, stop, status; log Krish-wait separately. `ve ledger <piece> add claude_tokens <n>` and add codex_tokens <n> log actual usage; log other providers' credits too, never Codex credits. `ve ledger <piece>` reports the caps in `config/ve/budget.json`: 300 engine min, 3.3M Claude, 2.1M Codex, 12.6M total tokens. At 80% carry on; refuse past caps. Ledger only warns; time start checks engine time only.
4. Lint after changes. Compile refuses unsupported device verbs (devices.json); test_grid draws carry. No SFX kit is approved yet: a cut adds no unapproved sound (sfx_missing blocks status) and records the gap as a final-review line. `ve stills` is an exit-2 placeholder; stop unless approved existing stills cover that stage. `ve file <piece> --subject <subject>` is real but runs only on a home computer (docs/ve/STATUS.md): it copies each READY cut to the archive and to its post's `3 Posts/<post>/3 Video` folder; the post folder is `YYYY-MM-DD Ddd <series> - Subject`, with `off.day` as the series on Tue, Thu and Sat. Never claim filing succeeded until `ve status` reads the copy back. Filing needs no approval.
5. `ve render <props> <out> --spec <spec> --assets-dir <assets>` uses ASCII transport, corrects AAC lag and prints residual <=1 ms. `ve qc <cut> --format <format> --props <props> --out <ve-dir>/qc.<cut>.json`: require measured_pass and complete. Max 2 fix loops, fresh Critic, Krish's final gate. Never hand-write passes/ready; `ve status <piece>` computes it.
   Opening: nothing before the hook, ever (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-12); `references/opening.md`.
   One round (F04, F13): `ve expect <spec>` goes beside the storyboard; after he records, the style is his lock's recipe (`ve styles <spec> --cut <cut>` checks it on his take); `ve proof <spec> --cut <cut>` is a preview he never has to answer, and the whole cut follows.
   Before any proof or cut is sent (F58, Krish 2026-11-19): open full-size frames at every beat change beside the last approved version and walk his standing notes; ve proof/render refuse plain dark bands and captions over content; a changed engine or asset needs `--compared`; the title frame needs `--title-looked` (RUNBOOK S5).
6. Motion: every cut holds the signed standard (`docs/ve/MOTION_STANDARD.md`). The spec's `style.motion.module` (required by `ve lint` from 9 Nov) names one motion system from the bank's motion layer (`config/style-references.json`; shortlist with `python3 scripts/post-pack/creative_direction.py motion --points ... --format ...`). `ve brief` adds its rules and avoid list to every plate brief. A module whose `ve_route` is `needs_device` gets a device card, never per-piece code. Looks: `.agents/skills/ve/references/looks.md`.
   Per-beat plan: the motion-designer skill.
   References come from the inspiration board, by card id (F26): follow `.agents/skills/ve/references/board-references.md` for `ve init`, `ve refs` and `ve verdict` on cards.
7. No per-piece code. New hero only: `ve card <fields.json>`, `ve dispatch <card.md> --piece <piece>`, `ve verify-card <card.md> --cwd <worktree>`, independent golden-still review. Stop/log clock before dispatch; --piece checks recorded caps.    **The Codex minimum** (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-15): every whole cut published from 16 Nov 2026 needs one Claude-checked Codex receipt of art direction, plates or a device (`ve codex receipt <piece> --card <id> --job art_direction --evidence <sheet> --verified-by Claude --cwd <worktree>`); `ve file` refuses it otherwise.
