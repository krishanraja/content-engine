# ve

1. Read docs/ve/ENGINE.md and docs/ve/RUNBOOK.md, then only the signed spec/session pack. No big files, individual images or raw logs: QC JSON and one contact sheet.
2. In Bash `. scripts/ve-env.sh` pins ffmpeg n8.1.2/tesseract. `python scripts/ve.py doctor`: stop on hard blockers. `python scripts/ve.py start <piece>` writes the <=36 KB pack. Below, ve means python scripts/ve.py.
3. Follow S0-S8 (55 min envelope). `ve time <piece> start <phase>`, stop, status; stop at gates, log Krish-wait separately. `ve ledger <piece> add claude_tokens <n>` and add codex_tokens <n> log actual usage; log allowance_points/credits too. `ve ledger <piece>` reports caps: 60 engine min, 3.3M Claude, 2.1M Codex, 12.6M total tokens, 1.2 allowance points. At 80% stop/ask at a gate; refuse past caps. Ledger only warns; time start checks engine time only.
4. Lint after changes. Compile refuses unsupported device verbs (devices.json); test_grid draws carry. SFX kits are absent: sfx_missing references block status. ingest and stills are exit-2 placeholders; stop unless approved existing inputs cover a stage. `ve file` is real but runs only on a home computer (docs/ve/STATUS.md): it files a READY cut into its post's `3 Posts` folder, `YYYY-MM-DD Ddd off.day - Subject` on Tue, Thu and Sat. Never claim filing succeeded until `ve status` reads the copy back.
5. `ve render <props> <out> --spec <spec> --assets-dir <assets>` uses ASCII transport, corrects AAC lag and prints residual <=1 ms. `ve qc <cut> --format <format> --props <props> --out <ve-dir>/qc.<cut>.json`: require measured_pass and complete. Max 2 fix loops, fresh Critic, Krish's final gate. Never hand-write passes/ready; `ve status <piece>` computes it.
   One round (F04, F13): `ve expect <spec>` goes beside the storyboard; after he records, `ve styles <spec> --cut <cut>` mocks the top 3 drawable styles on his take for his Pick a style press; then `ve proof <spec> --cut <cut>` renders the hardest beat. From 9 Nov `ve render` refuses a whole cut until he approves that proof (board press or spec approval by its sha256). One press per gate on the board; never ask in chat.
6. Motion: every cut holds the signed standard (`docs/ve/MOTION_STANDARD.md`). The spec's `style.motion.module` (required by `ve lint` from 9 Nov) names one motion system from the bank's motion layer (`config/style-references.json`; shortlist with `python3 scripts/post-pack/creative_direction.py motion --points ... --format ...`). `ve brief` adds its rules and avoid list to every plate brief. A module whose `ve_route` is `needs_device` gets a device card, never per-piece code.
   References come from the inspiration board, by card id (F26; Wave 3): `ve init <piece>` offers the top card per role (look, motion, captions) from `GET /api/inspiration/candidates` into `brand.references`, each marked `offered: true`. Open each card, confirm it serves this piece (delete `offered`, write the takeaway) or replace it with another board card, giving its `id` (inspiration_items.id) and keeping the file stem as `card`. ve lint refuses a reference with no `id` or with `offered` left. `ve refs` checks every id against `GET /api/inspiration/cards` and refuses an unknown, dismissed or strategy-lane card; offline it prints `board NOT CHECKED`, so say so. `ve verdict` records each card's use and Critic score (`POST /api/inspiration/uses`); `card uses NOT RECORDED` means run it again when the engine is reachable. Never type a card name the board does not hold.
6. No per-piece code. New hero only: `ve card <fields.json>`, `ve dispatch <card.md> --piece <piece>`, `ve verify-card <card.md> --cwd <worktree>`, independent golden-still review. Stop/log clock before dispatch; --piece checks recorded caps. Dispatch and every generation reserve against the credit caps Control Center places on the publication (`ve budget status`, `ve ledger <piece> reserve`); over it is refused, a card needing a tool the Codex profile forbids is held.
   **Codex route: never ask Krish how to reach Codex** (Krish, 2026-11-01: "You just do it first time round when I ask you to produce a video seamlessly"). Codex lives only on his home computer, inside WSL Ubuntu. `ve dispatch` picks the route itself: with the companion on this machine it dispatches here; without it (cloud, Claude Desktop, Windows-side Claude Code) it queues the card on the work board (`ve lane queue`; the `.fields.json` must be on origin/main; book it first with Control Center's `reserve_credit_spend`, engine growth-makeyourmindup, pool codex, amount the card's allowance_points or 1, and pass `--spend-id`; settle it with `settle_credit_spend` once the result is back) and his PC's recordings pass builds it in WSL within five minutes. Say "queued for Codex on your PC", carry on with the rest of the piece, then `ve lane status`: on `built`, fetch the pushed `ve-card/<card>` branch and re-run the acceptance yourself and review the golden stills; on `failed`, read the reason on the item and fix the card. Close the item (lane done) after your own check.
7. Batch questions/end at gates. Never publish/send/schedule. Recipes and realism/credit rulings follow config/ve/tools.json.
