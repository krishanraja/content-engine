# The engine's read of each card

Reference for `.agents/skills/plan-week/SKILL.md`, the gather (`sources.json`, `inspiration`).

Every card in the Inspiration block carries `style`, the engine's read of it in this repository's ids (inspirationhouse
tag-v4, `facets.style`, origin `engine_read`: series, script and production formats, captions, motion, transitions,
camera, energy, teaches), and `grounded_in`, which of his own fields on the card (why_here, why_like, would_do) the read
was built on.

- A grounded read (`style.grounded: true`) is his steer, not a guess (Krish, 2026-11-17: "If I put actual feedback in
  the inspiration board as to why I like something, that is not really a guess is it?"). Let it shape the series and
  format an idea is offered in, and say so in the idea's why.
- A bare read (`grounded: false`) is a guess, one input among the rest.
- His presses still pick. A read never puts an idea on the board and never answers a card for him.
- Files in a reference set he filed together (`facets.collection`, such as his thumbnail study) are left out of the
  story sources (`counts.reference_set_left_out`): they are for making, not what to say. One he wrote on or routed
  stays, and is owed like any other.
