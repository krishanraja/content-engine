# Mindmake Video and Carousel Studio: where it is right now

## What it is

Mindmake Video and Carousel Studio is the governed production engine behind Mindmake's publication: it turns an approved editorial brief into deterministic Shorts and swipe carousels for The Money of AI and Built With AI, and nothing renders, packages or uploads until Krish has approved the exact artifact hash. It is a TypeScript and Remotion studio with a hash-locked Python transcription runtime, a Windows Scheduled Task runner that executes Control Center commands without any chat session, and a carousel director that shares the video engine's evidence, approval and learning rules while using its own story, slide and render contracts. Since 2026-09-08 (PR #42) this repository also holds the Content Engine control plane itself: the editorial spine (capture, Composer, the weekly brief, the shifts register, the Monday purge), the headline pool and its two editorial lenses, the AEO research ingest, and the Video and Carousel Studio's own HTTP surface, all deployed from `apps/control-plane` as their own Vercel project that Control Center reaches through rewrites. Codex, Claude Code, Claude.ai, ChatGPT and Control Center are windows into the same engine (`docs/ENGINE_SESSION.md`); a repository checkout gives instructions, not production authority. The studio optimises honest reach, retention, sharing and qualified action. It never publishes publicly: YouTube upload is private-only, and LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram output stay local packages for a human to post (`AGENTS.md`). In the Content Engine registry this repo is `video-studio`, "the bespoke AI video production agency: a governed render engine, carousel engine and review surface"; that description was not updated when the control plane moved in (see "Do not trust").

## Who it is for and why it matters for Mindmake

The studio is not sold. It is the production system behind what Mindmake does sell and publish, and it is a proof asset. The room_face buyer (`control-center/docs/ICP.md`: a senior leader at a PE or VC backed media, adtech, publishing or data business, quietly behind on what is coming and unable to say so inside their organisation) runs a media business and is watching one person build a governed, deterministic production pipeline in which every taste rule needs explicit approval. What this repo proves is that AI production can be governed rather than merely generated: the machine proposes, the record shows why, and a person decides against an exact hash.

Stories a writer can carry without asking Krish:

- **A rule becomes taste only when a person says so.** Feedback moves through `observed`, `inferred`, `confirmed`, `trial`, `eligible`, `user_approved`, `active`. A rule is active only after the exact rule appears in reviewed Git configuration, and views alone can never make one eligible (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Learning boundary"). Codex and system diagnostics are observations and cannot become taste rules.
- **The first rendered carousel was rejected, and the record says why.** Repeated dark backgrounds, generic boxes, inert space and duplicate series labelling failed the standard "even though deterministic tests passed" (`docs/CAROUSEL_ENGINE_STATE.md`, "Appendix A: Decisions"). Three replacement concepts went to two blinded judges; the approval-interlock spine won 72.0 and 71.5 out of 80 (`docs/CAROUSEL_ENGINE_STATE.md`, "Appendix C: Reset trace").
- **Fail closed beats shrink to fit.** Every branded render must show the official wordmark's real lettering at 50 render pixels or more; if no collision-free placement exists, rendering and QA fail rather than covering Krish or shrinking the mark (`README.md`).
- **The machine keeps evidence, not transcripts.** The session contract records tool-backed actions, exact feedback excerpts and artifact differences, and never a whole chat transcript. Silence is not feedback (`docs/ENGINE_SESSION.md`).
- **Nothing publishes itself.** A merged code change is not an installed runner, and an installed runner is not a live integration until seven named steps succeed (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`).
- **A gate that exists but is not wired in is not a gate.** `api/_beat.ts` encoded Krish's own definition of what he does and does not cover, but the two routes that fed the corpus never called it, so "28 of 47 live arcs" were discarded downstream by hand at a 60 percent rate. Wiring it in, and adding a refusal tier the definition had missed for safety and security stories, cut that to 75 percent with no leak (PR #64).
- **An engine that cannot say "I do not know" will lie instead.** The editorial radar's model calls were truncating mid-response for months, and the fallback path wrote a fabricated rejection for every idea rather than reporting the failure, so every judged idea "read no_angle on BOTH lenses carrying the identical fallback reason" while the job reported ok throughout. The fix separates absence from judgement: an unanswered signal is now `unjudged` and retried, not recorded as a verdict (PR #63).

Objection it answers: "AI content is slop." Here is a pipeline that refuses to make the video when the source cannot clear the bar, and writes down the refusal.

## What changed recently

- 2026-09-09 **Beat gate wired into the corpus, and two holes closed** (PR #64, `733121f`). Why: Krish said plainly that "all the shifts spotted are so off the mark, about governance, risk, safety, negative things, and overly technical things. That is not what I do", and the gate that encodes exactly that already existed but the two routes that fed the corpus never called it, so a classifier downstream discarded "28 of 47 live arcs" by hand. A new `NEVER_BEAT` tier refuses safety and security stories outright, which the gate had never named even though it passed 88 percent of the corpus including 18 safety and incident rows; the gate now passes 75 percent and leaks none. A prefix-tolerant price match also rescued the gate's own worked example ("GPT-5.5 launch quietly repriced the whole agent market"), which had been failing.
- 2026-09-09 **Editorial radar stopped fabricating rejections; AEO left out of the editorial pool** (PR #63, `db7a0ef`). Why: a model call with too small a token budget truncated mid-JSON every time, and the fallback path wrote a fabricated `no_angle` for every idea instead of reporting the failure, so "as of today all 40 judged ideas read no_angle on BOTH lenses" while the job reported ok. Fixed by recording an unanswered signal as `unjudged` (retried, not cached as a verdict) rather than a fabricated rejection. Separately, every venture's AEO answer-engine copy was being scored by the same editorial lenses; a subject now needs an explicit allowlist entry, which "ships empty on purpose" because AEO is a different job with a different standard.
- 2026-09-08 **The Content Engine control plane moved into this repository** (PR #42, `a20e442`).
