# Mindmake Video and Carousel Studio: where it is right now

## What it is

Mindmake Video and Carousel Studio is the governed production engine behind Mindmake's publication: it turns an approved editorial brief into deterministic Shorts and swipe carousels for The Money of AI and Built With AI, and nothing renders, packages or uploads until Krish has approved the exact artifact hash. It is a TypeScript and Remotion studio with a hash-locked Python transcription runtime, a Windows Scheduled Task runner that executes Control Center commands without any chat session, and a carousel director that shares the video engine's evidence, approval and learning rules while using its own story, slide and render contracts. Codex, Claude Code, Claude.ai, ChatGPT and Control Center are windows into the same engine (`docs/ENGINE_SESSION.md`); a repository checkout gives instructions, not production authority. The studio optimises honest reach, retention, sharing and qualified action. It never publishes publicly: YouTube upload is private-only, and LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram output stay local packages for a human to post (`AGENTS.md`). In the Content Engine registry this repo is `video-studio`, "the bespoke AI video production agency: a governed render engine, carousel engine and review surface".

Since 2026-09-08 (PR #42) this repository is also the whole Content Engine control plane: `apps/control-plane` is what used to be `krishanraja/control-center`'s `api/` directory, and it deploys as its own Vercel project that Control Center reaches through rewrites. That is the editorial capture surface, the Composer's routes, the weekly brief, the judge panel and the edit ledger that measures it, the Monday purge, the AEO research lane, feed ingest and the creator scout, and the Video and Carousel Studio's own HTTP control plane (`apps/control-plane/README.md`). Two products, one governance model and one repository to develop in.

## Who it is for and why it matters for Mindmake

The studio is not sold. It is the production system behind what Mindmake does sell and publish, and it is a proof asset. The room_face buyer (`control-center/docs/ICP.md`: a senior leader at a PE or VC backed media, adtech, publishing or data business, quietly behind on what is coming and unable to say so inside their organisation) runs a media business and is watching one person build a governed, deterministic production pipeline in which every taste rule needs explicit approval. What this repo proves is that AI production can be governed rather than merely generated: the machine proposes, the record shows why, and a person decides against an exact hash.

Stories a writer can carry without asking Krish:

- **A rule becomes taste only when a person says so.** Feedback moves through `observed`, `inferred`, `confirmed`, `trial`, `eligible`, `user_approved`, `active`. A rule is active only after the exact rule appears in reviewed Git configuration, and views alone can never make one eligible (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Learning boundary"). Codex and system diagnostics are observations and cannot become taste rules.
- **The first rendered carousel was rejected, and the record says why.** Repeated dark backgrounds, generic boxes, inert space and duplicate series labelling failed the standard "even though deterministic tests passed" (`docs/CAROUSEL_ENGINE_STATE.md`, "Appendix A: Decisions"). Three replacement concepts went to two blinded judges; the approval-interlock spine won 72.0 and 71.5 out of 80 (`docs/CAROUSEL_ENGINE_STATE.md`, "Appendix C: Reset trace").
- **Fail closed beats shrink to fit.** Every branded render must show the official wordmark's real lettering at 50 render pixels or more; if no collision-free placement exists, rendering and QA fail rather than covering Krish or shrinking the mark (`README.md`).
- **The machine keeps evidence, not transcripts.** The session contract records tool-backed actions, exact feedback excerpts and artifact differences, and never a whole chat transcript. Silence is not feedback (`docs/ENGINE_SESSION.md`).
- **Nothing publishes itself.** A merged code change is not an installed runner, and an installed runner is not a live integration until seven named steps succeed (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`).
- **Disagreement is preserved, never averaged.** The judge panel runs six idea judges and seven draft judges blinded and in parallel; a verdict that cites no evidence is downgraded to an abstention rather than trusted, and the prosecutor judge argues for killing a piece but can never set the panel's high score, "since it argues for killing" (PR #43). The panel reports; Krish decides.
- **A hard delete gets a copy first.** The only unattended hard delete in the engine, the Monday purge, now writes every row it is about to remove to private storage before deleting it, and refuses to delete anything if that export fails: "a week of un-purged rows is a tidiness problem, a week of deleted rows with no copy is the thing this exists to prevent" (PR #43).
- **A filter that only ran downstream of itself was not a filter.** The beat gate matching Krish's own definition of what he covers existed since 2026-08-06 but the two routes that populate the corpus never called it, so a classifier further downstream was discarding 28 of 47 live arcs, 60 percent, by hand. Both routes call the gate now (PR #64).

Objection it answers: "AI content is slop." Here is a pipeline that refuses to make the video when the source cannot clear the bar, and writes down the refusal.

## What changed recently

- 2026-09-09 **The beat gate now filters the corpus it was written for** (PR #64, `733121f`). Why: Krish, on what the Content tab was showing him: "all the shifts spotted are so off the mark, about governance, risk, safety, negative things, and overly technical things. That is not what I do." The gate matching that definition existed since 2026-08-06, but `feed/ingest` and `shifts/detect` never called it, so a downstream classifier discarded 28 of 47 live arcs by hand, a 60 percent rate sitting exactly on the corpus's own `DISCARD_ALARM`. Both routes call the gate now. The gate itself had two holes measured against 113 live rows: safety and security stories were not named in it at all (88 percent pass rate, 18 of the passing rows safety or security), fixed with a new `NEVER_BEAT` tier that reads the headline only; and its own worked example, a "quietly repriced" launch story, failed because `price` matched but not `repriced`, fixed by making the price term prefix-tolerant.
- 2026-09-09 **Editorial radar: stop fabricating rejections, and keep AEO out of the pool** (PR #63, `db7a0ef`). Why: a token budget too small for its own answer meant every radar response truncated mid-JSON, and the fallback path wrote a permanent, fabricated `no_angle` rejection for every signal, on both lenses, for months, while the job kept reporting `ok`. Fixed by recording an unanswered signal as `unjudged` rather than rejected, so it retries instead of caching a lie; lens calls are now chunked to 5 signals so one bad batch costs five, not the run. Separately, every venture's AEO copy, including Fractionl products, was being scored by the Money of AI and Built With AI editorial lenses because nothing scoped the `publication` lane; a subject must now appear on an explicit, currently empty, allowlist to be treated as editorial.

Why: two earlier writes of the runner token and signing key as Enterprise-persisted were silently reverted by Windows credential roaming inside 25 minutes; this write held for 30 minutes and a freshly started runner came up healthy afterwards, "which is the half a running daemon cannot tell you."
