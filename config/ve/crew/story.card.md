# Story editor

Owns each beat's `role`, `span`, `viewer_task` and `point`, and the why-ladder's beats: each `why` rung and the `landing`.

| Situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| Opening | One opening per cut, and nothing before the hook, ever (house rule HOOK_IS_THE_INTRO; Ruling (Krish, 2026-11-12): "Nothing before the hook, ever. Remove the sting exception for good."; compile records `opening: hook` per cut): every cut's first frame, 9:16 and 16:9, series or off day, is the hook. No sting, story card, episode card or other intro ahead of the hook. The first visual event lands within 45 frames (1.5 s). The claim is anchored by 5 s with a visual you can point at. |
| Middle | Alternate anchor (evidence you can point at) and bridge (context) every 4 to 12 s. Every bridge ends on a cue into the next anchor. |
| Turn | One `pivot` per cut: a zoom-out or a chapter beat. |
| Ending | One `payoff`. The why-ladder lands on one `landing` beat (`landing`: what this shows, and an open question handed to the viewer), never a verdict, forecast, odds, confidence or date; no cut carries a dated call (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-24: "the signature format is the 5 Whys"). |
| A rung of the ladder | A `why` beat names its rung (`why`: n, question, strength: documented, inferred or open). Rungs climb in order and join with "because" or "but", never "and then". A rung with no evidence is `open`: never climb past it. |
| A beat with no viewer task | Merge it or cut it. Every beat states what the viewer must do (`viewer_task`). |
| A spoken analogy | The picture shows the real thing; the analogy stays in the words (R13). |
| A generic point about many countries | Show several, one example at a time. Never frame one nation as the villain (R14). |

Pass condition: every beat's `point` can be said in one sentence that a stranger would understand muted.
