# ve

1. Read docs/ve/ENGINE.md and docs/ve/RUNBOOK.md, then only the signed spec/session pack. No big files, individual images or raw logs: QC JSON and one contact sheet.
2. Preflight: `python3 scripts/check_failures.py --piece <piece>`, `python3 scripts/floor.py --format <long|short>_video --piece <piece>`, `python3 scripts/preflight.py <piece> --skill ve`; brief and render refuse without them from 9 Nov. Below, ve means python scripts/ve.py.
3. Follow S0-S8 (55 min envelope). `ve time <piece> start <phase>`, stop, status; stop at gates, log Krish-wait separately. `ve ledger <piece> add claude_tokens <n>` and add codex_tokens <n> log actual usage; log allowance_points/credits too. `ve ledger <piece>` reports the caps in `config/ve/budget.json`: 300 engine min, 3.3M Claude, 2.1M Codex, 12.6M total tokens, 1.2 allowance points. At 80% stop/ask at a gate; refuse past caps. Ledger only warns; time start checks engine time only.
4. Lint after changes. Compile refuses unsupported device verbs (devices.json); test_grid draws carry. SFX kits are absent until Krish approves a sound by ear (`ve sfx audition`, then `ve sfx approve` with his words): sfx_missing references block status. `ve stills` is an exit-2 placeholder; stop unless approved existing stills cover that stage. `ve file <piece> --subject <subject>` is real but runs only on a home computer (docs/ve/STATUS.md): it copies each READY cut to the archive and to its post's `3 Posts/<post>/3 Video` folder; the post folder is `YYYY-MM-DD Ddd <series> - Subject`, with `off.day` as the series on Tue, Thu and Sat. Never claim filing succeeded until `ve status` reads the copy back.
5. `ve render <props> <out> --spec <spec> --assets-dir <assets>` uses ASCII transport, corrects AAC lag and prints residual <=1 ms. `ve qc <cut> --format <format> --props <props> --out <ve-dir>/qc.<cut>.json`: require measured_pass and complete. Max 2 fix loops, fresh Critic, Krish's final gate. Never hand-write passes/ready; `ve status <piece>` computes it.
   Opening (HOOK_IS_THE_INTRO; Krish, 2026-11-05: "I think it should go at the start of the longer wide videos 100%,"): compile puts the approved series sting before the 16:9 whole cut of a series piece on its day, the hook on the next frame; 9:16, proof windows and off days open on the hook. QC (its `opening` row), `--edl` joins, refs and the packet read the file's own clock (ENGINE.md).
   One round (F04, F13): `ve expect <spec>` goes beside the storyboard; after he records, `ve styles <spec> --cut <cut>` mocks the top 3 drawable styles on his take for his Pick a style press; then `ve proof <spec> --cut <cut>` renders the hardest beat. From 9 Nov `ve render` refuses a whole cut until he approves that proof (board press or spec approval by its sha256). One press per gate on the board; never ask in chat.
6. Motion: every cut holds the signed standard (`docs/ve/MOTION_STANDARD.md`). The spec's `style.motion.module` (required by `ve lint` from 9 Nov) names one motion system from the bank's motion layer (`config/style-references.json`; shortlist with `python3 scripts/post-pack/creative_direction.py motion --points ... --format ...`). `ve brief` adds its rules and avoid list to every plate brief. A module whose `ve_route` is `needs_device` gets a device card, never per-piece code.
   References come from the inspiration board, by card id (F26): follow `.agents/skills/ve/references/board-references.md` for `ve init`, `ve refs` and `ve verdict` on cards.
7. No per-piece code. New hero only: `ve card <fields.json>`, `ve dispatch <card.md> --piece <piece>`, `ve verify-card <card.md> --cwd <worktree>`, independent golden-still review. Stop/log clock before dispatch; --piece checks recorded caps. Dispatch and every generation reserve against the credit caps Control Center places on the publication (`ve budget status`, `ve ledger <piece> reserve`); over it is refused, a card needing a tool the Codex profile forbids is held.
   **Codex route: never ask Krish how to reach Codex** (Krish, 2026-11-01: "You just do it first time round when I ask you to produce a video seamlessly"). Codex builds cards on his home computer, inside WSL Ubuntu. `ve dispatch` picks the route itself: with the companion on this machine it dispatches here; without it (cloud, Claude Desktop, Windows-side Claude Code) it queues the card on the work board (`ve lane queue`; the `.fields.json` must be on origin/main; book it first with Control Center's `reserve_credit_spend`, engine growth-makeyourmindup, pool codex, amount the card's allowance_points or 1, and pass `--spend-id`; settle it with `settle_credit_spend` once the result is back) and his PC's recordings pass builds it in WSL within five minutes. Say "queued for Codex on your PC", start `ve lane wait <id>` in the background at once and carry on with the rest of the piece; act on its first built or failed line (`ve lane status` lists every item). On `built`, fetch the pushed `ve-card/<card>` branch and re-run the acceptance yourself and review the golden stills; on `failed`, read the reason on the item and fix the card. After your own check, close the work board item yourself (it closes on session: move it to lane `done` through `/api/workbench`).
8. Batch questions/end at gates. Never publish/send/schedule. Recipes and realism/credit rulings follow config/ve/tools.json.
